# A close

> One close: the checklist as it stood on the day, sign-off and review notes between the owner and the accountant, and the AI-written narrative of the month.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/accounting/close/*
> **Order:** 320

## The top of the page

The line above the title reads `Close / 2026-08-31`, with the company's name after it when you keep more than one. `Close` goes back to the Close page. The title is `Close through 2026-08-31`, or `Maple Street LLC — close through 2026-08-31`. Under it: `Completed by Dan on 2026-09-01`, and, if the close was reopened, ` · reopened by Dan on 2026-09-03`.

Two badges at the right: `Completed` or `Reopened`, and `Signed off · Dan` with a pen icon once somebody has signed off.

## Checklist snapshot

The card reads `As recorded when the books were closed (2026-09-01).` and lists the eight checklist items as they stood at the moment of the close, each with a green tick or an amber alert and a count in brackets. It is a record, not a to-do list, so there are no Review links here. The live checklist is on the Close page. See [Close](close.md).

## Review

The card reads `Sign-off and notes — the owner ↔ accountant dialogue.` It is where the owner and the accountant agree the month is done.

**Sign off this close** appears at the top right for an owner or an accountant while the close is completed and not yet signed off. Click it and the button reads `Signing…`, then you see `Close signed off.` The badge `Signed off · [your name]` appears at the top of the page and in the close history. A close is signed off once: a second attempt answers `This close is already signed off.` A reopened close cannot be signed off: `That close was reopened — complete a new close first.` If the close changed while your page was open, you see `This entry changed since you opened it — reload and try again.`

Notes are listed under the description, each with who wrote it and the date, for example `Dan · 2026-09-02`, and the note as written. Before the first note the card reads `No notes yet.`

To add one, write in the box, `Leave a review note for this close…`, up to 2,000 characters, and click **Add note**. The button reads `Adding…`, then the note appears in the list. A note cannot be edited or deleted, so the conversation stays as it happened. Owners and accountants can add notes. Staff can read them.

## Close narrative

A plain-English summary of the month, written by AI for you to read. It is not a figure that goes into the books. The card reads `A plain-English summary of the period, written by AI.` until one exists, then `Generated 2026-09-01 · [model] — AI-written, review before relying on it.`

Owners and accountants see **Generate narrative** at the top right of a completed close, or **Regenerate narrative** when one exists. Click it and the button reads `Writing…`, then you see `Narrative generated.` Until then the card reads `No narrative yet. Generate one to get the month in plain English.` A reopened close keeps the narrative it has and cannot write a new one.

The narrative opens with up to five highlights in bold, each with a short detail, then a few short paragraphs. The period it covers runs from the day after the previous close, or from the start of the fiscal year for a first close, to the period end. To write it, the AI is given this company's profit and loss for the period with the previous period beside it, its cash activity, its balance sheet at the period end, the checklist items still open, the ten largest posted entries, and the names of vendors and customers added in the period. It is told never to invent a number and to flag open items plainly.

Read it as a reviewer's first draft. It can help you spot what changed, and it never posts, changes or signs off anything. Signing off is a separate click by a person.

If you click again within a few seconds, you see `Suggestions were just requested — try again in a moment.` If the AI service does not answer usefully, you see `The AI service didn't return usable suggestions. Try again.`

**Back to Close** at the bottom returns to the Close page.
