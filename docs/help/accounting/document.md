# A document in the Inbox

> One bill or receipt: the preview, what Yosher read off it and how sure it is, what it is attached to, and the same buttons as its row in the Inbox.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/accounting/receipts/*
> **Order:** 60

## The top of the page

`← Inbox` takes you back. The title is the vendor's name as it was read, or the file name. The line under it says how the document arrived: `Emailed by [sender] · “[subject]”` or `Uploaded`, then the date, and `· in trash` if it has been trashed.

The buttons at the right are the same as on the row in the Inbox: **Read**, **Create bill**, **Attach**, **Record expense**, the bin, or **Restore** for a trashed document. See **The Inbox** for what each does.

## Preview

The left-hand card shows the document itself: the image, or the PDF in your browser's own viewer with its own zoom and page controls. An email that arrived with nothing readable attached shows `This email arrived with no readable attachment — kept for the paper trail.`

## What we read

The right-hand card lists what Yosher read, six rows:

- **Vendor.**
- **Date.**
- **Total.**
- **Tax.** The tax shown on the document, when it was itemised.
- **Currency.**
- **Number.** The invoice, bill or receipt number.

Beside each value is a percentage, how sure the reading is. A row Yosher could not read shows a dash and no percentage. Under the rows, **Line items** lists up to 25 lines with their amounts, when the document had them.

Nothing here goes into your books on its own. These values only fill in the form when you create a bill or record an expense, and you can change any of them there.

If the document has not been read, the card says `Not read yet — use the Read button.` If reading failed: `We couldn't read this document automatically.` Press **Read** to try again, or file it by hand.

## Attached to

The lower right-hand card lists what the document is attached to, for example `Bill — Ridgeline Feed · INV-4471`, `Invoice 1042`, `Journal entry · 2026-08-14 · Fuel` or `Bank transaction · 2026-08-14 · TRACTOR SUPPLY`. The X beside one detaches it: `Detached.` Before anything is attached it reads `Not attached to anything yet.`

Detaching the last attachment sends the document back to **To file**.

## Trash

A document that is attached to something cannot be trashed: `Detach this file from its transactions before trashing it.` A trashed document can always be restored. Nothing in the Inbox is ever deleted for good.
