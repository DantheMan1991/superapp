# Write a journal entry

> A hand-written entry: the date and memo, the lines with their debits and credits, keeping it balanced, and saving it as a draft or posting it.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/accounting/journal/new
> **Order:** 200

## When to write one

Most of the books write themselves from invoices, bills and the bank feed. A journal entry is for the rest: a correction, a transfer, depreciation you record by hand, a loan drawdown. The title is **New journal entry** and the line under it reads `Debits on the left, credits on the right — they must match to post.`

## The form

- **Company.** Only when you keep more than one. `Which company's books?` Fixed once the entry exists.
- **Date.** Today to begin with.
- **Memo.** `What is this entry for?`

The lines start as two rows. Each has:

- **Account.** Any active account. A journal entry may name any of them, including receivables and payables, but not a bank account that belongs to another company.
- **Debit** or **Credit.** Typing in one clears the other.
- **Line memo.**
- A **Tag** button under the row when your business has tags.
- **Remove line**, once there are more than two.

**Add line** adds a row. The bar under the lines totals the two sides, `Dr 1,250.00` and `Cr 1,250.00`, and says **Balanced** or `Off by 250.00`.

## Saving or posting

**Save draft** keeps the entry without touching the books; it needs at least one complete line. **Post entry** writes it into the books and needs the entry balanced with at least two lines. Owners post; staff save drafts for an owner to post. Both take you back to the journal with `Entry posted` or `Draft saved`.

## Messages you may see

- `Debits and credits must be equal before posting.`
- `A journal entry needs at least two lines.`
- `Every line needs a non-zero amount.`
- `That date falls in a closed period. Use a reversal, or reopen the period first.` The month has been closed for this company.
- `That bank account belongs to a different company. Use one of this company's own accounts — money moving between two of your companies is a transfer, and recording it properly needs both sides.`
- `One of the selected accounts is inactive.`
- `One of the selected tags is invalid or inactive.`

Someone with accountant access can open the form, but a save does not go through.
