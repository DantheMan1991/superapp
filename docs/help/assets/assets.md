# Everything you own

> The list of what the business owns. Add something, and post a month of depreciation across everything at once.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/assets
> **Order:** 10

Open **Assets** in the sidebar. The heading reads `What the business owns — what it cost, where it lives, and what is still in service.` Everything still in service is listed here. To put something new on the books, click {button:Add asset|primary}.

## What you see

- **{button:Add asset|primary}.** Opens the dialog that puts something new on the books. Owners only.
- **{button:Post depreciation (2 assets, $145.00)|outline}.** Writes this month's depreciation to your accounts for everything that is due. The number of assets and the total are in the label, so you know what you are about to post before you click. Owners only, and it only appears when something is actually due. There is no confirmation step, so read the label first.
- **{icon:circle-question-mark}.** Opens this guide beside the screen.
- **`Asset`.** The name, with a small photo if the asset has one and a serial or tag underneath if you gave it one. Click the name to open the asset.
- **`Kind`.** What sort of thing it is. The list is always sorted by this first, then by name.
- **`Company`.** Whose books the asset sits on. This column only appears when your business keeps more than one set of books.
- **`Kept in`.** The asset this one sits inside, such as the barn a freezer is in. A dash means nothing, or means the thing it sits in has been disposed of.
- **`Acquired`.** The day you got it, as year, month and day. A dash means you did not record one.
- **`Cost`.** What it cost, with your currency symbol. A dash means no cost was recorded, which is not the same as zero.
- **The empty list.** Before anything is on the books you see `Nothing on the books yet`. An owner is told to add the first thing. Everyone else is told an owner will.

## How to add something

1. Click {button:Add asset|primary}. The dialog reads `Anything the business owns — a building, a machine, a vehicle, a fence.`
2. Type a `Name`. This is the only field you must fill in. It takes up to 200 characters.
3. Pick a `Kind`. To use a word of your own, pick `Something else…` and type it in the box that appears. Use lowercase letters, numbers and spaces only. A word starting with a digit is refused.
4. Fill in `Acquired` and `Cost` if you know them. `Cost` shows `Leave blank if unknown`, and blank is better than a guess.
5. Add a `Model` and a `Serial or tag` if the thing has them. Both take up to 200 characters.
6. Pick the `Company` if your business keeps more than one set of books. Choose carefully. The help under it reads `Whose books this asset sits on. It cannot be moved later — every depreciation entry belongs to whoever owned it then.`
7. Pick `Kept in` if it lives inside something else you already own. Otherwise leave it on `Nowhere in particular`.
8. Turn on `Things are kept here` if this is somewhere stock is held, such as a freezer, a barn or a garage.
9. Add `Notes` if you want to. Up to 5,000 characters.
10. Click {button:Add asset|primary}. You see `Asset added` and the new row appears in the list.

The dialog has no cancel button. Close it with the X in the corner. The `Kind` you picked and the `Things are kept here` switch stay as you left them the next time you open it, so check both before adding a second asset.

You cannot set up depreciation here. Add the asset, then open it and set it up on its own page. Until you do, it will never be due.

## How to post a month of depreciation

1. Look at {button:Post depreciation (2 assets, $145.00)|outline}. The label tells you how many assets are due and what they add up to.
2. Click it. The label changes to `Posting…` while it works.
3. You see `Posted 1 month across 2 assets`, or the same wording with larger numbers.
4. The button usually disappears, because nothing is due any more.

If some of the months fall before your closing date, the message adds ` — 3 pre-close months rolled into catch-up entries`. Those months are combined into a single entry dated after your close, so nothing lands in a period you have already closed.

To post one asset on its own instead, open it and use the button on its page.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Asset added` | It worked. The new row is in the list. |
| `Posted 1 month across 2 assets` | The depreciation reached your accounts. |
| `Nothing on the books yet` | The list is empty. An owner adds the first thing. |
| `Check the details and try again.` | Something in the dialog is not filled in the way it needs to be. Most often the `New kind` box is blank. |
| `A kind must be lowercase letters, numbers and underscores.` | Your own kind has a capital, a symbol or a leading digit in it. Try plain words, such as `chicken tractor`. |
| `Only an owner can change what the business owns.` | You are signed in as staff. Ask an owner. |
| `That container does not exist.` | The thing you picked under `Kept in` has been removed since the page loaded. Reload and pick again. |
| `Pick a month to post through.` | The depreciation button was clicked before the page finished loading. Reload and try again. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Try again, and tell us if it keeps happening. |

## Not on this page

- There is no search box, and no way to sort or filter the list. It is always kind first, then name.
- Disposed assets are not shown, and there is no way to show them from this page.
- You cannot edit or dispose of anything from the list. Open the asset first.
- You cannot delete an asset.
- Photos can only be added on an asset's own page.
- Nothing can be added in bulk or brought in from a spreadsheet. Ask us if you have a long list to load.

## Who can do what

Only an owner can add an asset or post depreciation. Staff and accountants see the list, the photos and every figure on it, with no buttons at all in the top corner.
