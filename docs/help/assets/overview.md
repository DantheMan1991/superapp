# Assets at a glance

> Everything the business owns that has a cost and a working life. This guide is the map of the Assets pages. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/assets/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Assets** in the sidebar. This is where you record what the business owns, what each thing cost, where it lives, and what is still in service. Two pages make up the whole tool: the list of everything, and one thing's own page.

## What you see

- **An asset.** Anything owned with a cost and a working life. A tractor, a building, a freezer, a fence. If it wears out over years rather than getting used up, it belongs here.
- **The Assets list.** Everything on the books, with its kind, where it is kept, when you got it, and what it cost. Owners add things here and post a month of depreciation for everything at once. See [Everything you own](assets.md).
- **One asset's page.** Its details, what is kept inside it, its depreciation, its service schedules, and its photos. This is where nearly all the work happens. See [One asset](asset.md).
- **Kinds.** Every asset has a kind, such as `Building`, `Equipment`, `Vehicle`, `Infrastructure` or `Fixture`. You can type your own. Kinds group the list and nothing more.
- **Kept in.** An asset can sit inside another asset. A freezer sits in a barn. Turn on `Things are kept here` and that asset can also be picked as somewhere stock is held.
- **Depreciation.** Writing an asset's cost down over its working life, a month at a time. Yosher works out the months and posts them to your accounts. Set it up on the asset's page.
- **Maintenance.** What comes round again: a service, an inspection, a filter. A schedule counts either months or hours run. Due work becomes a job on your Work list.
- **Photos.** A series of pictures of one asset over time. This needs Documents switched on.
- **Money and blanks.** Costs show with your currency symbol and two decimals. Anything not recorded shows as a dash, never as zero. Dates show as year, month and day.

## How to put something on the books

1. Open **Assets** and click {button:Add asset|primary}.
2. Give it a `Name` and pick a `Kind`. Everything else can wait.
3. Add the `Cost` and `Acquired` date if you know them. Leave them blank if you do not.
4. Click {button:Add asset|primary}. You see `Asset added` and the new row in the list.
5. Open the asset and set up its depreciation, if it is something that gets written down.
6. Add a service schedule for anything that comes round.

## Not on this page

- The list cannot be searched, sorted or filtered. It is always ordered by kind, then by name.
- Disposed assets are hidden from the list, and there is no way to show them.
- Nothing can be deleted. Something you no longer have is disposed of instead, and that keeps its history.
- Disposing of something cannot be undone. Check the date and the amount before you confirm.
- You cannot set up depreciation while adding an asset. Add it first, then open it.
- There is no bulk entry. Twenty assets is twenty dialogs.
- Nothing here tells you what an asset is worth today. Book value is cost less what has been written down, which is an accounting figure and not a market price.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

What the business owns belongs to the owner. Only an owner can add an asset, edit one, dispose of one, set up or post depreciation, add a service schedule, or raise jobs from due work.

Maintenance is the other kind of thing. Marking a service done and recording a meter reading are open to everyone, an accountant included, because whoever changed the oil is the person standing there when it needs writing down.

Everyone can see every page, including all the depreciation figures and every service schedule.

Jobs raised from an asset are open to everyone. Any member can mark one done, give it to somebody, or set its date.

Photos are open to everyone, an accountant included.
