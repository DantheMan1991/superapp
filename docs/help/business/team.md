# Team

> Who can get into your workspace, how to invite them, and how to give an outside accountant read-only access to the books.
> **Route:** /dashboard/team
> **Order:** 10

## The organisation panel

The large panel is your business's membership, run by the sign-in service Yosher uses. It has three areas.

**Members** lists everyone who has joined, with their role. An owner can change a member's role or remove them. Staff can see the list but not change it.

**Invitations** is where you bring someone in. Enter their email address, choose a role, and send. They get an email with a link. Until they accept, they show here as a pending invitation, which you can revoke. They only become a member, and only appear in the Accounting access list below, once they have accepted.

**General** holds the business's name and logo, and the options to leave or delete the organisation where you are allowed to.

Roles come in two kinds. An owner can change anything in Yosher, including the settings pages. A member is staff: they use the tools but cannot change business settings or approve money.

## Accounting access

Owners see a second card, **Accounting access**, described as `Mark your outside accountant or bookkeeper. Accountants can read everything, review and sign off closes, and export the books — they can never post or change anything.`

It lists every member with their name and email. The right-hand side of each row is one of three things:

- **Owner.** Owners always have full access and cannot be marked as accountants.
- **You.** You cannot change your own access.
- **Accountant** with a switch. Turn it on to give that person accountant access, off to take it away.

Turning it on shows `Marked as accountant — read and review access only.` Turning it off shows `Accountant access removed.`

What accountant access means in practice:

- They can read everything in Accounting, review a month-end close and sign it off, and export the books.
- They can never post, edit or approve anything. If they try, Accounting tells them `Accountant access is read-only — reviews, sign-offs and exports only.`
- They have no mailbox access, and the Mail row shows them no unread count.
- On What needs you they see only their own scheduling and work items, never bills or invoices.

Giving somebody accountant access narrows what they can do. It is for the outside professional, not for a staff member who also does the books.

## When a change does not show straight away

Roles are kept by the sign-in service and copied into Yosher when an owner opens this page, at onboarding, and each morning. If you change a role in the panel and it does not show elsewhere yet, open this page again as an owner.
