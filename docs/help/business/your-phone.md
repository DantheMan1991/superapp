# Your phone

> Letting a phone record what happened without opening Yosher, what it is allowed to do, and how to switch one off.
> **Route:** /dashboard/settings/phone
> **Order:** 20

Open **Your phone** under `Business` in the sidebar. Everyone has this page, not just owners — it sets up *your* phone, not the business. The page reads `Phones that can record things in <your business> without opening the app.`

The idea: your hands are full, so instead of unlocking your phone and finding the right screen, you say what happened. Yosher reads back what it understood and **writes nothing until you say yes.**

## What a phone can and cannot do

- **It can record what happened** — a move, a loss, a check, a feed. The same things you could type into the box on the daily round.
- **It can never see or touch money.** No invoices, no bills, no payments, no wages.
- **It can never do the things only an owner can do**, even if you are an owner. A phone always acts as ordinary staff. This is on purpose, and it is not a setting.
- **It reads nothing back to you** except what it just recorded. It will not read out a balance or a list.

## What you see

- **The `Talking to Yosher` card.** Everything on this page is inside it.
- **`Which phone is it?`** A box for a name, up to 60 characters. Placeholder `Dan's iPhone`. This is only so you can tell your phones apart later.
- **`Kind`.** A picker with two choices, `iPhone` and `Android`.
- **{button:Set up|primary}.** Greyed out until you have typed a name.
- **The list of phones you have already set up**, or `No phones set up yet.` if you have none.
- **The note at the foot.** The three rules about when a phone stops working. See below.

## How to set a phone up

1. Type a name into `Which phone is it?`.
2. Pick the `Kind`.
3. Click {button:Set up|primary}.
4. A panel appears with a long code in it, above the words **`Put this in the phone now — you will not see it again.`**
5. Put the code into the phone, then click {button:I have saved it|outline} to hide the panel.

**The code is shown once and there is no way to get it back.** Yosher only keeps a scrambled copy, which is what makes the list safe to store — if somebody got hold of the database they still could not talk to your business. The panel says the same thing: `Lost it? Switch this phone off and set it up again.`

If you lose it, that is the answer. Switch the phone off in the list and set it up again under the same name. Nothing you have already recorded is affected.

## What each row in the list tells you

- **The name** you gave it, in bold.
- **`iPhone`** or **`Android`**.
- **`last spoke <date>`**, or **`has not spoken yet`** if it has never been used.
- **`stops working <date> if unused`** — the date this phone goes quiet on its own if nothing more is said to it.
- **{button:Switch off|outline}** at the end of the row.

## How to switch a phone off

1. Click {button:Switch off|outline} on the row. There is no confirmation step.
2. You see `<name> can no longer speak to Yosher` and the row disappears from the list.

**It takes effect the next time that phone tries to say something.** Nothing is sent out to the phone, so a phone with no signal will not know until it reconnects. Switching off cannot be undone — set the phone up again if you change your mind, which gives it a fresh code.

## When a phone stops working

Three ways, and only the first is something you do:

- **You switch it off** here.
- **It goes 30 days without being used.** The date is on the row. Using it pushes the date back, so a phone you talk to most mornings never stops.
- **You leave this business.** The moment somebody is taken off the team, every phone they set up here stops working straight away. Nobody has to remember to come to this page.

## Messages you might see

- `Give the phone a name.` — the name box was empty or only spaces.
- `You already have 5 phones set up — revoke one first.` — five is the limit per person. Switch one off to make room. The form greys out and the page says `That is 5 phones, which is the limit. Switch one off to set up another.`
- `This platform is not set up for phones yet (SHARE_SECRET).` — nothing is wrong with your account. The platform is missing a setting and nobody can set a phone up until it is added. Tell us.

## Questions people ask

- **Can my manager see my phone here?** No. This page shows only your own phones. An owner who needs to stop a phone does it by taking the person off the team, which stops all of theirs at once.
- **Does it listen all the time?** No. Nothing is recorded until you start it.
- **Can somebody who picks up my phone use it?** Yes, the same way they could use anything else on an unlocked phone. That is why a phone can never touch money, and why switching one off is instant here.
- **Does it work with no signal?** What you say is kept on the phone and sent when it reconnects, and it is recorded at the time you *said* it, not the time it arrived.
