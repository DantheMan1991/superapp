# Documents at a glance

> Your filing cabinet: the Overview page, the menu, what a folder, a file, a version, a tag, a share link and a template are, how files get in, and who can do what. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/documents/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Documents** in the sidebar. The Overview shows the Inbox and your top-level folders, and the menu under the title takes you to every other page. To put a file in, click {button:Upload|outline|upload}. To make a folder, click {button:New folder|primary|folder-plus}.

## What you see

- **The line under the title.** `Every file the business runs on — office, field and shop floor.`
- **The buttons.** {button:Upload|outline|upload} puts files in the Inbox, to be filed later. {button:New folder|primary|folder-plus} makes a top-level folder. See [Browse](browse.md) for both.
- **The menu.** A row of tabs under the title on every Documents page, with a search box at its right end. `Overview`, this page. `Browse`, your folders and files. `Inbox`, files not yet filed into a folder. `Search`, which looks inside files as well as at their names. `Tags`, the labels you put on files. `Templates`, documents you write once and fill in every time after. `Shared links`, the links you have given people outside the business. `Trash`. Type in `Search files…` and press {kbd:Enter} to go to the Search page with what you typed.
- **`Inbox`.** A card reading `Nothing waiting to be filed.`, or `12 files waiting to be filed.` Click it to open the Inbox. Receipts captured by Accounting count here too.
- **`Folders`.** One card per top-level folder, reading `Empty` or `3 files`. The count is files directly in that folder, not in its subfolders, so a folder that holds only subfolders reads `Empty`. A folder only owners can see carries {badge:Owners|secondary}. Click a card to open the folder, or `Folders` to open Browse.

## How the cabinet is organized

- **A folder** holds files and other folders, up to ten levels deep. A new business starts with `Jobs`, `Contracts`, `Insurance & Bonds`, `Licenses & Permits`, `Safety`, `Equipment`, `Suppliers`, `Photos` and `Admin`. Anyone can rename a folder; an owner can move or delete one, and can make one owners-only, which hides it and everything filed inside it from staff and accountants.
- **A file** is anything you upload, up to 100 MB: photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, TIFF), PDFs, plain text, CSV and Markdown, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, zip files, and whole emails. Web pages and SVG images are refused. A file has a stored name and can carry a title of its own, tags, and versions.
- **A version** is an earlier copy kept when you upload a replacement. Nothing is overwritten. Restore any version and it becomes the current one again, with the history intact.
- **A tag** is a label you put on a file, up to 30 on one file and 200 in the business. Click a tag anywhere to see every file carrying it.
- **A share link** lets someone outside the business open one file or one folder without signing in, until the link expires. It always shows the current version. See [Shared links](shares.md).
- **A template** is a document you write once in Markdown with blanks in it, and fill in every time after. Each document made from it is a PDF filed in the cabinet with its own number. See [Templates](templates.md).
- **A saved view** is a search you keep, on the Search page. See [Search](search.md).
- **A folder's email address.** An owner can give a folder an address of its own. Anything a supplier or subcontractor attaches to an email sent there lands in that folder, already filed. See [Browse](browse.md).

## How files get in

1. **Upload**, from the Overview, Browse or a folder, or by dragging files from your desktop onto the Browse page. From the Overview or the top of Browse, a file lands in the Inbox; from inside a folder, in that folder.
2. **A folder's email address**, for files from people outside the business.
3. **Receipts captured by Accounting**, which share the Inbox and are marked `from Receipts`.
4. **Mail**, where a conversation and its attachments can be filed into a folder or the Inbox.
5. **Photos added to a record**, such as an animal or a piece of equipment, which land in `Photos`.
6. **Documents made from a template**, which land in the Inbox or the folder you chose.

## How search reaches inside files

Search matches the name, title and tags of every file, and the contents of PDFs with a text layer, spreadsheets, and plain text, CSV and Markdown files. It does not read scans, photos, Word or PowerPoint files, so those are found by their name and tags only. The viewer says whether a file's contents are searchable. Only the current version's contents are indexed.

## Not on this page

Nothing is ever deleted for good: the Trash keeps every file, and every version keeps its bytes. There is no settings page for Documents; sharing and how long a link may live are set for you. Ask us if you need either changed.

## Who can do what

- **Owners** do everything: make owners-only folders, move, delete and give an email address to folders, delete tags, reset a locked share link, and everything below.
- **Staff** upload, rename, file, tag, version, trash and restore files, make and rename folders, create tags, share links, save views, and write templates. They never see owners-only folders.
- **Your accountant** can open, preview, download and search everything staff can see, and nothing else. Each screen says so in a line under the tabs, and the controls that would write are not drawn — including drag-and-drop, which takes no file from them rather than accepting one and then refusing it.
