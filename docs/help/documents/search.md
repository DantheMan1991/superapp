# Search

> Finding files by what they are called and what they say: the search words, filtering by tag, the results, what Search cannot read, and saved views.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/documents/search
> **Order:** 40

Open **Search** in the Documents menu, or type in `Search files…` at the right of the menu on any Documents page and press {kbd:Enter}. The line under the title reads `Looks inside files as well as at their names, titles, descriptions and tags. Filter by tag with no search text at all.` Search matches names, titles and descriptions, and the contents of PDFs, spreadsheets and text files. To find every file carrying a tag, click the tag rather than typing its name.

## What you see

- **Before you search.** `Type in the box above. Quoted phrases, or, and -word to exclude all work.`, and, once the business has tags, `Or browse by tag:` with every tag as a chip.
- **The active filters.** Each as a chip with an {icon:x} to drop it: your words in quotes, `in [folder]`, `from Receipts` or `cabinet files`, and each tag. {button:Clear all|ghost} drops them all.
- **The results.** `12 results`, and `· page 2` past the first page. Each row: the file's title or name, which opens the file in this tab, the folder it is in or `Inbox`, its size, `· from Receipts` for a receipt, its tags, the date, and its menu, {icon:more-horizontal}, with the same items as on the Inbox. {button:Previous|outline} and {button:Next|outline} turn the pages, fifty at a time.
- **The searchability note.** When some files could not be read, a line such as `3 files are not searchable by content — 2 scans with no text layer, 1 file of a type we cannot read yet. Those still match on their name, title, description and tags.` The parts it can mention: `not read yet`, `we could not read`, `too large to read`, `of a type we cannot read yet`, and `scans with no text layer`.
- **{button:Save this view|outline|bookmark}.** At the top right once you have searched or filtered.
- **`Saved views`.** A row of pills above the results, one per saved view, with `(private)` after one only you can see. The one you are looking at is outlined. An {icon:x} on a pill deletes it, for the person who saved it or an owner.

## How to search

1. Type your words and press {kbd:Enter}. Put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly, `or` between words to match either, and `-` before a word to leave files with it out.
2. Narrow the results by clicking a tag chip on any file, or a tag under `Or browse by tag:`. Every tag you add must be on the file.
3. Click a result to open the file, or its folder to go there.

Search reads the current version of each file. It does not read scans, photos, Word or PowerPoint files; those are found by their name, title and tags only. The viewer says whether a file's contents are searchable.

## How to save a view

1. Search or filter, then click {button:Save this view|outline|bookmark}. The dialog reads `Saves the filters, not the results — the view always shows what matches today.`
2. Give it a `Name`, such as `Open permits`.
3. Pick `Who sees it`: `Everyone in the business`, or `Only me`. The note reads `A private view hides the shortcut from colleagues. It does not hide the files — those follow their folder's own rules.`
4. Click {button:Save view|primary}. You see `View saved`, and the pill appears under the title. Up to 30 views a person.

To drop a view, click the {icon:x} on its pill. You see `View deleted`. A view cannot be edited; save a new one.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing matches those filters.` | No file matches. Try fewer words, or drop a tag. |
| `That's as far as results go — try narrowing the search.` | Results stop after a thousand. Add a word or a tag. |
| `There's nothing to save yet — search for something or pick a tag first.` | A view needs at least one filter. |
| `You already have a saved view with that name.` | Pick another name. |
| `That's the maximum number of saved views.` | You have 30. Delete one first. |

## Not on this page

There is no total count of results, no sorting, no snippet of the matching text, and no date filter. Filtering by folder or by source has no control here; a saved view or a link can carry one. A view cannot be edited or reordered, and a view over a folder or tag that has since been deleted quietly matches more than its name says. Ask us if you need any of these.

## Who can do what

Everyone can search. Owners and staff save views; an owner can delete any view, staff their own. Accountants see the buttons, and each answers `Accountant access is read-only — reviews, sign-offs and exports only.`
