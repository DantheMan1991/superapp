# Templates

> Documents you write once and fill in every time after: the list, making a template, and where its finished documents go.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/documents/templates
> **Order:** 60

Open **Templates** in the Documents menu. The line under the title reads `Write a document once, fill in the blanks every time after. A published version is frozen, so you can always tell what a document said on the day it went out.` To start one, click {button:New template|primary|plus}.

## What you see

- **The list.** Each template's name and description. Click one to open it. The list does not say whether a template is published or still a draft; that is on the template's own page. See [A template](template.md).
- **`Archived`.** Under the live templates, the ones you have archived, grayed. They open too.

## How to make a template

1. Click {button:New template|primary|plus}. The dialog reads `Name it after the document it produces — “Lien Waiver”, “Change Order”.`
2. Type the `Name`, and a `Description` if you want one.
3. Click {button:Create|primary}. The template opens, empty, ready to be written.

Two templates cannot share a name, and an archived one keeps its name.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `No templates yet.` | None have been made. Click {button:New template|primary|plus}. |
| `A template with that name already exists.` | Pick another name. An archived template keeps its name too. |
| `Give the template a name.` | The name was blank. |

## Not on this page

A template's name and description cannot be changed once it is made. There is no search or sorting here.

## Who can do what

Owners and staff make and edit templates. Accountants can read them, and every button answers `Accountant access is read-only — reviews, sign-offs and exports only.`
