# Trash

> Files you have moved to the trash, and how to put one back where it was.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/documents/trash
> **Order:** 90

Open **Trash** in the Documents menu. The line under the title reads `Restoring puts a file back where it was filed. Nothing here is ever permanently deleted.` A file gets here through `Move to trash` in its menu on Browse, the Inbox or Search. To bring one back, open its menu and click `Restore`.

## What you see

- **Each row.** The file's title or name, and under it the size and when it was trashed, such as `1.2 MB · trashed 9/1/2026`. The name is not a link; a trashed file cannot be opened from here. At the right, its menu, {icon:more-horizontal}, with one item, `Restore`.
- **The newest 200** trashed files are shown.

## How to restore a file

1. Click {icon:more-horizontal} on the row and then `Restore`. You see `File restored`.
2. The file is back in the folder it was in, or in the Inbox if it was unfiled. If its folder was deleted meanwhile, it is in that folder's parent, or the Inbox.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `The trash is empty` and `Anything you delete lands here first, and can be put back where it was filed.` | Nothing has been trashed. |

## Not on this page

Nothing here can be deleted for good, and nothing is ever deleted on its own; the trash keeps every file. There is no search, no bulk restore and no viewer. A receipt trashed from the Documents Inbox goes to the Receipts trash in Accounting, not here. Folders are never trashed; deleting one moves its contents up a level. A file that was trashed while a share link pointed at it makes that link stop working, though the link still reads `Active` on the Shared links page.

## Who can do what

Owners and staff restore. Accountants see the list, with a line under the tabs saying that is all. `Restore` is not drawn for them.
