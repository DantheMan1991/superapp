# Your signature

> The few lines added to the bottom of every message you write from this mailbox. Write them here, save them once, and they apply in Outlook and on your phone as well.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/email?signature=1
> **Order:** 70
> **Area:** Writing

Open **Mail**, then click `Signature` in the row of small links along the top, to the right of {button:Write|outline|pen-square}. The panel opens where you normally read a message. Type your sign off, click {button:Save signature|primary|save}, and it goes on the bottom of everything you write from then on. On a narrow phone screen that row of links is not drawn at all, so open this on a computer or a tablet.

## What you see

- **`Signature` and `Close`.** The panel's name on the left. Click `Close` at the top right to leave without saving. Opening this panel closes a message you were reading and a message you were writing, and anything typed into that unsent message is gone. While the panel is open, clicking a message in the list beside it does not open the message. {button:Write|outline|pen-square} does not start a new one either. Click `Close` first.
- **The shared mailbox warning.** Only on a mailbox you share with colleagues, the one marked `Shared` at the top of the screen. It names the address, then reads `The mail server keeps one signature per mailbox, so saving this sets it for everyone who uses this address — and replaces whatever a colleague set.` Take that literally. There is one signature for the whole address, and saving replaces the one a colleague wrote.
- **The line above the box.** `Added to the bottom of messages you send from` and then your sending address. Your signature is kept on the mail server against that address rather than inside Yosher, which is why it is the same signature in Outlook, on your phone and here.
- **The note about a second address.** Shown only when the mailbox can send from more than one address: `This mailbox has more than one send address. This edits the signature for the one above — the address Yosher composes from.` There is one signature per mailbox and no picker for the others.
- **The box.** It opens holding the signature already on the server, so edit it in place. It is the same editor you write messages in, so it is also the preview. What you see in the box is what recipients get. A picture is the exception, and it is dropped when you save.
- **The toolbar, left to right.** `Font`, `Size`, undo, redo, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color, align, bulleted list, numbered list, decrease indent, increase indent, quote, insert link, remove link, insert emoji, clear formatting. Hover an icon to see its name.
- **`Font` and `Size`.** Nine fonts: `Sans Serif`, `Serif`, `Fixed Width`, `Wide`, `Narrow`, `Garamond`, `Georgia`, `Tahoma`, `Verdana`. Four sizes: `Small`, `Normal`, `Large`, `Huge`. Each row is drawn in the font or the size it sets. Sizes are relative, so a reader who has made their own text bigger keeps it bigger.
- **Color.** The icon named `Text and highlight colour` opens two grids side by side, headed `Text` and `Highlight`. Both hold the same 36 colors. Pick one and it applies to the words you selected.
- **Align.** The `Align` icon opens three buttons: `Align left`, `Align centre`, `Align right`. There is no justify.
- **Indent.** `Increase indent` moves a line right. Click it again to move it further. `Decrease indent` moves it back.
- **Emoji.** The icon named `Insert emoji` opens a search box, `Search emoji`, above seven groups of them. Type to narrow the list. Nothing matching shows `Nothing matches “[what you typed]”.` Click one to drop it in at the cursor.
- **Tidying up.** `Remove link` takes the link off the words you selected and keeps the words. `Clear formatting` puts the selected words back to plain text. `Quote` turns a line into a quoted block, which a signature rarely wants.
- **Keys.** {kbd:Ctrl+B} bold, {kbd:Ctrl+I} italic, {kbd:Ctrl+U} underline, {kbd:Ctrl+Z} undo, {kbd:Ctrl+Y} redo, {kbd:Ctrl+K} link, {kbd:Ctrl+Shift+8} bulleted list, {kbd:Ctrl+Shift+7} numbered list.
- **Pasting.** Everything you paste arrives as plain text. Copy your old signature out of Outlook or a web page and you get the words, never the fonts, colors, links or logo. Nothing on screen warns you, so set the formatting again here.
- **The line under the box.** `A -- separator line is added for you if it is not there. That is what stops your signature being quoted back into every reply in a thread.` You do not type those two dashes. Yosher adds the line when you save, and it recognizes one that is already there rather than adding a second. Open the panel again and that line sits at the top of the box.
- **{button:Save signature|primary|save}.** Writes the signature to the mail server. The icon becomes a spinner and the box cannot be typed in until it finishes.
- **{button:Clear|outline}.** Puts the box back to the signature that was on the server when you opened the panel, and drops anything you typed since. Read how to turn your signature off below before you use it to remove one.
- **When the mail server will not answer.** The panel sometimes shows one line and nothing else, no box and no buttons: `Your mail server didn't answer when we asked for your current signature, so there is nothing safe to edit here yet. Try again in a moment.` That is deliberate. An empty box you could save over the top of would be how a working signature gets deleted by accident.

## How to write your signature

1. Click `Signature` along the top of Mail.
2. Check the address in the line above the box. That address is the one this signature belongs to.
3. Type your name, your role and however you want to be reached. Keep it to a few lines.
4. Select any words you want to change, then use the toolbar. Bold, italic, underline, fonts, sizes, colors, lists, indents and links all work.
5. Click {button:Save signature|primary|save}. You see `Signature saved.` and the panel closes back to your mail.
6. The next message you start carries the new signature. So does the next one you write from Outlook or your phone.

The box holds about 20,000 characters, and formatting counts toward that as well as words. A signature longer than that is refused with `Invalid input`, and there is no counter to warn you first. A normal sign off is nowhere near it.

## How to put a link in your signature

1. Select the words the link should sit on, or click where it goes.
2. Press {kbd:Ctrl+K}, or click the icon named `Insert link`. A strip opens across the top of the box.
3. `Text to show` starts with whatever you selected. Change it if you want different words.
4. Fill in the second box, `example.com or name@example.com`.
5. Click `Add`, or press Enter. `Cancel` or Escape closes the strip and adds nothing.
6. A plain web address gets `https://` put in front of it. An email address becomes a link that opens a new message. Anything else, such as a file path on your computer, is refused, the strip closes and no link is added, with nothing on screen to tell you.

## How to check where it lands in a message

1. Click {button:Write|outline|pen-square}. Your signature is already in the message, under an empty first line where the words go.
2. Open a message and click {button:Reply|outline} or {button:Forward|outline}. Your signature sits above the quoted message, not at the very bottom of the page.
3. There is no way to leave the signature off one message. Delete it out of that message by hand before you send.

## How to turn your signature off

1. Click in the box, press {kbd:Ctrl+A} to select everything in it, then press Delete.
2. Click {button:Save signature|primary|save}. You see `Signature saved.` Nothing is added to your messages after that, here or in any other mail program.
3. {button:Clear|outline} does not do this. Its message reads `Cleared — press Save to remove your signature.`, but the box comes back holding the signature that was on the server when you opened the panel. Pressing Save then saves that same signature again. Empty the box yourself as in step 1.

Saving cannot be undone and there is no history of past signatures, so copy the old one somewhere first if you may want it back.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Signature saved.` | It is on the mail server. The panel closes and your next message carries it. |
| `Cleared — press Save to remove your signature.` | The box was reset to the signature that was on the server when you opened the panel. It removes nothing on its own, and pressing Save now saves that signature again. |
| `Your mail server didn't answer when we asked for your current signature, so there is nothing safe to edit here yet. Try again in a moment.` | Your current signature could not be read, so there is nothing to edit. Close the panel and open it again in a minute. |
| `Invalid input` | The signature is longer than 20,000 characters, formatting included. Shorten it. |
| `That mailbox isn't connected.` | The mailbox is no longer connected to Yosher. See [Connecting your mailbox](connect.md). |
| `This mailbox needs to be reconnected before it can be read again.` | The mailbox needs signing in to again before anything can be saved. |
| `Couldn't reach the mail server. Your mail is safe — try again shortly.` | The mail server did not answer. Nothing changed. Try again. |
| `The mail server wouldn't send that message. Nothing was delivered — check Drafts.` | The mail server refused to save the signature. Nothing was sent and nothing is in Drafts, whatever this says. Your old signature is still in place. Try again, and tell us if it keeps happening. |
| `Accountant access is read-only — reviews, sign-offs and exports only.` | You are signed in as the accountant, who has no mail access. |
| `Something went wrong. Please try again.` | Something unexpected failed. Nothing was saved. |
| `Nothing matches “[what you typed]”.` | No emoji matches your search. |

## Not on this page

A picture cannot go in a signature. There is no button for one and pasting brings text only, because the picture would have to be attached to each message separately and nothing does that yet. If your signature was built elsewhere and has a logo in it, saving here drops the logo without saying so, and a signature that is nothing but a logo saves as empty and leaves you with no signature at all. Keep such a signature in Outlook and do not save it from this panel. A table around your details goes the same way, and comes back as plain text with its layout gone.

There is one signature per mailbox. A mailbox with several sending addresses cannot have a different signature per address, and on a shared mailbox everyone gets the one that was saved last. There is no separate signature for replies, no switch to leave it off a single message, and no second preview under the box, because the box is the preview. Saved replies you insert by hand are a different thing, in [Templates](templates.md). Writing a message is in [Writing a message](compose.md), and the reply that goes out while you are away is in [Automatic reply](away.md). Ask us if you need a signature per address or a logo in yours.

## Who can do what

Owners and staff do exactly the same things here. Anyone with the mailbox open can edit and save its signature, and on a shared mailbox that means colleagues can change each other's. Accountants have no mail access, and saving answers `Accountant access is read-only — reviews, sign-offs and exports only.`
