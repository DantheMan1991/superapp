# Workouts

> Your workout programs in one place: import the PDF you were given, or build one by hand, and follow it with the exercise videos playing right here.
> **Route:** /personal/m/fitness/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Workouts** {icon:dumbbell} in the sidebar of your personal space. This page lists your programs and any draft still waiting for you. To start, click {button:Import a program|primary|upload} and choose the PDF you were given, or click {button:Build one by hand|outline|plus}.

## What you see

- **`Workouts`**, the title, with the line `Follow a program you were given, or build your own, with the videos playing right here.`
- **{button:Build one by hand|outline|plus}.** Opens an empty program to fill in. See [Building and editing a program](editor.md).
- **{button:Import a program|primary|upload}.** Opens the import. See [Importing a program](import.md).
- **`Drafts`.** Appears only while an import is waiting for you. Each row shows the file's name and where it stands:
  - `Drafting, started just now` (or `2 minutes ago`) while Claude is reading it. This takes about a minute.
  - `Ready to review · 59 pages` with {button:Review|primary}, which opens the draft to check and save.
  - The reason it failed, in red, with {button:Discard draft|outline}, which throws it away after asking.
- **`Your programs`.** One card per saved program: its name, who wrote it, how many phases and exercises it has, and {badge:Imported} or {badge:By hand}. Click a card to open the program.
- **When you have no programs and no drafts yet**, a box reads `Start with the program you were given`, with the same two buttons.

## How to start with a program you were given

1. Click {button:Import a program|primary|upload}.
2. Choose the PDF. It is read on your device, and Claude drafts the program from its words and links.
3. Check the draft, fix anything that is wrong, and click {button:Save program|primary}.
4. You land on the program, ready to follow.

## Not on this page

- **Doing a workout.** A screen for the session itself, with the breaths counted for you and your sets logged, is next. Until then, open the program and follow it exercise by exercise.
- **Your progress.** Days done, the week against the program's target, and when the next phase opens come after that.
- **Putting a program away without deleting it.** Not built. You can delete a program from its edit screen.

## Who can do what

Only you. Workouts lives in your personal space, and nobody else can open it.
