# The posture check

> Measure how you stand with your phone's camera and small stickers on your bones. This page says what you need, how to set up the room, and where each sticker goes, and it is where you check your setup.
> **Route:** /personal/m/fitness/posture
> **Order:** 50

Open **Workouts** {icon:dumbbell} and, on the `Posture check` card, click {button:Open|outline}. This page gets you ready: the things to buy, the room, and the stickers. When you have them, click {button:Check your setup|primary}. The check itself, from all four sides with its report, comes in a later update.

Nothing the camera sees leaves your phone. Every picture is read on the phone and forgotten straight away. The check keeps numbers, not pictures.

## What you see

- **`Posture check`**, the title, with {icon:scan-line} and the line `Measure how you stand, with your phone's camera and small stickers on your bones.`
- **`Nothing the camera sees leaves your phone`**, a card saying what happens to the pictures, and what to wear: snug shorts or briefs pushed down below your front hip bones and the dimples above your buttocks. The check reads just as well that way.
- **`What you need`**, a list:
  - a tripod, or anything that holds the phone upright and still at hip height;
  - round matte stickers, 19 to 25 mm across: blue for your left side, green for your right. Office color-coding dots work. Glossy ones shine and hide their color;
  - a plumb line: a dark cord about 5 mm thick with a weight on the end, and two pieces of bright red or orange tape around it exactly 1 m apart;
  - painter's tape for a foot outline on the floor;
  - someone to put on the three stickers you cannot reach well.
- **`Set up the room`**, a drawing of the room from above, and a list:
  - a plain matte wall behind you, gray or white. Not blue or green, because those are the stickers' colors;
  - a foot outline taped about 0.6 m out from the wall. Stand in it every time;
  - the plumb line beside the outline, the same distance from the phone as you, hanging from above the top of the picture to the floor;
  - the phone 3 to 3.5 m from the outline, upright, at hip height, with the camera on its back facing you. Keep the height and distance the same every time;
  - soft, even light from beside the phone, and no window, mirror or other person in the picture;
  - a warm room, because shivering is movement.
- **`Where the stickers go`**, drawings of the front, the back and the right side with each sticker's spot: blue on your left side, green on your right, white on the middle. Under them, one row for each spot, with how to find the bone under it:
  - `Base of the neck` {badge:Needs a helper}, and `Top of the breastbone`, one sticker each, in either color;
  - `Ear`, `Shoulder tip`, `Front hip bone`, `Low back dimple` {badge:Needs a helper}, `Side hip bone`, `Outer knee`, `Kneecap`, `Front of the ankle` and `Outer ankle bone`, one on each side.
- **`Check your setup`**, a card with {button:Check your setup|primary}, which opens the setup check. See [Checking your setup](posture-setup.md).
  - **`Last setup check on this phone`** and its date, once you have run one on this phone, with {button:Copy its readout|outline|copy}, which copies that check's readout again. The button reads `Copied` when it worked.
- **A last line** saying the check itself comes next, and that it describes how you stood that day, for fitness and body awareness, not as a medical assessment.

## How to get ready for your first check

1. Buy the stickers, the cord and the tape on the list under `What you need`.
2. Set up the room as the drawing shows. Tape the foot outline, hang the plumb line beside it, and put the tripod 3 to 3.5 m away.
3. Put the stickers on standing, on clean dry skin, in the same order each time. Follow the words in each row under `Where the stickers go`. Ask someone to put on the base of the neck and the two low back dimples.
4. Click {button:Check your setup|primary}. See [Checking your setup](posture-setup.md).

## Not on this page

- **The check itself and its report.** They come in the next update. This page and the setup check are the first part.
- **Your earlier checks.** Nothing is saved yet.
- **A picture of you.** None is ever kept by the account. When the check comes, a photo you choose to keep stays on the phone that took it.

## Who can do what

Only you. The posture check is part of Workouts, in your personal space, and nobody else can open it.
