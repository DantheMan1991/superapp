# Finding your side

> Take a program's side self-assessment: a few tests, each answered with which side went further. The app works out which side you lean to, and the exercises the program does on one side follow it.
> **Route:** /personal/m/fitness/programs/*/side
> **Order:** 33

Open a program that has a self-assessment. Under `Your side`, click {button:Take the tests|outline}. Watch the author's video, do each test, and say which side went further. Then click {button:Save my side|primary}. From your next workout, each exercise the program does on one side is done on your side only.

Some tests count the other way round: on those, the left going further means you lean right. You only say what you saw. The app counts each test the way the program's table says.

## What you see

- **`Find your side`**, the title, with the program's name and how many tests it has, for example `Starter Mobility: 5 tests, a few minutes.`
- **The video**, when the program has one: the author's video of the tests. Tap `Tap to play here` to play it on this page. A video that only plays on YouTube reads `This video only plays on YouTube.` with {button:Open in YouTube|link|external-link}.
- **`Watch how each test is done. Then do each one, and say which side went further. The app works out what it means.`**
- **What the program says about the result**, in gray, when it says anything.
- **When you have taken the tests before**, when and what they found, for example `Taken Sep 29: you lean left.`
- **{button:Start the tests|primary}.** Opens the first test. When you came back to the video part way, it reads {button:Back to the tests|primary} and opens the first test you have not answered, or your result when you have answered them all.
- **{button:Back to the program|ghost}.** Leaves without saving anything.

### A test

- **A bar** with a part for each test, filled up to the one you are on, and `Test 2 of 5`.
- **The test's name**, and its question, for example `Which side went further?`
- **{button:Left|outline}, {button:About the same|outline} and {button:Right|outline}.** Tap what you saw, and the next test opens. `About the same` points to neither side. When you come back to a test, the answer you gave is highlighted.
- **{button:Back|ghost|arrow-left}.** Opens the test before, or the video from the first test.
- **{button:Watch the video again|ghost|play}.** Opens the video. Your answers are kept.

### Your result

- **`Your result`**, and what the tests found: `You lean left`, `You lean right`, or `No clear side`.
- **How the tests add up**, for example `4 point left, 1 point right. The program needs 3 to agree.` A side needs at least the program's number of tests, and more than the other side.
- **Each test**, with what you said and where it points, for example `Arm sweep` with `Right went further → Left`. A test answered `About the same` points to `no side`.
- **`What changes`**, when a side was found: each exercise the program does on one side, with its phase and your side for it, for example `Phase 2 · Knee sway` and `Lying on your left side`.
- **With no clear side**, `Every exercise stays on both sides. You can take the tests again any time.`
- **{button:Save my side|primary}** saves what the tests found, and opens the program. It reads {button:Save the result|primary} when no side was found. You see `Your side is saved: left.`, or `Saved. No side is clear, so every exercise stays on both sides.`
- **{button:Redo|ghost}.** Clears your answers and opens the first test again.
- **`5 tests answered. Saving replaces any side saved before.`**

## How to find your side

1. Open the program and, under `Your side`, click {button:Take the tests|outline}.
2. Watch the video, then click {button:Start the tests|primary}.
3. Do each test and tap {button:Left|outline}, {button:About the same|outline} or {button:Right|outline} for which side went further.
4. Read your result. To change an answer, click {button:Redo|ghost} and go through the tests again.
5. Click {button:Save my side|primary}. You see `Your side is saved: left.` and the program opens, with `Your side: Left` under Reminders.

To take them again later, click {button:Redo the tests|outline} under `Your side`. The new result replaces the old one.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Your answers could not be read. Take the tests again.` | What you answered could not be sent. Take the tests again. |
| `The program's tests changed while you were taking them. Take them again.` | The program was edited, for example in another tab, while you were answering. Take the tests again. |
| `This program has no self-assessment to take.` | The self-assessment was removed from the program while you were answering. |
| `That program is not here any more. It may have been deleted.` | The program was deleted after you opened this page. |
| `Your side could not be saved. Try again.` | Something went wrong. Click {button:Save my side|primary} again. |
| `This program has no self-assessment. Add one in the editor, or read the program's PDF again to find it.` | Shown instead of the tests on a program without them. See [Building and editing a program](editor.md) or [Reading the PDF again](read-again.md). |

## Not on this page

- **Doing a one-sided exercise on both sides for one session.** Your side holds for every session until you take the tests again.
- **Your earlier results.** Only the latest is kept.

## Who can do what

Only you. Your side lives in your personal space.
