# Cooking with cook mode

> A recipe one step at a time in big type, with the screen kept on: gather the ingredients first, tap a time in a step to start a timer, and log that you made it when you finish. Turn on hands-free and the steps are read aloud while the phone listens for "next step" and a few other phrases.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/recipes/*/cook
> **Order:** 40

Open a recipe and click {button:Cook|food|chef-hat}. Cook mode opens on the ingredients, at the servings you chose on the recipe page. The screen stays on while cook mode is open, so you never have to wake the phone with your hands full.

## What you see

- **{button:Recipe|ghost|arrow-left}**, at the top. It goes back to the recipe page. Cook mode keeps your place: open it again and you are where you left it.
- **The recipe's name and what you are making**, such as `Grandma Ida's Skillet Cornbread · 12 wedges`, with {button:Hands-free|outline|mic-off} beside it. That turns hands-free on: see [Hands-free](#hands-free) below.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **Gather the ingredients**, the first screen, with `Tick each one as you set it out.` Every ingredient is listed at your servings, its amount in colour. Tick a box as you set the ingredient out and the line is crossed out. Then click {button:Start cooking|primary}. A recipe with no steps says `No steps written down.`, and the button says {button:Done cooking|primary} instead.
- **A step**, one at a time:
  - `Step 2 of 4` at the top, and {button:Ingredients|outline|checks}, which opens the whole list at your servings, with the same ticks.
  - The heading of the step's group, when the recipe has one, such as `For the sauce`.
  - The step itself, in big type. Every time in it is a button with a {icon:timer}, such as `25 to 30 minutes`: tap it to start a timer for it.
  - `Uses:` and the ingredients the step names, at your servings, such as `Uses: 4 tbsp butter, melted`. They are found from the step's words, so a step that says "the dry ingredients" shows none; {button:Ingredients|outline|checks} always has them all.
  - {button:Back|outline} and {button:Next|primary}, or {button:Done cooking|primary} on the last step. Back from the first step goes to the ingredients.
- **The timers**, under the step once you have started one, with `Timers keep running between steps.` Each shows where it came from, such as `Step 4 · 25–30 min`, and the time left. {button:1|ghost|plus} adds a minute; {button:×|ghost|x} stops it.
- **A timer that ends**: a red box at the top, such as `Step 4 · 25–30 min: time's up`, with `Check it now. It can take up to 30 min.` for a time the recipe gave as a range, or `Done.` for one time. It chimes, and buzzes on an Android phone, every second and a half until you click {button:Stop|primary}, or {button:1 min|outline|plus} for one more minute.
- **Done. Enjoy it.**, after the last step:
  - {button:Log that you made it|primary}, with `The recipe shows how many times you have made it, and when last.` Once logged it says, for example, `Logged: made today, for 12 wedges.`, with {button:Undo|ghost|undo} if you logged it by mistake.
  - {button:Log what you ate|outline}. Opens Log food with this recipe chosen, to log the servings you ate on a meal. Logging that you made it counts the batch; this is what you ate. See [Logging what you ate](log.md).
  - {button:Back|outline}, to the last step, and {button:Back to the recipe|outline}.

## How to cook a recipe

1. On the recipe page, set the servings with {button:|ghost|minus} and {button:|ghost|plus} beside `Ingredients`, then click {button:Cook|food|chef-hat}.
2. Set out each ingredient and tick it. Click {button:Start cooking|primary}.
3. Read the step. When it names a time, tap the time to start its timer. Click {button:Next|primary} when the step is done; timers keep running.
4. When a timer rings, check the food, then click {button:Stop|primary}, or {button:1 min|outline|plus} if it needs longer.
5. After the last step, click {button:Done cooking|primary}, then {button:Log that you made it|primary}.

## Hands-free

Hands-free reads each screen aloud and listens for a few short phrases, so you can cook with flour on your hands and never touch the phone.

### Turning it on

1. Click {button:Hands-free|outline|mic-off} beside the recipe's name.
2. The first time on a phone, a box called **Hands-free** says what happens:
   - **How it listens**: a small listener downloads once (about 15 MB) and runs on this phone. It only knows the phrases below, and nothing you say leaves the phone.
   - **What you can say**: the phrases, each in a coloured chip.
   - **The voice**: the recorded voice the workout coach uses, such as `Arcas`.
   - `Your browser asks once for the microphone.` (in the Yosher app, `The app asks once for the microphone.`)

   Click {button:Turn on hands-free|primary|mic}, or {button:Not now|outline} to close the box and leave it off. After the first time, the switch turns hands-free on straight away.
3. Allow the microphone when your browser or the app asks. It asks once.
4. The first time, the listener downloads. A box under the recipe's name says `Getting hands-free ready · 42%`, with a bar that fills. After that it starts in a moment, because the phone keeps it.
5. The switch becomes {button:Hands-free on|primary|mic} at once. When the listener is ready, the screen you are on is read aloud, and the box says **Reading**, then **Listening**.

### The hands-free box

While hands-free is on, a box under the recipe's name says what the phone is doing:

- **Listening**, with a pulsing dot: say a phrase.
- **{icon:volume-2} Reading**: the voice is speaking. The phone does not listen while it reads, so it never hears itself. Wait for it to finish, then speak.
- **{icon:mic-off} Paused**: you came back from another app or a locked screen, and the microphone is starting again.
- `Say “next step”, “go back”, “repeat”, “start timer” or “ingredients”.` While a timer rings, `“stop timer”` takes the place of `“start timer”`.
- After a phrase, what it heard and did, for a few seconds: `Heard “next step”.`, or `Heard “start timer”: 25–30 min started.`

### What you can say

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| “next step” | The next step comes up and is read aloud. On the ingredients, the first step does. After the last step, the voice says “That was the last step.” |
| “go back”, or “previous step” | The step before comes up and is read. From the first step, the ingredients come back. On the ingredients, the voice says “This is the start.” |
| “repeat” | The screen is read again. |
| “start timer” | A timer starts for the step's first time, just as tapping that time would, and the voice says, for example, “Timer started: 25 to 30 minutes.” Say it again for the step's next time. A step with no time: “There is no time in this step.” Every time in the step already running: “This step's timer is already running.” On the ingredients or the last screen: “There is no timer on this screen.” |
| “stop timer” | Only while a timer rings. It stops the one that has been ringing longest and says “Timer stopped.” For a time the recipe gave as a range, it adds how long it can take, such as “Check it now. It can take up to 30 minutes.” If another timer is still ringing: “Another one is ringing.” |
| “ingredients”, or “show ingredients” | On a step: the ingredient list opens, and the voice says what the step uses, such as “This step uses 1 and a half cups flour and a half teaspoon salt.”, or “This step names nothing from the list.” On the ingredients screen, the whole list is read. After the last step, the list opens and is read. |

Any phrase but “ingredients” closes the ingredient list if it is open. Say the phrase on its own, at a normal voice, with a short pause before and after. The phone listens only for these phrases, so talking in the kitchen does nothing; if a phrase is missed, say it again a little more clearly.

### What is read aloud

- Each screen as it comes up, whether a phrase or a tap brought it:
  - The ingredients: “Gather the ingredients, then say next step.”
  - A step, with its group's heading first when a new group starts, such as “For the sauce.”
  - After the last step: “Done. Enjoy it.”
- The step as the recipe wrote it, with shorthand said in full: `1 ½ cups` as “1 and a half cups”, `¼ cup` as “a quarter cup”, `tsp` as “teaspoon”, `9x13-inch` as “9 by 13 inch”, `400F` as “400 degrees Fahrenheit”.
- When a timer starts ringing, the voice stops at once, so you can say “stop timer” over the chime.

### The voice

- Hands-free reads in the recorded voice the workout coach uses, chosen on a workout's start screen. It is `Arcas` until you choose another there.
- Each line is recorded once, when hands-free is turned on, and kept on this phone. The words of the recipe are sent to the voice service Yosher uses to make the recording; what you say is not sent anywhere.
- If a recording has not arrived in time, or the recorded voice is not set up, the phone's own voice reads the line instead.

### Turning it off

- Click {button:Hands-free on|primary|mic}. The phone stops listening and reading at once.
- Leaving cook mode turns it off too. While another app is in front or the phone is locked, the microphone is let go; it listens again when you come back to cook mode.

## How times are found

- A number followed by a unit of time is a timer: `25 minutes`, `25 min`, `1 hour`, `1 hr 15 min`, `1 hour and 15 minutes`, `1½ hours`, `30 seconds`, `2h`. So are `half an hour`, `an hour` and `a minute`.
- A range, such as `25 to 30 minutes` or `25-30 min`, rings at the shorter time, and the red box says how long it can take.
- Not timers: temperatures (`400°F`), sizes (`2-inch pieces`), and times without a number (`overnight`, `until golden`).
- Several timers can run at once, on different steps.

## Sound, the buzz and the screen

- The screen stays on while cook mode is open, in the Yosher app and in Chrome. A phone in battery saver may still let it dim.
- The timers chime only while cook mode is on the screen. If you lock the phone or open another app, nothing sounds; when you come back, a timer that ended while you were away is ringing.
- A phone lets a page make sound only after a tap. Your first tap in cook mode (a tick, Start cooking, a time, or turning on hands-free) is that tap. A timer started by voice rings because turning hands-free on was a tap.
- An Android phone buzzes with the chime. An iPhone does not buzz from a web page.
- Turn the phone's volume up: the chime follows it.

## What is kept, and where

- Your place, your ticks and your timers are kept on this phone, for this recipe, for 12 hours. Another phone does not see them. After 12 hours, cook mode starts afresh.
- While a cook is under way, the recipe page says {button:Back to cooking|food|chef-hat} with where you are. A cook under way keeps the servings it started with: to cook for a different number, click {button:Start over|ghost} on the recipe page, set the servings, and click {button:Cook|food|chef-hat}.
- {button:Back to the recipe|outline} on the last screen clears them, unless a timer is still running: then they wait for the next time you open cook mode.
- What you log is kept in your account. The recipe page says how often, such as `Made 3 times, last on Sep 30.`, and your recipes show `made 3×` on its row.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `It could not be logged. Try again.` | The log did not reach Yosher, often because the phone has no signal. Your place is kept; try again in a moment. |
| `It could not be undone. Try again.` | The undo did not reach Yosher. The log is still there; try again. |
| `That recipe is not here any more.` | The recipe was deleted, maybe on another device, while cook mode was open. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent could not be read. Reload the page and try again. |
| `No ingredients written down.` | The recipe has no ingredient lines. Add them in the editor. |
| `No steps written down.` | The recipe has no steps. Add them in the editor, or go straight to Done cooking. |

Hands-free's messages appear in a red box under the recipe's name, with {button:Try again|outline|rotate-ccw} (when trying again can help) and {button:Turn off hands-free|ghost}. Cook mode still works by tapping whatever the box says.

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `The microphone is blocked for this site. Allow it in the browser's site settings, then try again.` | The microphone was refused, now or earlier. In Chrome, tap the icon left of the web address, allow the microphone, then click Try again. |
| `The microphone is blocked for the Yosher app. Allow it in the phone's settings, then try again.` | The app was refused the microphone. In the phone's settings, find the Yosher app's permissions, allow the microphone, then click Try again. |
| `No microphone would start. Another app may be using it.` | A call, a recording or another app is holding the microphone. Close it, then click Try again. |
| `Hands-free could not be downloaded. Check the connection, then try again.` | The listener's first download stopped, usually for want of signal. Nothing is half-kept; Try again starts it afresh. |
| `Hands-free needs a newer browser. Chrome, or the Yosher app, has what it needs.` | This browser cannot run the listener. There is no Try again: open cook mode in Chrome or the app. |
| `Hands-free would not start on this phone.` | The listener downloaded but would not run here. Cook by tapping; tell us through the feedback button. |

## Not here yet

- **Speaking over the voice.** The phone does not listen while it reads; say the phrase when it has finished.
- **Other phrases, or your own words for them.** It listens for the phrases above and nothing else.
- **A timer heard with cook mode off the screen.** The chime and the listening both need cook mode in front.
- **Amounts inside the steps.** They stay as the recipe wrote them, for its own servings; `Uses:` and the ingredient list are at yours.
- **Your history of cooks.** The recipe shows the count and the last day. Undo works right after logging; older logs cannot be changed yet.

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open.
