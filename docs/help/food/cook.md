# Cooking with cook mode

> A recipe one step at a time in big type, with the screen kept on: gather the ingredients first, tap a time in a step to start a timer, and log that you made it when you finish.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/recipes/*/cook
> **Order:** 40

Open a recipe and click {button:Cook|primary|chef-hat}. Cook mode opens on the ingredients, at the servings you chose on the recipe page. The screen stays on while cook mode is open, so you never have to wake the phone with your hands full.

## What you see

- **{button:Recipe|ghost|arrow-left}**, at the top. It goes back to the recipe page. Cook mode keeps your place: open it again and you are where you left it.
- **The recipe's name and what you are making**, such as `Grandma Ida's Skillet Cornbread · 12 wedges`.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **Gather the ingredients**, the first screen, with `Tick each one as you set it out.` Every ingredient is listed at your servings, its amount in colour. Tick a box as you set the ingredient out and the line is crossed out. Then click {button:Start cooking|primary}. A recipe with no steps says `No steps written down.`, and the button says {button:Done cooking|primary} instead.
- **A step**, one at a time:
  - `Step 2 of 4` at the top, and {button:Ingredients|outline|checks}, which opens the whole list at your servings, with the same ticks.
  - The heading of the step's group, when the recipe has one, such as `For the sauce`.
  - The step itself, in big type. Every time in it is a button with a {icon:timer}, such as `25 to 30 minutes`: tap it to start a timer for it.
  - `Uses:` and the ingredients the step names, at your servings, such as `Uses: 4 tbsp butter, melted`. They are found from the step's words, so a step that says "the dry ingredients" shows none; {button:Ingredients|outline|checks} always has them all.
  - {button:Back|outline} and {button:Next|primary}, or {button:Done cooking|primary} on the last step. Back from the first step goes to the ingredients.
- **The timers**, under the step once you have started one, with `Timers keep running between steps.` Each shows where it came from, such as `Step 4 · 25–30 min`, and the time left. {button:1|ghost|plus} adds a minute; {button:×|ghost|x} stops it.
- **A timer that ends**: a red box at the top, such as `Step 4 · 25–30 min: time's up`, with `Check it now. It can take up to 30 min.` for a time the recipe gave as a range, or `Done.` for one time. It chimes, and buzzes on an Android phone, every second and a half until you click {button:Stop|primary}, or {button:1 min|outline|plus} for one more minute.
- **Done. Enjoy it.**, after the last step:
  - {button:Log that you made it|primary}, with `The recipe shows how many times you have made it, and when last.` Once logged it says, for example, `Logged: made today, for 12 wedges.`, with {button:Undo|ghost|undo} if you logged it by mistake.
  - {button:Back|outline}, to the last step, and {button:Back to the recipe|outline}.

## How to cook a recipe

1. On the recipe page, set the servings with {button:−|outline|minus} and {button:+|outline|plus}, then click {button:Cook|primary|chef-hat}.
2. Set out each ingredient and tick it. Click {button:Start cooking|primary}.
3. Read the step. When it names a time, tap the time to start its timer. Click {button:Next|primary} when the step is done; timers keep running.
4. When a timer rings, check the food, then click {button:Stop|primary}, or {button:1 min|outline|plus} if it needs longer.
5. After the last step, click {button:Done cooking|primary}, then {button:Log that you made it|primary}.

## How times are found

- A number followed by a unit of time is a timer: `25 minutes`, `25 min`, `1 hour`, `1 hr 15 min`, `1 hour and 15 minutes`, `1½ hours`, `30 seconds`, `2h`. So are `half an hour`, `an hour` and `a minute`.
- A range, such as `25 to 30 minutes` or `25-30 min`, rings at the shorter time, and the red box says how long it can take.
- Not timers: temperatures (`400°F`), sizes (`2-inch pieces`), and times without a number (`overnight`, `until golden`).
- Several timers can run at once, on different steps.

## Sound, the buzz and the screen

- The screen stays on while cook mode is open, in the Yosher app and in Chrome. A phone in battery saver may still let it dim.
- The timers chime only while cook mode is on the screen. If you lock the phone or open another app, nothing sounds; when you come back, a timer that ended while you were away is ringing.
- A phone lets a page make sound only after a tap. Your first tap in cook mode (a tick, Start cooking or a time) is that tap.
- An Android phone buzzes with the chime. An iPhone does not buzz from a web page.
- Turn the phone's volume up: the chime follows it.

## What is kept, and where

- Your place, your ticks and your timers are kept on this phone, for this recipe, for 12 hours. Another phone does not see them. After 12 hours, cook mode starts afresh.
- While a cook is under way, the recipe page says {button:Back to cooking|primary|chef-hat} with where you are. A cook under way keeps the servings it started with: to cook for a different number, click {button:Start over|ghost} on the recipe page, set the servings, and click {button:Cook|primary|chef-hat}.
- {button:Back to the recipe|outline} on the last screen clears them, unless a timer is still running: then they wait for the next time you open cook mode.
- What you log is kept in your account. The recipe page says how often, such as `Made 3 times, last on Sep 30.`, and the Food page shows `made 3×` on the recipe's row.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `It could not be logged. Try again.` | The log did not reach Yosher, often because the phone has no signal. Your place is kept; try again in a moment. |
| `It could not be undone. Try again.` | The undo did not reach Yosher. The log is still there; try again. |
| `That recipe is not here any more.` | The recipe was deleted, maybe on another device, while cook mode was open. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent could not be read. Reload the page and try again. |
| `No ingredients written down.` | The recipe has no ingredient lines. Add them in the editor. |
| `No steps written down.` | The recipe has no steps. Add them in the editor, or go straight to Done cooking. |

## Not here yet

- **Reading each step aloud, and saying "next".** They come together, later, so cook mode can be used without touching the phone.
- **Amounts inside the steps.** They stay as the recipe wrote them, for its own servings; `Uses:` and the ingredient list are at yours.
- **Your history of cooks.** The recipe shows the count and the last day. Undo works right after logging; older logs cannot be changed yet.

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open.
