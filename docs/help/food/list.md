# Your shopping list

> What the planned days need, added up across your recipes and sorted by aisle: tick things off in the shop, even with no signal, and add your own.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/list
> **Order:** 6

Open **Food** {icon:utensils} and click `List`, or click {button:Shopping list|food-soft|shopping-cart} on the week. The list is made from what you put on the week: put a meal on and what it needs comes here; take it off and it goes. Tap a thing to tick it off.

## What you see

### The top

- **`Shopping list`**, the title, with the days the list covers: above it on a phone, such as `Food · Oct 7 to 13`, and under it on a computer, such as `Oct 7 to 13`. To start, the list covers today and the six days after it.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **`Today`, `Week`, `Recipes` and `List`**, Food's sections. You are on `List`. `List` carries how many things are still to buy, the same number as the big one below.

### How much is left

A card at the top of the list.

- **How many things are still to buy**, in large type, such as `9 to buy`: what is on the list, less what you have ticked off and what you said you have at home, plus your own items not yet ticked. A staple the list is still asking you about is not counted. Once everything is ticked it says `Nothing left to buy`, and with nothing on the list at all, `Nothing to buy`.
- **Under it**, what the days have planned, such as `For 2 recipes to cook and 1 food.`, or `Nothing is planned for these days.` With nothing planned and nothing on the list, it adds `Put meals on the week, and what they need comes here.`, with {button:Go to the week|food-soft}.
- **On the next line**, how many things are ticked, such as `5 things ticked.`, or `Nothing ticked yet.`
- **A bar** that fills orange as you tick things off.
- **{button:Change days|food-soft|calendar-days}** opens `From` and `to`. `From` can be any day of this week or next week, up to the last day; `to` can be today or any day after the first, up to next Sunday. Under them: `A new first day is a new trip: it starts with nothing ticked. A batch is bought for the day it is cooked.` Click it again to close them.
- **Once you tick something**, the days stay as they are until their last day has gone, so the list does not move under you; then a new list starts from that day.

### While new lines are sorted

- **`Sorting 12 ingredients into aisles. It takes a few seconds.`**, while Claude names what new lines buy. It shows once for each new line: a recipe you put on the week is usually sorted before you open the list.
- If it could not be done, `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time.` and {button:Try again|food-soft}. Until then, those lines are under `Not sorted yet`, as the recipe wrote them.

### The aisles

A card for each aisle, in the order a shop is walked, each with its own picture: `Produce`, `Meat and fish`, `Dairy and eggs`, `Bread and bakery`, `Pantry`, `Frozen`, `Drinks` and `Other`. An aisle with nothing in it is not shown. On a computer the cards run down two columns.

- **Each thing to buy**: a circle to tick, its name in bold and how much, such as `Yellow onions, 3` or `Ground turkey, 1 ⅓ lb`, and under it what needs it, such as `Turkey chili`, or with more than one, `Turkey chili 1 ⅓ · Chicken rice bowl 2`.
- **Tap it** to tick it off: the circle fills orange, and the thing stays where it is, struck through and the same height, so the next row is still under your finger. Tap it again to untick it.
- If you ticked it and the week then asked for more, it comes back up with `You ticked 3 earlier; the week now needs more.`
- **`Not sorted yet`**, a card like an aisle's, for lines Claude has not named yet, as the recipe wrote them.

### Have these at home?

Salt, pepper, oil, spices and the like, which most kitchens keep. Each has a circle to tick, like the rest, and:

- {button:Have it|food-soft}: you have it this time. It moves to the `At home:` line at the bottom of the card, which has `Need` and its name to put it back.
- {button:Always have|ghost}: you always have it. It is left off this list and every list after, until you put it back.

On a phone the two buttons are under the thing; on a computer, beside it. Tick one and its buttons go, but its row keeps its height, so nothing under your finger moves.

### Your items

Things to buy that no recipe asks for. Type one in the box (such as `Coffee beans`) and click {button:Add|food-soft|plus}, or press Enter. Each has a circle to tick it off, and {button:|ghost|x} takes it off the list. One you ticked is gone from the next list; one you did not stays on it.

### At the bottom

What you always have: `Left off, as you always have them: salt, black pepper.`, or `You always have 2 things: left off every list.` `Change` opens them, each with {button:Put back|ghost}, which puts it back on the list.

## How the list adds up

- **A recipe you cook is bought once, for the batch.** Cook 4 of a recipe that makes 6 and the list buys two thirds of each line. Its leftovers on later meals add nothing: they come from the batch.
- **The same thing across recipes is one line.** Claude names what each line buys, once, and its aisle; "1 cup rice" and "2 cups long-grain white rice" become one `Long-grain white rice`. The amounts are added up from your recipes by Yosher, never by Claude.
- **Weights add up across ounces, pounds and grams, and volumes across spoons, cups and milliliters.** A weight and a volume of the same thing are shown side by side, such as `4 cloves + 1 tsp`, because one cannot be turned into the other.
- **A range buys its larger end**: `2-3 cloves` buys 3.
- **A can of a size stays that size**: `2 cans (15 oz)`, never 30 oz.
- **A line with no amount**, such as `Salt and pepper to taste`, is listed without one. A line that buys two things lists both, each with the line's amount. Water is not listed.
- **A food you planned** is bought by its amount, such as `5 × banana`.

## Kept on this phone

The days, your ticks and your own items are kept on this phone, so the list works with no signal in the shop. Another phone or another browser has its own. What you always have is kept with your space, on every phone.

## How to shop from the week

1. Put the week's meals on, on `Week`.
2. Click `List`. Check the days: change them if this shop covers fewer.
3. Under `Have these at home?`, click {button:Have it|food-soft} for what you have, or {button:Always have|ghost} for what you never need to buy.
4. In the shop, tap each thing as it goes in the cart. The number at the top counts down, and the bar fills.
5. Next time, the list starts again on its own once the days have gone, or click {button:Change days|food-soft|calendar-days} and give it a new first day.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing to buy` | Nothing is on the list for these days. Put meals on the week, or change the days. |
| `Nothing left to buy` | Everything on the list is ticked off or at home. |
| `Nothing is planned for these days.` | The days have no meals on the week. Put some on, or change the days. |
| `Sorting 12 ingredients into aisles. It takes a few seconds.` | Claude is naming what new lines buy. Wait on the page. |
| `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time.` | Claude could not be reached. Click {button:Try again|food-soft}; meanwhile the lines are under `Not sorted yet`. |
| `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time. Try again.` | The same, as a note. Try again in a moment. |
| `You ticked 3 earlier; the week now needs more.` | You put a meal on the week after ticking it. Buy the difference, and tick it again. |
| `That could not be saved. Try again.` | Always have, or putting one back, did not work. It went back as it was. Try again. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Food keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
