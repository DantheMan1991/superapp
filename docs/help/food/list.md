# Your shopping list

> What the planned days need, added up across your recipes and sorted by aisle: tick things off in the shop, even with no signal, and add your own.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/list
> **Order:** 6

Open **Food** {icon:utensils} and click `List`, or click {button:Shopping list|outline|shopping-cart} on the week. The list is made from what you put on the week: put a meal on and what it needs comes here; take it off and it goes. Tap a thing to tick it off.

## What you see

- **`Food`**, the title, with the line `What you ate, the week ahead, and your recipes.`
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **`Today`, `Week`, `Recipes` and `List`**, Food's sections. You are on `List`. `List` carries how many things are still to buy on the list for its days, once you have ticked off what you got on this phone; a staple the list is asking you about is not counted.
- **The days the list covers**, such as `Shopping for Oct 7 to 13`: today and the six days after it to start. Once you tick something, the days stay as they are until their last day has gone, so the list does not move under you; then a new list starts from that day. Under it, what they have planned, such as `For 2 recipes to cook and 1 food.`, or `Nothing is planned for these days.`, and how many things are ticked, such as `5 things ticked.`
  - **{button:Change days|outline}** opens `From` and `to`. `From` can be any day of this week or next week, up to the last day; `to` can be today or any day after the first, up to next Sunday. Under them: `A new first day is a new trip: it starts with nothing ticked. A batch is bought for the day it is cooked.`
- **`Sorting 12 ingredients into aisles. It takes a few seconds.`**, while Claude names what new lines buy. It shows once for each new line: a recipe you put on the week is usually sorted before you open the list. If it could not be done, `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time.` and {button:Try again|outline}. Until then, those lines are under `Not sorted yet`, as the recipe wrote them.
- **With nothing to buy**: `Nothing to buy for these days yet.` and `Put meals on the week, and what they need comes here.`, with {button:Go to the week|outline}.
- **The aisles**, in the order a shop is walked: `Produce`, `Meat and fish`, `Dairy and eggs`, `Bread and bakery`, `Pantry`, `Frozen`, `Drinks` and `Other`. An aisle with nothing in it is not shown.
  - **Each thing to buy**: a box to tick, its name and how much, such as `Yellow onions, 3` or `Ground turkey, 1 ⅓ lb`, and under it what needs it, such as `Turkey chili`, or with more than one, `Turkey chili 1 ⅓ · Chicken rice bowl 2`.
  - **Tap it** to tick it off: it stays where it is, struck through, so the next row is still under your finger. Tap it again to untick it.
  - If you ticked it and the week then asked for more, it comes back up with `You ticked 3 earlier; the week now needs more.`
- **`Have these at home?`**: salt, pepper, oil, spices and the like, which most kitchens keep. Each has a box to tick, like the rest, and:
  - {button:Have it|outline}: you have it this time. It moves to the `At home:` line at the bottom of the box, which has `Need` and its name to put it back.
  - {button:Always have|ghost}: you always have it. It is left off this list and every list after, until you put it back.
- **`Your items`**: things to buy that no recipe asks for. Type one in the box (such as `Coffee beans`) and click {button:Add|outline|plus}, or press Enter. Tap one to tick it off; {button:|ghost|x} takes it off the list. One you ticked is gone from the next list; one you did not stays on it.
- **At the bottom**, what you always have: `Left off, as you always have them: salt, black pepper.`, or `You always have 2 things: left off every list.` `Change` opens them, each with {button:Put back|ghost}, which puts it back on the list.

## How the list adds up

- **A recipe you cook is bought once, for the batch.** Cook 4 of a recipe that makes 6 and the list buys two thirds of each line. Its leftovers on later meals add nothing: they come from the batch.
- **The same thing across recipes is one line.** Claude names what each line buys, once, and its aisle; "1 cup rice" and "2 cups long-grain white rice" become one `Long-grain white rice`. The amounts are added up from your recipes by Yosher, never by Claude.
- **Weights add up across ounces, pounds and grams, and volumes across spoons, cups and milliliters.** A weight and a volume of the same thing are shown side by side, such as `4 cloves + 1 tsp`, because one cannot be turned into the other.
- **A range buys its larger end**: `2-3 cloves` buys 3.
- **A can of a size stays that size**: `2 cans (15 oz)`, never 30 oz.
- **A line with no amount**, such as `Salt and pepper to taste`, is listed without one. A line that buys two things lists both, each with the line's amount. Water is not listed.
- **A food you planned** is bought by its amount, such as `5 × banana`.

## Kept on this phone

The days, your ticks and your own items are kept on this phone, so the list works with no signal in the shop. Another phone or another browser has its own. What you always have is kept with your space, on every phone.

## How to shop from the week

1. Put the week's meals on, on `Week`.
2. Click `List`. Check the days: change them if this shop covers fewer.
3. Under `Have these at home?`, click {button:Have it|outline} for what you have, or {button:Always have|ghost} for what you never need to buy.
4. In the shop, tap each thing as it goes in the cart.
5. Next time, the list starts again on its own once the days have gone, or click {button:Change days|outline} and give it a new first day.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing is planned for these days.` | The days have no meals on the week. Put some on, or change the days. |
| `Sorting 12 ingredients into aisles. It takes a few seconds.` | Claude is naming what new lines buy. Wait on the page. |
| `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time.` | Claude could not be reached. Click {button:Try again|outline}; meanwhile the lines are under `Not sorted yet`. |
| `The list could not be sorted into aisles this time. Try again.` | The same, as a note. Try again in a moment. |
| `You ticked 3 earlier; the week now needs more.` | You put a meal on the week after ticking it. Buy the difference, and tick it again. |
| `That could not be saved. Try again.` | Always have, or putting one back, did not work. It went back as it was. Try again. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Food keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
