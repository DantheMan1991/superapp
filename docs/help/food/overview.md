# Food

> What you ate today, with its calories, protein, carbs and fat, against your targets; what is up next from your week; and each meal. Search for what you ate right at the top, or take a photo of the plate. The week ahead, your recipes and your shopping list are a tab over.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Food** {icon:utensils} in the sidebar of your personal space. This page is your day: a greeting, how the day adds up against your targets, the meal you planned next, and every meal with what is in it. To log something on a computer, type it in the search box at the top. On a phone, tap `What did you eat?` at the bottom of the screen. What you planned for a meal waits in it: click {button:Ate it|food|check} once you have eaten it. The week ahead is under `Week`, your recipes under `Recipes`, and the shopping list under `List`.

## What you see

### The top

- **`Food`**, and under it a greeting for the time of day: `Good morning` from 4 in the morning, `Good afternoon` from 11, `Good evening` from 5. On a computer it has your first name, such as `Good afternoon, Dan.` On a phone the date is above it, such as `Food · Saturday, Oct 3`.
- **The date**, on a computer, such as `Saturday, Oct 3`. When a meal later today is planned, it says so: `Saturday, Oct 3 · dinner's already planned.`
- **An earlier day** has its date as the title, such as `Thursday, Oct 1`, and no greeting.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **The day.** On a computer, a pill with {button:|ghost|chevron-left}, the day (`Today`, `Yesterday`, or a date), and {button:|ghost|chevron-right}. On a phone, {button:|outline|chevron-left} on its own, and {button:|outline|chevron-right} too on an earlier day. The left arrow goes to the day before, back to two weeks ago, so you can log a meal you forgot. The right arrow goes to the day after, up to today. An arrow that cannot go further is grayed out.

### The tabs

**`Today`, `Week`, `Recipes` and `List`**, Food's sections. You are on `Today`. `Week` is the week ahead: see [Planning the week](week.md). `Recipes` is your recipes: see [Your recipes](recipes.md). `List` is the shopping list made from the week: see [Your shopping list](list.md). `List` carries a number when there is something to buy: the things on the list for its days, less what you have ticked off on this phone and what you said you have at home. A staple the list is still asking you about is not counted.

### The search bar (on a computer)

- **The box**, `What did you eat? Try "banana" or "chicken rice bowl"`. At its right end it says which meal it adds to, such as `into Snacks`: the meal the time of day suggests (breakfast from 4 in the morning, lunch from 11, snacks from 3 in the afternoon, dinner from 5, and snacks again from 10 at night), or dinner on an earlier day. You can change it before you add.
- **As you type**, the results drop down under the box: `Your recipes` first, then `Foods` from USDA's list, each with its picture, its kind and its first portion, such as `Bananas · 1 banana · 122 kcal`. `Searching…` shows while it looks. `Nothing found for "…". Try fewer words, or other ones.` means nothing matched. {button:|ghost|x} clears the box, and so does Esc.
- **Choose a result** to open the sheet, below.
- **{button:|food-soft|mic}** lets you say it instead of typing. Click it, say what you ate, such as "two eggs and toast", and the words go into the box to search, for you to check. It stops on its own when you stop talking, after 30 seconds at the most, or when you click it again. The short recording goes to a speech service to be turned into words and is not kept. Where a browser cannot record, the button is not there.
- **{button:Photo of the plate|food|camera}** opens your camera on a phone, or asks for a photo on a computer. The photo goes to Log food, which reads it and asks you to check what was found. See [Logging what you ate](log.md).
- **`Again?`** offers the last four things you logged, each with the amount you had, such as `Almonds, unsalted · 28 g`.
  - **The + on a chip** adds it at once, at that amount, to the meal in the box. A message says what was added, such as `Added to snacks: Almonds, unsalted, 28 g`, with {button:Undo|outline} for 8 seconds.
  - **The chip itself** opens the sheet with that amount filled in, to change it first.

### The sheet

What you chose, and how much. On a phone it comes up from the bottom; on a computer it opens in the middle.

- **Its picture, its name and its kind**: a food's category on USDA's list, such as `Yogurt, Greek`, or `Your recipe`. **`Back`** closes it without adding anything.
- **The meal buttons**, `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`, with the one in the box chosen. Choose another to add it there.
- **How much.** The number, which you can type over, with − and + beside it: a recipe goes a serving at a time, a food half a portion at a time, or 10 at a time in grams. Next to it, what the number counts: for a food, its own portions, such as `1 egg` or `1 cup`, then `g` and `oz`; for a recipe, `servings`.
- **Its numbers for that amount**, as you change it: `kcal`, `protein`, `carbs` and `fat`.
- **{button:Add to snacks|food}**, named for the meal chosen, logs it on the day and closes the sheet. The meal below shows it at once, and a message says what was added, with {button:Undo|outline}.
- A recipe with no nutrition yet says `This recipe has no nutrition yet, so it is logged without numbers.` with `Work it out from its ingredients`, which opens [Working out a recipe's nutrition](recipe-nutrition.md). You can still add it.
- A number that is not one shows `Type how much, a number above 0.`, and a portion the food cannot be counted in shows `That amount is not one this food can be logged in.` Add stays grayed out for both.

### The day's numbers

- **The rings**, when you have targets. The outer ring is calories, in Food's orange; the inner ring is protein, in red. Each fills toward its target and stops at full. In the middle, what is left of your calories, such as `1,150 kcal left`, or how far over, such as `+123 kcal over`. With a protein target and no calorie target, the middle shows the protein left, such as `38 g protein left`.
- **On a computer**, beside the rings: `Calories` and how far along, such as `1,050 of 2,200 kcal`, and `Protein`, such as `74 g of 150 g`. Under them, **`Where today's calories came from`**: a bar in three colors for protein, carbs and fat, each as its share of the calories in them (protein and carbs count 4 kcal a gram, fat 9), and their grams, such as `74 g protein · 129 g carbs · 30 g fat`.
- **On a phone**, beside a smaller ring: `Calories`, `Protein` (such as `74 / 150 g`), `Carbs` and `Fat`.
- **With no targets**, there are no rings: the four numbers are shown large, `Calories`, `Protein`, `Carbs` and `Fat`.
- **Something with no nutrition**: a recipe with no nutrition yet is logged without numbers, and the card says so, such as `1 thing has no nutrition: its recipe has none yet. Tap it to work the recipe's nutrition out from its ingredients, and it counts here too.`
- **A line about the day**, when you have targets:
  - Before the meal up next is eaten, where it takes you, such as `Dinner gets you to 70% of calories and 75% of protein.`, or `Your snack gets you to…` for a planned snack.
  - With nothing up next, what is left to go, such as `670 kcal and 38 g protein to go. A good day.`
  - On target for both: `On target for calories and protein. A good day.` A day is on its calorie target within a tenth of it, either way, and on its protein target at or above it.
  - More than a tenth over your calories: `300 kcal over your calorie target.`
  - On an earlier day, only `On target for calories and protein.`, when it was.
- **{button:Change targets|ghost|target}**, or {button:Set targets|ghost|target} when you have none, opens two boxes in the card:
  - `Calories a day`, a whole number from 500 to 10,000.
  - `Protein a day, g`, a whole number from 10 to 500.
  - `Leave a box empty for no target. A day is on its calorie target within a tenth of it, either way, and on its protein target at or above it.`
  - {button:Save targets|food} keeps them, and {button:Cancel|food-soft} closes the boxes unchanged. A number out of range shows `Calories between 500 and 10,000.` or `Protein between 10 and 500 g.` in red, and Save targets stays grayed out.

### Up next

Today, the next meal you planned and have not eaten yet, while its time is not over: breakfast until 11, lunch until 3 in the afternoon, dinner until 10 at night, and a snack until the day ends. When nothing is up next, this card is not shown and the day's numbers take its place.

- **Its picture**: your recipe's photo, or a picture for its kind.
- **`Up next · Dinner, planned`**, then its name and what it is, such as `Cook 4 servings, eat 1 · 480 kcal · 38 g protein`.
- **{button:Ate it|food|check}** logs it as planned, in its meal, at its servings or amount. The buttons give way to `Logged to dinner. Nice work.` and {button:Undo|outline}, which takes it off the day again. The card moves on to the next meal the next time you open the page.
- **{button:Change first|food-soft}** opens Log food with it chosen and its amount filled in, when you ate more, less or something a little different. Adding it there logs it as this planned meal. See [Logging what you ate](log.md).
- **{button:Cook|food-soft|chef-hat}**, on a recipe you cook at this meal, opens cook mode at the whole batch.
- **On a phone**, {button:Ate it|food|check} is beside the name and turns into `Logged`. Change first and Cook are on the meal's planned row below.

### Meals

**`Meals`**: `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`. On a computer, a card each, side by side, with `Tap anything to change how much or move it` beside the heading. On a phone, one card with a row for each meal: its name, up to three pictures of what is in it, and its calories. A meal with nothing eaten but something planned shows it, such as `Turkey chili, planned`. Tap a meal's row to open it.

- **{button:|food-soft|plus}** beside a meal opens Log food for that meal and this day.
- **A meal with nothing in it**, logged or planned, says `Nothing yet`.
- **Each thing you logged**, in the order you logged it: its picture, its name, how much, such as `1 banana`, `150 g` or `1.5 servings`, its protein, carbs and fat, such as `1 cup · 24 g protein · 9 g carbs · 1 g fat`, and its calories on the right. A recipe shows its own photo, or a chef's hat without one; a food from USDA's list shows a picture for its kind, such as an egg or an apple. A recipe with no nutrition yet says `no nutrition` instead of calories.
- **Tap a thing** to change it. Its panel opens under it:
  - `How much`, a number above 0.
  - Beside it, what the number counts. For a food: its own portions from the food list, such as `1 banana` or `1 cup`, then `g` and `oz`. For a recipe: `servings`.
  - The meal buttons, `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`, to move it to another meal.
  - Its numbers for the new amount, as you type. They are worked out from the numbers it was logged with, so a recipe you changed since keeps counting as it was when you ate it.
  - {button:Work out its nutrition|food-soft}, on a recipe logged with no numbers, opens [Working out a recipe's nutrition](recipe-nutrition.md). Saving it there fills in this log too.
  - {button:Save|food} keeps the change. {button:Cancel|food-soft} closes it unchanged. {button:Remove|ghost} asks `Remove it?`: click {button:Remove|destructive} to take it off the day, or {button:Keep it|food-soft}.
  - A number that is not one shows `Type how much, a number above 0.`, and a portion the food cannot be counted in shows `That amount is not one this food can be logged in.` Save stays grayed out for both.
- **What you planned and have not logged yet**, under what you logged, in a dashed box marked `PLANNED`: its picture, its name, what it is, such as `Cook 4 servings, eat 1`, `Leftovers · 1 serving` or `1 cup`, and its calories. See [Planning the week](week.md).
  - {button:Ate it|food} logs it as planned, in this meal, at its servings or amount. The box goes, and it shows with what you logged.
  - Tap the box for {button:Change first|food-soft} and, on a recipe you cook at this meal, {button:Cook|food-soft|chef-hat}.
  - A batch made ahead, which you eat none of here, says `for later` and `Made for later meals: cook 4 servings, and eat it as its leftovers.`, with only {button:Cook|food-soft|chef-hat}.
  - Something you logged and then removed from the day is waiting under its meal again.

### On a phone: the bar at the bottom

- **`What did you eat?`** opens Log food for the meal the time of day suggests, with the search box ready to type in.
- **{button:|food|camera}** takes a photo of the plate. Log food opens, reads it, and asks you to check what was found.

Every change shows at once, {button:Ate it|food|check} too. If it could not be kept, it goes back as it was and a message says why. The page keeps up with the day: if you leave it open overnight, it fetches the new day the next time you look at it.

## How to log what you ate

1. On a computer, type it in the search box at the top. On a phone, tap `What did you eat?`.
2. Choose the food or your recipe from the results.
3. In the sheet, set how much, check the meal, and click {button:Add to snacks|food}. The meal shows what you added, and the day's numbers include it.

## How to add something you have again

1. On a computer, find it under `Again?`.
2. Click its +. It is added at the amount you had last time, to the meal in the box. Click {button:Undo|outline} in the message if you did not mean to.

On a phone, `What did you eat?` opens Log food, where the + beside each recent thing does the same.

## How to log a meal you planned

1. Find it in the `Up next` card, or in its meal in a `PLANNED` box.
2. If you ate it as planned, click {button:Ate it|food|check}. If not, click {button:Change first|food-soft} (on a phone, tap the planned box first), set how much you ate, and add it.

## How to log a meal you forgot

1. Click {button:|ghost|chevron-left} until the day shows, up to two weeks back.
2. Search for it at the top, or click {button:|food-soft|plus} beside the meal, and log it as usual. It goes on that day.

## How to log a plate from a photo

1. Click {button:Photo of the plate|food|camera} on a computer, or {button:|food|camera} on a phone.
2. Take the photo, or choose one.
3. Log food reads it. Check what was found and the amounts, then add it all. See [Logging what you ate](log.md).

## How to set your targets

1. Click {button:Set targets|ghost|target}.
2. Type the calories and the protein you aim for each day. Leave one empty to have no target for it.
3. Click {button:Save targets|food}. The rings appear, and Health's Progress counts the days you hit them.

## Not on this page

- **Fiber, sugar and sodium.** They are kept for every food on the list, but not shown yet.
- **Brands and barcodes.** The food list is USDA's everyday foods. Packaged foods by brand, and scanning a barcode, are not built.
- **Photos of foods.** A food from the list is drawn as a picture for its kind; only your recipes have photos.
- **Progress over weeks.** It is in Health, with your workouts, sleep and plunges.

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
