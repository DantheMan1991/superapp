# Putting a meal on the week

> Choose a day and a meal, then one of your recipes or a food. A recipe is cooked there: say how much to make and how much you eat, and put the rest on later meals as leftovers.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/week/add
> **Order:** 4

Click {button:Put on the week|primary|plus} on Food's `Week`, or {button:|ghost|plus} beside a meal there. You also get here from {button:Add to the week|outline|calendar-plus} on a recipe, which opens with that recipe chosen.

## What you see

- **{button:Week|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to the week, with nothing put on.
- **`Put on the week`**, the title, with {icon:circle-question-mark} beside it, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **The day**: `Today`, then each day after it up to next Sunday, such as `Thu, Oct 8`. It starts on the day you came from.
- **The meal buttons**: `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`. The one lit up is the meal it goes on. From {button:Put on the week|primary|plus}, it starts on the meal the time of day suggests (dinner on a later day); from a meal's {button:|ghost|plus}, on that meal.
- **The search box**, `Search your recipes or foods`. Type a few letters: your recipes whose names have every word come first, under `Your recipes`, then foods on USDA's list under `Foods`, each with its kind, such as `Bananas`. The {icon:x} in the box clears it. While it looks, it says `Searching…`; with nothing found, `Nothing found for “quinoa bake”. Try fewer words, or other ones.`
- **Before you type**: `Your recipes`, the ones you cooked most lately first, and `Foods you had lately`, with the amount you last had. Click one to choose it.
- **What you chose**, in a card: its name ({icon:chef-hat} before a recipe), and under it, for a recipe, what it makes and its nutrition, such as `Makes 6 servings · 520 kcal and 44 g protein a serving`, or `no nutrition yet`. A recipe's nutrition is its own, then what was worked out from its ingredients. For a food, its kind.
  - **For a recipe:**
    - `Cook`: how much to make, with {button:|outline|minus} and {button:|outline|plus}, or type it. It starts at what the recipe makes. Under it, the recipe's unit, such as `servings`.
    - `You eat`: how much you eat at this meal, the same way. It starts at 1. 0 means the batch is made for later meals, and the card says `Nothing is eaten at this meal: the batch is made for later ones.`
    - `The other 3, as leftovers`, when the batch has more than you eat: a button for each lunch and dinner in the four days after this meal, such as `Tomorrow's lunch` and `Fri lunch`, up to next Sunday. The next free lunches are lit up to start, then dinners. A meal that has something planned already is not lit up to start. Click a button to put a leftover there or take it off; once the batch is used up, the rest are grayed out. Under them: `Each is 1 serving. Leave some off if someone else eats them. Cook opens the recipe at 4.`
  - **For a food:** `How much`, and beside it the food's own portions, such as `1 banana` or `1 cup`, then `g` and `oz`. It starts at the food's first portion, or at what you last had.
  - **What you eat at this meal comes to**, such as `520 kcal · protein 44 g · carbs 38 g · fat 18 g`. `no numbers` means the recipe has no nutrition yet.
  - {button:Put on the week|primary} puts it on, says `Turkey chili is on the week.`, and goes back to the week. While it works it says `Putting it on…`. {button:Back|ghost} goes back to the search.

## How to put a recipe on the week

1. Choose the day and the meal you cook it at.
2. Type a few letters of the recipe, and click it.
3. Set `Cook` to the batch you make and `You eat` to your serving.
4. Check the leftovers: keep the lunches lit up, choose others, or click them off if someone else eats them.
5. Click {button:Put on the week|primary}.

## How to put a food on the week

1. Choose the day and the meal.
2. Type the food, and click it under `Foods`, or click it under `Foods you had lately`.
3. Say how much, and click {button:Put on the week|primary}.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Type how many to cook and how many you eat (0 is fine).` | `Cook` or `You eat` is not a number. |
| `You can't eat more than the batch makes.` | `You eat` is more than `Cook`. |
| `That is more leftovers than the batch makes.` | Fewer leftovers, or cook more. |
| `Type how much, a number above 0.` | The food's amount is not a number above 0. |
| `That amount is not one this food can be planned in.` | The portion is not one of this food's. Choose another, or use `g` or `oz`. |
| `You can plan this week and next.` | The day is past next Sunday, or the page was left open into another week. Reload it. |
| `Leftovers come after the meal they are cooked at. Move the cook earlier, or the leftovers later.` | A leftover is on a meal before the cook. |
| `That is more than the batch makes. Cook more, or put fewer leftovers on the week.` | What you eat and the leftovers add up to more than the batch. |
| `That recipe is not here any more.` | It was deleted, perhaps in another tab. Choose another. |
| `That food is not on the list any more. Search for it again.` | The food list changed. Search for it again. |
| `That amount is not one this food can be logged in. Choose another.` | The portion is not one of this food's. Choose another. |
| `The search did not work this time. Try again.` | The search could not reach the server. Type again. |
| `It could not be put on the week. Try again.` | Saving did not work. Nothing was put on. Try again. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Food keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Days before today, and after next Sunday.** Plan this week and next week.
- **Breakfast and snacks for leftovers.** They are not offered; move a leftover there from the week.
- **Working out a recipe's nutrition.** It is on the recipe: see [Working out a recipe's nutrition](recipe-nutrition.md).

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
