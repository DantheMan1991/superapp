# Planning the week

> The week ahead, Monday to Sunday: your recipes and foods on each day's meals, a recipe cooked once and eaten again as leftovers, and each day's calories and protein against your targets.
> **Route:** /personal/m/food/week
> **Order:** 3

Open **Food** {icon:utensils} and click `Week`. On a phone you see one day at a time: tap a day in the strip to see it. On a computer you see the whole week as a board, the days across and the meals down. To plan a meal, click {button:Put on the week|food|plus}, or the {button:|food-soft|plus} beside a meal. Tap anything planned to change how much, move it, plan its leftovers, or take it off.

You can plan this week and next week. An earlier week, up to twelve weeks back, is shown as it was, so you can repeat it.

## What you see

### The top

- **The week's name**, the title: `This week`, `Next week` or `Last week`, or only the dates for an earlier week, such as `Sep 21 to 27`. On a phone the dates are above it, such as `Food · Oct 5 to 11`. On a computer they are under it, with how many meals are planned: `Oct 5 to 11 · 10 meals planned`, or `Oct 5 to 11 · nothing planned`.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark}**, which opens this guide beside the page.
- **The week's arrows.** On a computer, a pill with {button:|ghost|chevron-left}, the week's name, and {button:|ghost|chevron-right}. On a phone, {button:|outline|chevron-left} on its own, and {button:|outline|chevron-right} too when there is a week after to go to. The left arrow goes to the week before, back to twelve weeks ago. The right arrow goes to the week after, up to next week. An arrow that cannot go further is grayed out.
- **{button:Put on the week|food|plus}**, on a computer, on this week and next week. It opens Put on the week on today's next meal, or on next Monday's dinner when you are looking at next week. On a phone the same button is in the week's numbers, below. See [Putting a meal on the week](week-add.md).
- **`Today`, `Week`, `Recipes` and `List`**, Food's sections. You are on `Week`. `List` carries how many things are still to buy on the list for its days, once you have ticked off what you got on this phone; a staple the list is asking you about is not counted.

### The week's numbers

A card at the top of the week.

- **`Calories a day, planned`** and **`Protein a day, planned`**: the average of the days that have something planned, so an empty day does not pull it down. A day counts what you eat at each meal: the servings you eat of a recipe, or a food's amount.
  - With **your targets** set on Today, each has a bar and how far along it is, such as `1,940 of 2,200 kcal`. The bar stops at full when the plan goes over.
  - With nothing planned, the number shows `–` and there is no bar.
- **Under them**, `The average of the 5 days with something planned.`, or `Nothing is planned this week yet.` With no targets set it adds `Set your targets on Today to see the week against them.`
- **A bar for each day**, Monday to Sunday, under `Each day against 2,200 kcal`, or `Each day's calories, planned` with no calorie target. A bar is full at your target. Today's bar is orange, the days ahead light orange, and the days gone by pale. The first letter of each day is under its bar.
- **On this week and next week**, three buttons:
  - **{button:Put on the week|food|plus}**, on a phone, across the card. It does the same as the one at the top on a computer.
  - **{button:Shopping list|food-soft|shopping-cart}** opens the list made from the week, with how many things are still to buy beside it, the same number as on `List`. See [Your shopping list](list.md).
  - **{button:Repeat a week|food-soft|copy}** opens the weeks you can repeat, under `Each meal goes on the same day of the week shown. Days already gone are left out, and it adds to what is planned.` Each earlier week with something planned is a row, such as `Last week, Sep 28 to Oct 4` and `9 meals`. Click one to copy it onto the week you are looking at. With none, it says `No earlier week has anything planned.` {button:Cancel|ghost} closes it.
- **On a week gone by**, instead of the buttons: `A week gone by is shown as it was. Repeat it from this week or the next.`

### On a phone: one day at a time

- **The days**, a strip of seven buttons, Monday first. Each has the day's short name and its date, such as `Wed` and `7`, and a dot for each thing planned that day, up to four. Today's name is orange. The day you are looking at is filled orange. The days gone by are paler. The week opens on today; any other week opens on its Monday. Tap a day to see it.
- **The day's card.** Its name, such as `Wed, Oct 7`, with `Today` beside today's, and on the right what the day comes to, such as `1,800 kcal · 164 g protein`.
  - **Its meals**: `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`, each with its picture. On today and the days after it, {button:|food-soft|plus} beside a meal opens Put on the week for that day and meal. A meal with nothing in it says `Nothing planned`. A day gone by with nothing planned is a single line, `Nothing planned`.
  - **Each planned meal**: its picture (the recipe's photo, a chef's hat for a recipe with no photo, or a picture of the kind of food), its name, and a line saying what it is:
    - A recipe cooked there: `Cook 4 servings, eat 1`. When you eat none of it there (a batch made ahead), `Cook 4 servings, eat none here`. Its leftovers follow, such as `3 more: Tue lunch, Wed lunch, Thu dinner`.
    - Leftovers: `Leftovers · 1 serving`, and where it was cooked, such as `From Mon dinner` or `From today's dinner`.
    - A food: its amount, such as `1 banana` or `150 g`.
    - On the right, the calories you eat there. `no numbers` means the recipe has no nutrition yet: tap it and click {button:Work out its nutrition|food-soft}. `for later` means nothing is eaten at that meal: the batch is made for its leftovers. {icon:check} `Eaten` means you logged it, from Today's {button:Ate it|food|check} or from Log food.
  - **Under the meals**, when some recipes have no nutrition yet: `1 meal has no numbers: its recipe has none yet. Tap it and choose Work out its nutrition.`, or `2 meals have no numbers: their recipes have none yet. Tap one and choose Work out its nutrition.`

### On a computer: the board

- **The days across**, Monday to Sunday, each with its short name and date. Today's column is shaded, with `Wed · Today`. The days gone by are paler.
- **The meals down**: `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`, each with its picture.
- **Each planned meal** is a small card in its day and meal: its picture, the calories you eat there at the top right (or `no numbers`, `for later` or {icon:check} `Eaten`, as on a phone), its name, and what it is: `Cook 4, eat 1`, `Leftovers`, or a food's amount.
- **A dashed {button:|food-soft|plus}** under each meal on today and the days after it opens Put on the week for that day and meal.
- **Under each day**, what it comes to, such as `1,800 kcal` and `164 g protein`, or `Nothing planned`.
- **Under the board**, when some recipes have no nutrition yet: `1 meal has no numbers: its recipe has none yet. Click it and choose Work out its nutrition.`

### A planned meal, opened

Tap a planned meal on this week or next week to open it. On a phone it comes up from the bottom; on a computer it opens in the middle. A meal on an earlier week cannot be opened.

- **At the top**, its picture, its name, and where it is and what, such as `Today's dinner · Cook 4 servings, eat 1 · 1 more: Fri dinner`. {button:Close|ghost} closes it.
- **How much.** Each number has {button:|ghost|minus} and {button:|ghost|plus} either side, and you can type over it.
  - For a recipe cooked there, `Cook` and `You eat`, then the recipe's own unit, such as `servings`. `You eat` can go down to 0.
  - For leftovers, `Servings`.
  - For a food, `How much` and the portion beside it: the food's own portions, then `g` and `oz`.
  - {button:Save|food} shows once you change something.
- **`Move to`**: the day (today to next Sunday), and the meal buttons, `Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner` and `Snacks`. {button:Move|food-soft} shows once you choose another place.
- **`Plan leftovers`**, on a recipe cooked there while its batch has some left, such as `Plan leftovers: 2 more of 1 serving`. A button for each lunch and dinner in the four days after it (today on, up to next Sunday), without the ones it already has. Choose up to as many as it has left, then click {button:Put 2 on the week|food-soft}.
- **At the bottom**:
  - {button:Cook|food|chef-hat}, on a recipe cooked there, opens cook mode at what you cook.
  - {button:Recipe|food-soft} opens the recipe.
  - {button:Work out its nutrition|food-soft}, on a recipe with no numbers, opens [Working out a recipe's nutrition](recipe-nutrition.md).
  - {button:Take off|ghost} asks `Take it off?`, or for a recipe with leftovers `Take it off? Its 3 leftovers go too.` Click {button:Take off|destructive} to take it off the week, or {button:Keep it|food-soft}. What you already logged as eaten stays in your log.

Every change shows at once. If it could not be kept, it goes back as it was and a message says why.

## How to plan your week

1. Click {button:Put on the week|food|plus}, or the {button:|food-soft|plus} beside the meal.
2. Choose the day and the meal, then one of your recipes or a food. For a recipe, say how much to cook and how much you eat, and choose which later meals get the leftovers. See [Putting a meal on the week](week-add.md).
3. Back here, the meal shows on its day with its leftovers on theirs, and the week's numbers include them.

## How to cook once and eat it again

1. Put the recipe on the meal where you cook it. Set `Cook` to the whole batch and `You eat` to your serving.
2. Leave the leftovers on the lunches it offers, or choose others. Leave some off if someone else eats them.
3. On the day, tap the cook and click {button:Cook|food|chef-hat}: cook mode opens at the whole batch. On Today, each leftover waits in its meal with {button:Ate it|food|check}.

To add leftovers later, tap the recipe on the day you cook it and use `Plan leftovers`.

## How to make a batch ahead

1. Put the recipe on the meal where you cook it, and set `You eat` to 0.
2. Choose the meals that get it. The cook says `for later`, and each of those meals has its leftovers.

## How to repeat a week

1. Go to this week or next week, and click {button:Repeat a week|food-soft|copy}.
2. Click the week to copy. Each of its meals goes on the same day of the week you are on, with its leftovers. On this week, the days already gone are left out. It adds to what is planned.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing is planned this week yet.` | The week is empty. Click {button:Put on the week|food|plus}, or repeat a week. |
| `No earlier week has anything planned.` | There is no week to repeat yet. |
| `Put 9 meals on the week.` | The week you chose was copied. |
| `Nothing was put on: that week's days have already gone by here.` | Every meal of that week falls on a day already gone this week. Repeat it onto next week instead. |
| `Type how many, a number (0 is fine for what you eat).` | `Cook` or `You eat` is not a number. |
| `You can't eat more than the batch makes.` | `You eat` is more than `Cook`. |
| `Its leftovers take 3: cook more, or take some off.` | The batch would not cover the leftovers already on the week. |
| `Type how many servings, a number above 0.` | A leftover's servings are not a number above 0. |
| `Type how much, a number above 0.` | A food's amount is not a number above 0. |
| `That amount is not one this food can be planned in.` | The portion is not one of this food's. Choose another, or use `g` or `oz`. |
| `Its leftovers would come before it is cooked. Move them first.` | A recipe cannot move after its own leftovers. Move the leftovers later first. |
| `Leftovers come after the meal they are cooked at.` | Leftovers cannot move to before their cook. |
| `You can plan this week and next.` | The day is before this week's Monday or after next Sunday, or a page left open has fallen behind. Reload the page. |
| `That meal is not on the week any more. Reload the page.` | It was taken off, perhaps in another tab. |
| `Leftovers come after the meal they are cooked at. Move the cook earlier, or the leftovers later.` | The change would put leftovers before their cook. |
| `That is more than the batch makes. Cook more, or put fewer leftovers on the week.` | The leftovers and what you eat would add up to more than the batch. |
| `Choose a past week to repeat, on this week or the next.` | Repeat a week can copy an earlier week onto this week or next week only. Reload the page and try again. |
| `That amount is not one this food can be logged in. Choose another.` | The portion is not one of this food's any more. Choose another. |
| `It could not be moved. Try again.` | Moving it did not work. It went back. Try again. |
| `It could not be changed. Try again.` | Saving the change did not work. It went back as it was. Try again. |
| `The leftovers could not be put on the week. Try again.` | Adding the leftovers did not work. Try again. |
| `It could not be taken off the week. Try again.` | Taking it off did not work. It is back on the week. Try again. |
| `The week could not be repeated. Try again.` | Copying the week did not work. Nothing was put on. Try again. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Food keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Dragging a meal to another day.** Use `Move to`.
- **Planning further ahead than next week.**
- **The whole week at once on a phone.** A phone shows one day at a time; a computer shows the board.

## Who can do what

Only you. Food is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
