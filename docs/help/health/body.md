# Body

> Your weigh-ins and the trend through them, your goal weight and when you get there, your progress photos and your tape measures.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/body
> **Order:** 10

On Health, click {button:Body|ghost|chevron-right} on the `Weight` card. This page shows your weight over time as a trend, your goal, your tape measures, and every weigh-in, which you can change or remove here. You weigh in on Health itself, today or on a day in the last two weeks.

## What you see

- **{button:Health|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Health.
- **`Body`** {icon:weight}, the title, with `Your weigh-ins, the trend through them, your goal and your tape measures.`
- **Three numbers** across the top:
  - **`Trend`**: your trend as of today, such as `185.7 lb`, or `None yet` before your first weigh-in. See [The trend](#the-trend).
  - **`This week`**: how the trend moved in the last seven days, such as `down 0.5 lb`. It is in Health's color when that is the way to your goal. It says `Not yet` until you have a weigh-in from a week or more ago.
  - **`Goal`**: with a goal set, its weight, such as `Goal 175 lb`, and when you get there at your recent pace, such as `Around Nov 24`. It says `No date yet` while there is no pace, or the pace is not heading there, and `You're there` once your trend is within half a pound of it. Without a goal, `Not set`.
- **The chart.**
  - **{button:30 days|primary}, {button:90 days|ghost} and {button:All|ghost}**: how far back it shows. The one chosen is filled in. It opens on 30 days.
  - **Each weigh-in is a dot**, and **the trend is a line** through them. **Your goal is a dashed line** marked `goal`, when it is close enough to draw without flattening the rest; further off, it is only in the numbers. The chart starts at your first weigh-in in the days chosen, dated at the bottom left, and ends at `Today`. The numbers down the left are pounds.
  - Before your first weigh-in it says `No weigh-ins yet. Weigh in on Today to start the line.`, and when the days chosen have none, `No weigh-ins in these days. Try All.`
  - Under it, the key: `a weigh-in`, `the trend`, and `your goal` when the goal is drawn.
- **`Goal`** {icon:target}.
  - **Without a goal:** `A goal weight and about how fast. Body shows when you get there at your real pace, and Progress shows which way is better for you.`, and {button:Set a goal|outline|target}, which opens the form.
  - **With a goal:** what it is, such as `Lose to 175 lb, about 1 lb a week`, and a line on where you stand, such as `10.7 lb to go. At your recent pace (0.9 lb a week), 175 lb around Dec 20.` See [What the goal says](#what-the-goal-says). {button:Change|ghost|pencil} opens the form with the goal in it. {button:Remove|ghost} asks `Remove the goal? Your weigh-ins stay.`, with {button:Remove|destructive} to remove it and {button:Keep it|ghost} to leave it.
  - **The form:** `Goal weight`, in pounds, from 50 to 700; anything else shows `Type a weight between 50 and 700 lb.` in red. `About how fast`: `0.25 lb a week`, `0.5`, `0.75`, `1`, `1.5` or `2 lb a week`; it starts at 1 lb a week. Under them, `Losing or gaining is worked out from where the goal is against your trend.` {button:Save|primary} keeps it (it reads `Saving…` while it works, and stays grayed out until the weight is right). {button:Cancel|outline} closes the form unchanged.
- **`Progress photos`** {icon:images}.
  - **When the last were taken on this phone** and which poses, such as `Last taken Oct 3: front, side, back`, and how many days of photos this phone holds, such as `6 days of photos on this phone`. Before any, `Front, side and back, taken on a timer with the phone propped up, and compared over the weeks.`
  - **{button:Take photos|primary|camera}** opens [Taking progress photos](photos-take.md). **{button:Compare|outline|images}**, once there are photos, opens [Progress photos](photos.md).
  - Under them: `On this phone only, never uploaded. A new phone starts with none.` {icon:lock} The card reads which days this phone holds, never a photo; on another phone or a laptop it shows none.
- **`Tape measures`** {icon:ruler}.
  - **{button:Measure|outline}** opens [Measure](measure.md) for today. **{button:Change|ghost|settings-2}** opens [Your tape measures](measures.md), where you choose them. Before you have any, only {button:Choose|ghost|settings-2} shows, and the card says `Choose what you measure, such as your waist, and take them whenever you like. Once a week is plenty.`
  - **Each tape measure**, by name, with its latest and the day, such as `36.25 in, Oct 3`. Under it, how it has moved since the first time you took it, such as `down 0.5 in since Sep 26`, in Health's color when that is its better way. Before you take one, `Not taken yet`.
  - **`Days measured. Open one to change or take off what was kept.`**, with your eight most recent days, such as `Saturday, Sep 26`. Each opens Measure on that day. With more, `and 3 before`.
- **`Weigh-ins`**: every weigh-in, newest first: `Today`, `Yesterday`, then the day, such as `Tuesday, Sep 29`, with the year when it was not this year. The first 14 show; {button:Show all 20|ghost} lists them all, and {button:Show fewer|ghost} goes back to 14.
  - **{button:|ghost|pencil}** beside one opens it: a box with its weight, {button:Save|primary} to keep a new number, {button:Cancel|outline} to close it, and {button:Remove|ghost}, which asks `Remove this weigh-in?` with {button:Remove|destructive} and {button:Keep it|ghost}. Any weigh-in can be changed or removed here, however long ago.
  - Before your first: `None yet. Your weigh-ins from Today are listed here, newest first.`

What you change here shows at once: the numbers at the top and the chart follow.

## The trend

From one morning to the next, a scale can move a pound or two with water and food alone, which hides what your weight is doing. The trend follows your weigh-ins slowly, so those swings mostly cancel out:

- Your first weigh-in starts it.
- Each weigh-in a day after the last moves the trend a tenth of the way towards it. After a gap it moves further: about half the way after a week. Weighing in every day, or once a week, both work.
- A run of weigh-ins one way moves it; one heavy morning hardly does.
- It runs a little behind a steady change. Losing a pound a week, the trend sits about a pound above your latest weigh-ins.

## What the goal says

Losing or gaining is whichever way the goal is from your trend. Before your first weigh-in, it says `Reach 175 lb, about 1 lb a week`.

| What it says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Weigh in to see how far you have to go.` | No weigh-ins yet, so there is no trend to measure from. |
| `10.7 lb to go. Two weeks of weigh-ins give a pace and a date.` | Your pace needs at least four weigh-ins, ten days or more apart first to last, in the last four weeks. |
| `10.7 lb to go. At your recent pace (0.9 lb a week), 175 lb around Dec 20.` | Your recent pace is the straight line closest to your weigh-ins of the last four weeks. The date is when your trend gets to the goal at that pace. |
| `Your recent pace is about level, so there is no date yet.` | Under a tenth of a pound a week either way. |
| `Your recent pace is up 0.3 lb a week, away from 175 lb.` | Your weight is moving the other way from your goal. |
| `At your recent pace (0.1 lb a week), more than three years off.` | Heading there, slowly. No date is given that far off. |
| `Your goal: 175 lb` and `Your trend is within half a pound of it.` | You are there. |

## How to set a goal

1. Under `Goal`, click {button:Set a goal|outline|target}.
2. Type the weight you are working towards in `Goal weight`.
3. Choose `About how fast`.
4. Click {button:Save|primary}. The card says the goal and where you stand, and Progress colors your weight's row by it.

## How to fix a weigh-in

1. Under `Weigh-ins`, click {button:|ghost|pencil} beside it.
2. Type the right number and click {button:Save|primary}. Or click {button:Remove|ghost}, then {button:Remove|destructive}.

The trend, the chart and the numbers at the top change with it.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Type a weight between 50 and 700 lb.` | The weight is not one Health keeps. Check the number. |
| `The weigh-in could not be changed. Try again.` | Saving did not work. Nothing changed. Click {button:Save|primary} again. |
| `It could not be removed. Try again.` | Removing the weigh-in did not work. It is still there. |
| `That is not here any more. Reload the page.` | The weigh-in was removed, perhaps in another tab. Reload to see your weigh-ins as they are. |
| `The goal could not be kept. Try again.` | Saving the goal did not work. Nothing changed. |
| `The goal could not be removed. Try again.` | Removing the goal did not work. It is still there. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Health keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Weighing in.** You weigh in on Health's `Weight` card, today or on a day in the last two weeks.
- **Kilograms and centimeters.** Pounds and inches only. Ask us if you want metric.
- **A smart scale.** Not built: you type the number in.
- **Body fat.** Not kept.
- **What your eating and your trend say you burn a day.** Coming once you have a few weeks of weigh-ins.

## Who can do what

Only you. Health is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
