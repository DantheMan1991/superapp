# Your habits

> The things you want to keep doing, such as a sauna, stretching or a supplement: add them here, and mark them done on Health each day.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/habits
> **Order:** 40

On Health, click {button:Change|ghost|settings-2} beside `Your habits`, or {button:Add|ghost|settings-2} before you have any. Add a habit with its name, and a unit when you want to say how much, such as minutes in the sauna. Each habit becomes a button on Health, to tap when you have done it.

## What you see

- **{button:Health|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Health.
- **`Your habits`**, the title, with `Add your own to mark them done on Today: a sauna, stretching, a supplement.`
- **`Add a habit`**, a card:
  - **`Name`.** What you call it, up to 60 characters, such as `Sauna` or `Magnesium`. Each name can be used once: `sauna` and `Sauna` are the same name.
  - **`Counted in`.** Optional, up to 20 characters. Leave it empty for a habit you either did or did not, such as stretching. Give it a unit, such as `min` or `g`, to type how much each day you mark it. The unit is shown after the amount, so `min` reads `Sauna · 20 min`.
  - **{button:Add|primary|plus}.** Adds the habit to the end of the list, and to Health. It stays grayed out until there is a name.
  - Under them: `Leave “Counted in” empty for a habit that is done or not. Give it a unit, such as min or g, to say how much each day.` With 30 habits, the most Health keeps, it says `You have 30, the most Health keeps. Delete one to add another.`, and {button:Add|primary|plus} stays grayed out.
- **Your habits**, in the order you added them, the same order as on Health. Each shows its name and how it is marked: `· counted in min`, or `· done or not`.
  - **{button:Change|ghost|pencil}.** Opens the habit's `Name` and `Counted in` in its row. Change them, then click {button:Save|primary}, or {button:Cancel|ghost} to leave it as it was.
  - **{button:|ghost|trash}.** Deletes the habit. It asks `Delete it and its days?`. Click {button:Delete|destructive} to delete the habit and every day you marked it, or {button:Keep it|ghost} to keep it. A deleted habit leaves Health and Progress.
- **With no habits**, the page says `No habits yet. Add one above, and it appears on Today to mark each day.`

## How to add a habit

1. Type its name in `Name`, such as `Sauna`.
2. To say how much each day, type a unit in `Counted in`, such as `min`. Otherwise leave it empty.
3. Click {button:Add|primary|plus}. The habit appears in the list, and as a button under `Your habits` on Health.

## How to change or delete a habit

1. Click {button:Change|ghost|pencil} in its row, change `Name` or `Counted in`, and click {button:Save|primary}.
2. To delete it, click {button:|ghost|trash}, then {button:Delete|destructive}.

Renaming a habit keeps the days you marked. Changing its unit keeps them too: a habit that gains a unit shows only the amounts you type from then on, so the days marked before count as nothing in its totals on Progress. A habit that loses its unit counts its days instead.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `You already have a habit with that name.` | Another habit has that name, whatever its capitals. Choose a different name. |
| `That is the most habits Health keeps. Delete one first.` | You have 30 habits. Delete one you no longer keep to add another. |
| `That is not here any more. Reload the page.` | The habit was deleted, perhaps in another tab. Reload to see your habits as they are. |
| `The habit could not be added. Try again.` | Something went wrong. Nothing was added. Click {button:Add|primary|plus} again. |
| `The habit could not be changed. Try again.` | Something went wrong. Nothing changed. Click {button:Save|primary} again. |
| `The habit could not be deleted. Try again.` | Something went wrong. The habit is still there. Try again in a moment. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | The name or the unit is not one Health keeps, such as a name of spaces only. Check them and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Reordering habits.** They stay in the order you added them. Ask us if you want to move them.
- **A goal for a habit**, such as three saunas a week. Not built. Progress shows how often you did each one.
- **Habits that repeat on some days only.** Every habit shows on Health every day.

## Who can do what

Only you. Health is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
