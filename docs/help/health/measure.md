# Measure

> A day's tape measures, typed in together: your waist and the others you chose.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/body/measure
> **Order:** 12

On Body, click {button:Measure|outline} under `Tape measures`, or **Measure** on Health's `Weight` card. It opens on today, or on the day you came from: an earlier day on Health, or a day under `Days measured` on Body.

## What you see

- **{button:Body|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Body, nothing saved.
- **`Measure`** {icon:ruler}, the title, with the day under it: `Today`, `Yesterday`, or the date, such as `Saturday, Sep 26`.
- **A box for each of your tape measures**, by its name, such as `Waist`, with `in` beside it. Each already holds what you kept that day, if anything. Type the inches as a decimal, such as `36.25`, or as the tape reads, such as `36 1/4`. From 1 to 150 inches. Anything else shows `Inches between 1 and 150, such as 36.25 or 36 1/4.` in red under the box.
- **Under each box**, the last time you took that one before this day, such as `Last: 36.5 in, Sep 28`.
- **`Leave a box empty to skip that one. Emptying one kept that day takes it off.`**
- **{button:Save|primary}**, across the bottom. It keeps every box with a number in it and takes off any you emptied that had one. It reads `Saving…` while it works; then you see `Tape measures kept.` and land on Body. It stays grayed out while a box is wrong, and when every box is empty and nothing was kept that day.
- **Before you choose any tape measures:** `Choose what you measure first, such as your waist.` and {button:Choose your tape measures|outline|list-plus}, which opens [Your tape measures](measures.md).

## How to take your measures

1. Open Measure.
2. Measure each one with a soft tape, at the same place each time.
3. Type each in its box. Skip any you did not take.
4. Click {button:Save|primary}. Body shows the new numbers, and how far each has moved since the first time.

To change a day's measures later, open it from `Days measured` on Body, change a box, and click {button:Save|primary}. To take one off, empty its box and save.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Inches between 1 and 150, such as 36.25 or 36 1/4.` | The box holds something that is not a measure Health keeps. Fix it or empty it. |
| `Tape measures kept.` | Saved. You are back on Body. |
| `You can fill in today and the two weeks before it.` | The day is more than two weeks back and nothing was kept on it, so there is nothing to change there. |
| `It is a new day. Reload the page, then try again.` | The page was opened as today, and the day has moved on since. Reload it. |
| `That is not here any more. Reload the page.` | One of the tape measures on the page was deleted, perhaps in another tab. Nothing was kept. Reload the page. |
| `The tape measures could not be kept. Try again.` | Saving did not work. Nothing changed. Click {button:Save|primary} again. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Health keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Choosing what you measure.** On [Your tape measures](measures.md).
- **Centimeters.** Inches only. Ask us if you want metric.
- **A day more than two weeks back with nothing kept on it.** Measure opens on today instead.

## Who can do what

Only you. Health is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
