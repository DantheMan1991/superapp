# Your tape measures

> Choose what you measure, such as your waist, and which way is better for each.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/body/measures
> **Order:** 14

On Body, click {button:Change|ghost|settings-2} under `Tape measures` ({button:Choose|ghost|settings-2} before you have any), or **Choose them** on Health's `Weight` card. Measure asks for each one you add here.

## What you see

- **{button:Body|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Body.
- **`Your tape measures`** {icon:ruler}, the title, with `Choose what you measure, and which way is better for each.`
- **`Add a tape measure`.**
  - **The common ones you have not added**: {button:Waist|outline|plus}, {button:Chest|outline|plus}, {button:Hips|outline|plus}, {button:Arm|outline|plus}, {button:Thigh|outline|plus}, {button:Neck|outline|plus} and {button:Calf|outline|plus}. Click one to add it. `Waist` starts as smaller is better; the rest as neither way.
  - **`Another, by name`**, for any other, such as `Left arm` or `Shoulders`, up to 40 characters, with **`Which way`**: `Smaller is better`, `Bigger is better` or `Neither way is better`. **{button:Add|primary|plus}** adds it; it stays grayed out until there is a name.
  - Under them, `Which way is better colors the measure on Body and in Progress. The waist starts as smaller is better; the rest are your call.` With 12 tape measures, the most Health keeps, it says `You have 12, the most Health keeps. Delete one to add another.`, and the common ones are hidden.
- **Your tape measures**, in the order you added them, each with which way is better, such as `Waist · smaller is better`.
  - **{button:Change|ghost|pencil}** opens it: `Name` and `Which way`, with {button:Save|primary} and {button:Cancel|ghost}.
  - **{button:|ghost|trash}** asks `Delete it and its days?`. {button:Delete|destructive} deletes it and every day it was taken; {button:Keep it|ghost} leaves it.
  - Before you add any: `No tape measures yet. Add one above, and Measure asks for it.`

## Which way is better

It decides one thing: the color. On Body, a tape measure's `since` line is in Health's color when it moved the better way, and on Progress, so is its row's change.

- **Smaller is better**: a waist, while you are losing.
- **Bigger is better**: an arm or a thigh, while you are building.
- **Neither way is better**: no color either way.

Change it any time with {button:Change|ghost|pencil}. What you kept stays as it was.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `You already have a tape measure with that name.` | Names are one each, whatever their capitals. Choose another name. |
| `That is the most tape measures Health keeps. Delete one first.` | You have 12. Delete one to add another. |
| `The tape measure could not be added. Try again.` | Adding did not work. Nothing changed. |
| `The tape measure could not be changed. Try again.` | Saving the change did not work. Nothing changed. |
| `The tape measure could not be deleted. Try again.` | Deleting did not work. It is still there. |
| `That is not here any more. Reload the page.` | It was deleted, perhaps in another tab. Reload to see your list as it is. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | What was sent was not something Health keeps. Reload the page and try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Taking your measures.** On [Measure](measure.md).
- **Moving one up or down the list.** They stay in the order you added them.
- **Centimeters.** Inches only. Ask us if you want metric.

## Who can do what

Only you. Health is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
