# Taking progress photos

> Front, side and back, taken in a row with the phone propped up: ten seconds to stand, and a voice that says when to turn. Kept on this phone only.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/photos/take
> **Order:** 16

On Body, click {button:Take photos|primary|camera} on the `Progress photos` card, or **Take** on Health's `Weight` card when it says your photos are four weeks old. Prop the phone up so your whole body fits, start, and step into place. The phone takes the front, side and back, then shows them to you to keep.

## What you see

- **{button:Photos|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to [Progress photos](photos.md), nothing kept.
- **`Take photos`** {icon:camera}, the title, with `Kept on this phone only. Never uploaded, never in your gallery.` {icon:lock}
- **Before the camera opens:** what to do, `Prop the phone up at about waist height, 2 to 3 metres away, so your whole body fits. Then Start, and step into place.`, and {button:Open the camera|primary|camera}. The first time, the phone asks to allow the camera.
- **The camera's picture**, from the main back lens (never the wide one, which bends a body at the edges). In its top corner, the pose being taken: `Front · 1 of 3`, `Side · 2 of 3` or `Back · 3 of 3`, or the pose alone when one is taken again.
- **The outline of last time's photo**: your last photo of the same pose, faint over the picture, so you can stand where you stood. The box `Show the outline of last time's photo` turns it off and on. There is none before your first photos.
- **{button:Start|primary}**, big, under the picture. Today's photos already kept show `Today's photos are kept already. New ones replace them.` above it.
- **While it counts:** the seconds left, big over the picture, and {button:Stop|outline}, which stops and throws away what was taken so far.
- **When all three are taken:** each one small, `Front`, `Side` and `Back`, each with {button:Take again|ghost|rotate-ccw}; then {button:Keep|primary} and {button:Discard|outline}.

## What happens when you press Start

1. The voice says `Ten seconds. Step back and face the phone.`, and the count starts at 10.
2. A beep at 3, 2 and 1, and a click as the photo is taken.
3. `Turn a quarter to your right.`: five seconds, the beeps, the click. Your left side faces the phone, the same every time, so two sides compare.
4. `Turn again, so your back is to the phone.`: five seconds, the beeps, the click.
5. `That's all three. Come and have a look.` The three are on the screen to check.

The screen stays on while the camera is open. The voice is the one you chose for the workout coach; on a phone where it is not set up, the phone's own voice says it. Inside the app, the screen cannot be screenshotted while the camera or the photos are on it.

## How to take them

1. Prop the phone up, upright, at about waist height, 2 to 3 metres away. A shelf, a tripod or a chair works.
2. Click {button:Open the camera|primary|camera} and check your whole body will fit where you will stand.
3. Click {button:Start|primary} and step into place, facing the phone.
4. Turn when the voice says, and hold still at each click.
5. Look at the three. Click {button:Take again|ghost|rotate-ccw} under any you want again: ten seconds, then that one alone (`Got it.`).
6. Click {button:Keep|primary}. You see `3 photos kept on this phone.` and land on [Progress photos](photos.md).

The same spot, the same light and the same time of day (morning, before eating, as you weigh in) make two photos easiest to compare.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `3 photos kept on this phone.` | They are in this phone's own storage for Yosher, under your personal space. |
| `The camera was not allowed. Allow it for this site in Chrome's settings, then try again.` | Chrome was told no. Allow the camera for yosherapp.com in Chrome's site settings, then click {button:Try again|outline}. |
| `The camera was not allowed. Allow it for Yosher in your phone's settings, under Apps, Yosher, Permissions, then try again.` | The same, inside the app. |
| `This version of the app cannot use the camera. Update the app, or open yosherapp.com in Chrome.` | An app from before version 1.0.8 has no camera. Update it. |
| `This phone's storage would not keep them. Free some space on the phone, then try again.` | The phone is full, or refused. Nothing was kept; the photos are still on the screen. |
| `Discard these photos? They are not kept anywhere.` | {button:Discard|destructive} throws them away. {button:Keep them|outline} goes back to them. |

## Not on this page

- **The front camera.** Photos are taken with the back camera, propped up, on a timer.
- **A photo from the gallery.** Only photos taken here; nothing is read from the phone's photos.
- **Taking them for an earlier day.** Photos are kept under today.

## Who can do what

Only you, and only on this phone. The photos never leave it: they are not sent to Yosher, and nobody at Yosher can see them. Your personal space is yours alone.
