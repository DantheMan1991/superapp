# Progress photos

> Two days of photos side by side, front, side or back, with your weight trend on each. Kept on this phone only, never uploaded.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/photos
> **Order:** 15

On Body, click {button:Compare|outline|images} on the `Progress photos` card. You see one pose at a time, your first photos beside your latest, and how much your weight trend moved between them. Step either day through the days you have. Below, every day on this phone, each deleted with a second tap.

The photos are only on this phone, in its own storage for Yosher. Nothing about them is on Yosher's servers: another phone, or a laptop, shows none.

## What you see

- **{button:Body|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Body.
- **`Progress photos`** {icon:images}, the title, with `On this phone only. Never uploaded.` {icon:lock}
- **{button:Take photos|primary|camera}.** Opens [Taking progress photos](photos-take.md).
- **Before any photos:** `No photos on this phone yet.` and `Take your first front, side and back today. In a few weeks, take them again, and compare them here.`
- **{button:Front|primary}, {button:Side|ghost} and {button:Back|ghost}**: the pose. The one shown is filled in. Changing it starts again on that pose's first and latest days.
- **Two photos side by side**, the earlier on the left. Each has:
  - its day, such as `Sep 5`, with the year when it was not this year;
  - your weight trend that day, such as `Trend 186.0 lb`, or `No weigh-in yet` when it was before your first weigh-in (see [Body](body.md#the-trend));
  - {button:|ghost|chevron-left} and {button:|ghost|chevron-right} to step that photo one day earlier or later, through the days with that pose. The two never land on the same day, and each stops at its end;
  - {button:Save a copy|outline|download}. See [Saving a copy](#saving-a-copy).
- **Under them**, how far the trend moved from the earlier to the later, such as `down 3.6 lb between them, by your trend`, when both days have a trend.
- **With only one day of that pose**, that photo alone, and `Take them again in a few weeks, and compare the two here.`
- **`On this phone`**: every day with photos, newest first, and its poses, such as `Saturday, Oct 3 · front, side, back`. {button:|ghost|trash} beside a day asks `Delete this day's photos from this phone?`, with {button:Delete|destructive} and {button:Keep them|ghost}. Under the list: `A new phone, or clearing this site's data in the browser, starts with none. Save a copy of any you want to keep elsewhere.`

## Saving a copy

{button:Save a copy|outline|download} puts that one photo in your phone's own files, named by pose and day, such as `yosher-front-2026-10-03.jpg`:

- **In Chrome**, it downloads, into Downloads. You see `Saved to your Downloads.`
- **In the app (version 1.0.9 or later)**, it goes into Documents, in a `Yosher` folder, which the phone's Files app shows. You see `Saved to Documents, in the Yosher folder.`

A copy is an ordinary photo file: once it is in your files, your phone may back it up wherever it backs up files. That is why it only happens when you tap.

## How to compare

1. Choose the pose.
2. Read the trend under each photo and the line under both.
3. Step the left photo with {button:|ghost|chevron-right} to see a later start, or the right one with {button:|ghost|chevron-left} for an earlier end.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Reading this phone's photos…` | The phone is finding them. A moment. |
| `This phone's storage could not be read. Reload the page to try again.` | The browser would not open Yosher's storage. Reload the page. |
| `Saved to your Downloads.` | The copy is in Chrome's downloads. |
| `Saved to Documents, in the Yosher folder.` | The copy is in the phone's Documents, inside the app. |
| `This version of the app cannot save a copy. Update the app, or open yosherapp.com in Chrome.` | An app from before version 1.0.9 cannot save files. Update the app. |
| `The copy could not be saved. Try again.` | Saving did not work. Nothing was saved. |
| `That photo is not on this phone any more.` | It was deleted, perhaps in another tab. |
| `The photos of Oct 3 are deleted from this phone.` | Deleted. They cannot be brought back. |
| `They could not be deleted. Try again.` | Nothing was deleted. |

## Not on this page

- **Photos on another phone or a laptop.** Each phone has only the photos taken on it.
- **A backup.** Nothing is backed up by Yosher. Save a copy of the photos you want to keep.
- **Photos in Progress.** Progress shows numbers; the photos are here.

## Who can do what

Only you, and only on this phone. The photos never leave it unless you save a copy: they are not sent to Yosher, and nobody at Yosher can see them. Inside the app, the screen cannot be screenshotted while the photos are on it.
