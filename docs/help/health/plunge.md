# The cold plunge timer

> Time a cold plunge on your phone: set the water's temperature, tap Start as you get in, and Done as you get out. A soft tone sounds each minute, so you can keep your eyes shut. Then say how you feel and save it.
> **Route:** /personal/m/health/plunge
> **Order:** 20

On Health, click {button:Start the timer|primary|play} under `Cold plunge`. The timer is kept on this phone: lock the phone, open another app or reload the page, and it is still counting from when you started. To log a plunge you timed some other way, click {button:Type one in|outline} instead. For a plunge on an earlier day, step back to that day on Health and click {button:Type one in|outline} there.

## What you see

### Before you start

- **{button:Health|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Health.
- **`Cold plunge`** {icon:snowflake}, with `Set the water's temperature, then tap Start as you get in.`
- **`Water temperature, °F`.** Optional. It starts at your last plunge's temperature, so a tub you keep at the same temperature needs nothing. Type a number from 28 to 110, or leave it empty. Anything else shows `Between 28 and 110 °F, or leave it empty.` in red, and the timer cannot start.
- **{button:Start the timer|primary|play}.** Starts counting the moment you tap it. Tap it as you get in.
- **{button:Type one in instead|link}.** Switches to typing in a plunge. See [Typing in a plunge](#typing-in-a-plunge) below.

### In the water

- **`Cold plunge · water 48°F`** at the top, or `Cold plunge` when you gave no temperature.
- **The time in the water**, in big numbers, counting up from `0:00`, with `in the water` under it.
- **A soft tone as each minute turns**, and a short buzz on an Android phone, so you can keep your eyes shut and count the minutes. The first tone is at one minute. The page says so: `A soft tone each minute, so you can keep your eyes shut. The screen stays on.`
- **The screen stays on** while the timer runs, so the phone does not lock in your hand.
- **{button:Done|primary}**, big, at the bottom. Tap it as you get out. The time stops there.

There is no back button while you are in the water. If you leave the page anyway, Health shows {button:Back to the timer|primary|play}, and the time has kept counting. If the page reloads while you are in the water, tap the screen once to bring the tones back. A plunge is timed for up to an hour; a timer left running for six hours is dropped.

### Out of the water

- **{button:Health|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Health. The plunge waits here until you save or discard it.
- **`Out of the water`**, with the time and the water, such as `3 min 10 s in 48°F`.
- **`How do you feel?`**, with a button for each number from `0` to `10`, and `0 is awful, 10 is wonderful.` under them. Tap a number to choose it. Tap it again to clear it. It is optional.
- **{button:Save|primary}.** Keeps the plunge. You see `Plunge kept: 3 min 10 s in 48°F.` and land back on Health, where the plunge is listed under `Cold plunge`.
- **{button:Discard|outline}.** For a timer you started by mistake. It asks `Discard this plunge? Its time is not kept.` Click {button:Discard|destructive} to throw it away and go back to the start, or {button:Keep it|outline} to go back to saving it.

### Typing in a plunge

For a plunge you timed with another clock, or forgot to time.

- **{button:Health|ghost|arrow-left}.** Back to Health, nothing saved.
- **`Type in a plunge`**, the title. Opened from an earlier day on Health, the day is under it, such as `For Friday, Oct 2`.
- **`Minutes`** and **`Seconds`.** Whole numbers. Leave one empty for none, so 45 seconds is `Seconds` 45 alone. A plunge is up to 60 minutes, and seconds go from 0 to 59. Anything else shows `Up to 60 minutes, and seconds 0 to 59.` in red.
- **`Water temperature, °F`.** The same as above: optional, from 28 to 110.
- **`How did you feel after?`**, from `0` to `10`, optional.
- **{button:Save|primary}.** Keeps the plunge as today's, or on the day under the title. It stays grayed out until the time is right. You see `Plunge kept: 2 min in 50°F.` and land back on Health, on that day.
- **{button:Use the timer|outline}.** Back to the timer. Not on an earlier day: the timer only times a plunge now.

## How to time a plunge

1. On Health, click {button:Start the timer|primary|play}.
2. Check `Water temperature, °F`, or change it.
3. Tap {button:Start the timer|primary|play} as you get in.
4. Count the tones: one a minute.
5. Tap {button:Done|primary} as you get out.
6. Tap how you feel, from `0` to `10`, and click {button:Save|primary}. You see `Plunge kept:` with the time, and land back on Health.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Plunge kept: 3 min 10 s in 48°F.` | The plunge is saved. It is listed under `Cold plunge` on Health and counted on Progress. |
| `Between 28 and 110 °F, or leave it empty.` | The temperature is not a number in that range. Fix it or clear it. |
| `Up to 60 minutes, and seconds 0 to 59.` | The typed time is not one Health keeps. Fix the minutes or the seconds. |
| `The plunge could not be kept. Try again.` | Saving did not work, often because the phone lost its signal. The plunge is still on the screen. Click {button:Save|primary} again. A plunge saved twice is kept once. |
| `Something in that was not right. Check it and try again.` | Health could not accept the plunge. The usual cause is the phone's clock: check its date and time, then save again. |
| `You can fill in today and the two weeks before it.` | The day under the title is more than two weeks back now. Go back to Health and choose the day again. |

## Not on this page

- **Plunges in Celsius.** The temperature is in °F. Ask us if you want °C.
- **A countdown to a target time.** The timer counts up. Ask us if you want it to count down to a time you choose.
- **Spoken minutes.** The timer sounds a tone; it does not speak.

## Who can do what

Only you. Health is in your personal space, which nobody else can open. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either.
