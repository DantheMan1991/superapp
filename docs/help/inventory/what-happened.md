# What happened

> Every entry ever recorded against anything you hold, newest first, with a search and pages.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/inventory/entries
> **Order:** 25

Open **Inventory** and click `What happened`. The heading reads `Every entry ever recorded, newest first. Every figure in this pack is added up from these.`

Nothing on this page changes anything. It is the record: every delivery, every bag used, every move, every correction, across every {{item|lower}}. What you see on the {{item|lower}} pages and on `What it is worth` is added up from exactly these lines.

## What you see

- **A search box.** `Search what, batch, reason or note`. It looks in the {{item|lower}}'s name, the batch code, the batch that ate it, the reason on a correction, and any note. It does not look at dates; the date has its own column. Typing narrows the list after a short pause, or press Enter. The term is in the address, so a search survives a refresh and can be sent to somebody.
- **A row of kinds.** `All`, then each kind you hold. `All` is a pill of its own, so you can see whether you are narrowed.
- **`Place`.** `All`, each place, and `No place` for entries that never said where. It only appears once you have somewhere to keep things.
- **The list.** On a phone, one card per entry: the {{item|lower}}, what happened and the reason, then the date, the place and the batch under it, any note, and the amount on the right with its cost under it. On a wide screen: `When`, `What`, `What happened`, `Batch`, `Where`, `Amount` and `Cost`. Tap or click anywhere on a row to open the {{item|lower}}.
- **`Batch`** names the batch the stock left, and for feed that was fed to animals, the batch that ate it: `BAG-12 · fed to PEN-3`.
- **`Amount`** carries a `+` when something came in. **`Cost`** is shown without a sign in both directions, and a dash means no price was recorded.
- **`Showing 1–50 of 312 entries`** with {button:Newer|outline} and {button:Older|outline}, under the list, once there is more than one page. The page is in the address too.

The kind and place pills are the same ones the {{item|lower}} list and `What it is worth` use, with the same words.

## How to find one entry

1. Type part of what you remember: the {{item|lower}}, the batch code, or a word from the note.
2. Narrow by kind or place if the list is still long.
3. The line at the bottom reads `Showing Market truck, Feed, matching “organic”.` with `Show everything` beside it to clear all of it at once, the search included.

A `%` or `_` you type is looked for as that character. Searching `100%` finds the note that says 100%, and nothing else.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing recorded yet` | No entry exists anywhere. Record a delivery and it will be the first. |
| `Nothing matches` | The search or the filters left nothing. Clear them. |
| `Showing 1–50 of 312 entries` | There is more than one page. `Older` walks back in time. |

## Not on this page

- Nothing here can be changed. An entry is corrected by recording another one, from the {{item|lower}}'s page.
- There is no date range. Search and the pages are the way through.
- Nothing narrows by who recorded an entry, because that is not recorded.
- Cost corrections and weight corrections are not entries and do not appear here. They are on the {{item|lower}}'s page, under the batches.
- There is no export.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone can read this page, an accountant included. There is nothing on it to press.
