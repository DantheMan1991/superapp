# {{parcel|plural}} and {{zone|plural}}

> The ground your business holds, and what each part of it is for. Everything that happens on the ground is recorded against a {{zone|lower}}, so this is the place to start.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/land/**
> **Order:** 0

## Two words

- A **{{parcel|lower}}** is a legal unit of ground: a deed or a lease.
- A **{{zone|lower}}** is a management area inside a {{parcel|lower}}. It is what the rest of Yosher points at when it records where something happened.

## Add your ground

Owners add {{parcel|plural|lower}}. Staff can see them but not change them.

1. Open **Land** from the sidebar.
2. Choose **Add parcel**. Give it a name, choose its tenure, and enter its area if you know it. The deed or lease reference and the notes are optional.
3. Open the {{parcel|lower}} to divide it into {{zone|plural|lower}}.

If a public parcel source covers your area, a **Find my parcels** button appears at the top of the page. It looks the boundaries up for you.

## Two deeds, one block of ground

When two {{parcel|plural|lower}} are really one piece of land, choose **Combine parcels** to join them. A fence line that crosses the deed boundary can then be drawn as one {{zone|lower}}. The button appears once you have more than one {{parcel|lower}}.

## In the field

Once a boundary has been traced, a shortcut appears at the top of the page that tells you which {{zone|lower}} you are standing in. Open it on your phone.
