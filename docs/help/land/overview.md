# Land at a glance

> The ground your business holds and what each part of it is for. This guide is the map of the Land pages; each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/land/**
> **Order:** 0

## Two words

- A **{{parcel|lower}}** is a legal unit of ground: a deed or a lease. It is what you own or rent.
- A **{{zone|lower}}** is a management area inside a {{parcel|lower}}. A grazier calls it a paddock, a market garden calls it a bed. It is what the rest of Yosher points at when it records what happened where, and what each cost is charged to.

Everything that happens on the ground has somewhere to land once the ground is divided into {{zone|plural|lower}}.

## The four pages

**Land**, from the sidebar, is the list of your {{parcel|plural|lower}}: their tenure, how many {{zone|plural|lower}} each has, and their area. Owners add {{parcel|plural|lower}} here, combine two deeds that are really one block of ground, and jump to the county's parcel records. See the guide **Your {{parcel|plural|lower}}**.

**One {{parcel|lower}}** is its own page: its details, its boundary measured against the deed figure, the site plan where fences, gates, lanes, water and every {{zone|lower}} are drawn, a growing-weather panel, the rotation arithmetic, and the table of {{zone|plural|lower}} with what each is for and how long it has rested. See **One {{parcel|lower}}** and **The site plan**.

**One {{zone|lower}}** is where stays are recorded: what is on it, from when to when. Every rest and rotation figure is worked out from those records. See **One {{zone|lower}}**.

**Find my parcels** searches the county's own parcel records and brings your ground in with its boundary and acreage already drawn. Ohio only today. See **Find my parcels**.

## Who can do what

The shape of the farm belongs to the owner. Only an owner can add, edit, combine or retire a {{parcel|lower}}, add or retire a {{zone|lower}}, set what a {{zone|lower}} is for, trace or replace a boundary, divide ground into {{zone|plural|lower}}, or save a materials list.

The chores are open to everyone. Any member can draw fences, gates, water and the rest on the site plan, mark them built or removed, walk to a corner with the phone, and record what is on a {{zone|lower}} and when it moved off.

Staff see the same pages without the owner's buttons. On the Land list, staff see no buttons at all.

## Units

Areas are shown in acres or hectares, lengths in feet or metres, and the weather panel in Fahrenheit or Celsius, according to how your business was set up. Every `Area in acres` field follows the same setting. If you need a different unit, ask us.

An unknown figure always shows as a dash, never as zero. A total with nothing known reads `not recorded` for area and `not drawn` for length.

## What Land does not do

- It never tracks anyone's position in the background. Every use of the phone's location is a button you press, and nothing records where you were.
- It does not store whether a gate is open or closed.
- It does not count head. Land supplies acreage; animal numbers belong to Livestock.
- The weather panel predicts nothing. It tells you how the season is running against past years, not when to move the herd.
- Nothing is refused for being under your rest target. The target is a comparison, not a rule.
- Retiring a {{parcel|lower}} or {{zone|lower}} is one way. History is kept, but it cannot be brought back, and combining two {{parcel|plural|lower}} cannot be undone.
- There is no bulk entry. Twenty {{zone|plural|lower}} is twenty dialogs, unless you divide ground on the site plan, which creates them together.
