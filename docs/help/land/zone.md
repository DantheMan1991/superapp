# One {{zone|lower}}

> A {{zone|lower}}'s own page: what is on it now, how long it has rested, what it has been for, and the stays that every rest figure is worked out from.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/land/*/zones/*
> **Order:** 40

Open a {{parcel|lower}} from the Land list, then click a {{zone|lower}}'s name in its table. This page is where you record what is on the ground, where it goes next, and when it moves off. To record something going on, click {button:Record a stay|primary}. To move something on or off in one act, use {button:Move something here|outline} or {button:Move to…|outline}. Recording is a chore, not an owner's decision, so everyone sees the buttons on an active {{zone|lower}}.

## What you see

- **The top of the page.** The link above the title carries the {{parcel|lower}}'s name and takes you back to its page. The title is the {{zone|lower}}'s name. The line under it shows its area, what it is currently for, a {badge:not productive|outline} badge for ground that is not expected to earn, and {badge:retired|outline} if it has been retired. At the right: **one move button, and which one depends on what is here.** While something is on this {{zone|lower}} you get {button:Move to…|outline}, then {button:Move off|outline}, then {button:Record a stay|primary}. While nothing is on it you get {button:Move something here|outline} and {button:Record a stay|primary} — and the move button only appears when there is something on this {{parcel|lower}} to bring.
- **`Rested`**, or **`Currently`** while something is on it. The large figure is `Occupied now`, `Never used`, or the days since the last stay ended, such as `18 days`. Under it: what is on it and since when, `Nothing has been recorded on it yet.`, or `Since [date]`. If the {{parcel|lower}} has a rest target, a line compares: `Target on this parcel is 21 days — met.` or `— 3 short.` It is a comparison only. Nothing is refused for being short.
- **`Grazing days`.** The total days something has been on it, `Across 6 stays, all time.`
- **`What it is for`.** The last five uses with their dates, or `Nothing declared yet.` A use is set from the {{zone|lower}}'s menu on the {{parcel|lower}}'s page.
- **`Boundary`.** The same reading as on the {{parcel|lower}}'s page: the measured area against the recorded one. The boundary itself is traced on the {{parcel|lower}}'s site plan, and `the site plan` in this section is a link there. Owners can also paste a boundary with {button:Add a boundary|outline} or {button:Replace boundary|outline}.
- **`Stays`.** A list with a count. On a computer it is a table: `What`, with any notes under it; `On`; `Off`, which shows {badge:still on it|outline} for an open stay and {badge:not yet|outline} for one that has not started; `Days`, counted with both ends included, or a dash while open; `Area used`, or `all of it`; and {button:Remove|outline} on each row. On a phone each stay is a card carrying the same things — the name, the dates, the days, the area and the notes — with {button:Remove|outline} at the bottom of it.

## How to record a stay

1. Click {button:Record a stay|primary}. The dialog is `What is on this {{zone|lower}}?` While nothing is on it, it reads `Leave the end date blank while it is still there. Rest is counted from the day it moves off.` While something is already on it: `Something is already here — move it off first, or record a stay that has already finished.`
2. Fill in `What is on it`. Required. A name you type, such as `Cow herd`. It is a name, not a pick list — but names this {{parcel|lower}} has carried before are offered as you type, so you do not have to remember exactly how you spelled it. Something recorded from Livestock arrives here with its own name.
3. Set `On`, the first day. Today to begin with. Leave `Off` blank while it is still there.
4. Fill in `Area used, in acres` only for a strip: `Running polywire? Put the strip size here — it is recorded against this stay, not as a place of its own.` Leave it blank for the whole {{zone|lower}}. Add `Notes` if you want them.
5. Click {button:Record|primary}. It reads `Saving…`, then you see `Recorded`.

The same name cannot be on two places at once. A stay recorded for a future date counts for nothing until that day arrives. A stay with an end date in the future means they are still there.

## How to move something to the next {{zone|lower}}

This is one act, not two. It closes the stay where they are and opens one where they are going, and it gets the dates right between them.

1. **Standing in the {{zone|lower}} they have just gone into**, open it — {button:Which paddock am I in?|outline} on the Land list gets you there — and click {button:Move something here|outline}.
2. **Or from the {{zone|lower}} they are leaving**, click {button:Move to…|outline}. The dialog is `Move off [name] — where to?`
3. Both read `One act, not two. The {{zone|lower}} they leave is closed the day before they arrive, so the same day is never grazed twice.`
4. Pick `What is moving`. The list is what is actually on this {{parcel|lower}} today, each one saying where it is and since when — `Cow herd — on North 40 since 2026-09-05`. You point at one rather than typing a name. If only one thing is on the {{parcel|lower}}, it is already picked.
5. From {button:Move to…|outline}, pick `Where to`. Type to narrow it if you have a lot of {{zone|plural|lower}}. From {button:Move something here|outline} there is no such field, because the {{zone|lower}} you are on is where they are going.
6. Set `On`, the first day on the new {{zone|lower}}. Today to begin with.
7. Fill in `Area used` only for a strip. It starts blank on purpose: a strip on the {{zone|lower}} they left says nothing about the one they are going to. Add `Notes` if you want them.
8. Click {button:Move|primary}. It reads `Moving…`, then you see `Cow herd is on Creek field — last day on the old one was 2026-09-09`.

That closing date is the point of doing it in one act. Move them on the 10th and the old {{zone|lower}} is closed on the 9th, so the 10th is grazing on the new one only. Move them on the same day they arrived and the old stay is one day long, which is the honest record when you are counting whole days.

A {{zone|lower}} you are already on is never offered, so you cannot move something onto the ground it is standing on.

## How to move something off

Use this when they are leaving the {{parcel|lower}} altogether — sold, or gone to another block. To send them to another {{zone|lower}} here, use the move above instead.

1. Click {button:Move off|outline}. The dialog is `Move [name] off?` and reads `Rest is counted from this day. It went on [date].`
2. Set `Last day on it`, today to begin with, and click {button:Move off|primary}. You see `Moved off — the rest clock starts now`.

The day it moves off counts as a day on this {{zone|lower}}. On Monday and off Monday is one grazing day.

## How to remove a stay that did not happen

1. Click {button:Remove|outline} on the row. The dialog is `Remove this stay?` and repeats what it was and when, then: `Every rest and rotation figure for this area is computed from these records, so remove it only if it did not happen.`
2. Click {button:Remove|primary}. It reads `Removing…`, then you see `Removed`.

A stay cannot be edited. A wrong date is fixed by removing the stay and recording it again.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Cow herd is on Creek field — last day on the old one was 2026-09-09` | The move worked. It tells you the closing date because that is the half you cannot see. |
| `That stay is not there any more, or it has already been closed.` | Somebody moved or removed it while your dialog was open. Close the dialog and look again. |
| `Nothing is recorded on this parcel to move. Record a stay instead.` | You opened {button:Move something here|outline} and there is nothing on the {{parcel|lower}} to bring. |
| `There is nowhere else on this parcel to move to yet.` | This {{parcel|lower}} has only the one {{zone|lower}}. |
| `Nothing recorded yet` and `Every rest and rotation figure is computed from these, so the first one is what makes the rest of the page work.` | No stay has been recorded on this {{zone|lower}}. |

## Not on this page

Setting what the {{zone|lower}} is for is done from its menu on the {{parcel|lower}}'s page. A move only reaches {{zone|plural|lower}} on the same {{parcel|lower}}; to send something to a different block of ground, move it off here and record a stay there.

## Who can do what

Everyone can record a stay, move something on or off, and remove a stay. Owners can also add or replace the boundary. A retired {{zone|lower}} shows owners {button:Put the {{zone|lower}} back|outline} and nothing else.
