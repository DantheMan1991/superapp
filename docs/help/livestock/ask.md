# Ask

> Husbandry questions answered against your own records, kept as threads you can come back to, and the things it will not answer.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/livestock/ask
> **Order:** 50

Open **Livestock** and click `Ask`. The heading reads `Husbandry questions, answered against your own records as of 2026-09-08.` Beside it, {button:New thread|outline|message-square-plus}.

You land on your newest thread, with everything you asked in it and everything it answered. A thread is yours: nobody else on the farm sees it, and you do not see theirs.

## What you see

- **{button:New thread|outline|message-square-plus}.** Opens an empty thread with the starter questions. The first question you ask starts the thread and puts it in the list. Nothing is made until you ask.
- **`Threads`.** Your threads, newest first, up to twenty. On a wide screen they run down the left. On a phone they are folded under `Threads · 3`; tap it to open the list. Each one shows its first question as its title and the day it was last used. The one on screen is shaded. Click a title to open that thread. {button:Remove|ghost} beside each one takes it off after asking `Remove "…"?`.
- **The starter questions.** Up to four buttons with a question written on each, picked from what your farm actually has. They show on an empty thread. Click one to ask it. To see them again, click {button:New thread|outline|message-square-plus}.
- **The conversation.** Your questions in gray boxes, the answers under them. Reloading the page keeps all of it.
- **The box.** Type a question, up to 2,000 characters. Enter sends it. Shift and Enter together start a new line.
- **{button:Ask|primary|send}.** Sends the question. It shows a spinner while it thinks.
- **The disclaimer, always under the box.** `Rules of thumb, anchored to your own records. It will not give you a medication dose or a withdrawal period — the label and your vet decide those.`

## What it knows about your farm

It is told, fresh for every question: each group's name, species, breed, sex, age and head; how many were lost of how many placed and the rate; which paddock they are on and since when; when they were last checked; what feed has cost and how that is known; the withdrawal state for meat and milk with the product and where the period came from; the latest weight, how it was taken, the daily gain and the feed conversion or the reason there is not one; and every loss with its date and how old the animals were that day.

It also knows your ground, what stock you hold, and how faithfully the daily round has been walked.

It remembers the thread you are in. A follow-up such as `And on HATCH-4A?` is read against what was asked before it, up to the last twenty turns. It does not remember other threads.

If you have nothing recorded yet it says so plainly and answers from general husbandry instead.

## What it will answer

Feed rates and rations, weaning and breeding ages, expected growth, what a normal loss rate looks like and whether yours points at brooding or heat, stocking density, rotation intervals, condition score, and what to look at when something seems off.

## What it will not answer

- **A medication dose, a route or a withdrawal period.** The label and your vet decide those. It will repeat a period you yourself recorded, and tell you that is where it came from.
- **Which rules apply where you are**, inspection or exemption limits, or what may be sold through which channel.
- **A diagnosis.**

When a question touches selling, processing, milk or moving animals on, it raises withdrawal first, and it treats a period nobody looked up as not clear.

## How to ask

1. Click a starter question, or type your own.
2. Press Enter, or click {button:Ask|primary|send}.
3. You see `Thinking…` and a spinner. A full answer can take a while. There is no progress bar and no way to cancel.
4. The whole answer appears at once, and the thread is kept.

If something goes wrong, your question is put back in the box so you do not lose it, and nothing is kept.

## How to start over

Click {button:New thread|outline|message-square-plus}. The old thread stays in the list. To get rid of one, click {button:Remove|ghost} beside it and confirm; you see `Thread removed`. Removing the thread you are in lands you on an empty one.

## The daily limit

A farm can ask a hundred questions in a rolling day, across everyone on it. At the limit the box closes and reads `This farm has asked 100 questions in the last day. It starts again as the oldest ones fall out of the day.` A question that got no answer does not count.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Thinking…` | It is working. Give it time. |
| `Ask a shorter question.` | Over 2,000 characters. |
| `This farm has asked 100 questions in the last day. It starts again as the oldest ones fall out of the day.` | The daily limit. Come back later. |
| `That thread is not yours or no longer exists. Start a new one.` | The thread was removed, or the link is somebody else's. |
| `That thread is not yours or no longer exists. This is a fresh one.` | Same, when you opened a link to it. |
| `The advisor gave no answer. Ask again.` | It came back empty. Nothing was kept. |
| `The advisor is not configured on this deployment yet.` | Ask is not switched on for you. Tell us. |
| `Thread removed` | It is gone, with everything in it. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Your question is back in the box. Try again. |

## Not on this page

- **What is kept is what you asked and what it said**, never the picture of the farm it was answered against. An old answer does not update when your records change.
- Only your last twenty threads are listed. Older ones are still yours; nothing shows them yet.
- There is no way to rename a thread, search them, or export one.
- It cannot change anything. It reads your records and answers; it never records, moves or books anything.
- It cannot see a photo, a document, the breeding calendar, or anything outside Livestock and your ground.
- There is no way to stop a long answer once it has started.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone can ask, and everyone gets the same kind of answer. Each person keeps their own threads. Nothing here writes to your records, so there is nothing to gate.
