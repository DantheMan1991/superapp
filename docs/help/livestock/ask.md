# Ask

> Husbandry questions answered against your own records, and the things it will not answer.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/livestock/ask
> **Order:** 50

Open **Livestock** and click `Ask`. The heading reads `Husbandry questions, answered against your own records as of 2026-09-03.`

## What you see

- **The starter questions.** Up to four buttons with a question written on each, picked from what your farm actually has. Click one to ask it. **They disappear after your first question and do not come back** unless you reload the page.
- **The box.** Type a question, up to 2,000 characters. Enter sends it. Shift and Enter together start a new line.
- **{button:Ask|primary|send}.** Sends the question. It shows a spinner while it thinks.
- **The disclaimer, always under the box.** `Rules of thumb, anchored to your own records. It will not give you a medication dose or a withdrawal period — the label and your vet decide those.`

## What it knows about your farm

It is told, fresh for every question: each group's name, species, breed, sex, age and head; how many were lost of how many placed and the rate; which paddock they are on and since when; when they were last checked; what feed has cost and how that is known; the withdrawal state for meat and milk with the product and where the period came from; the latest weight, how it was taken, the daily gain and the feed conversion or the reason there is not one; and every loss with its date and how old the animals were that day.

It also knows your ground, what stock you hold, and how faithfully the daily round has been walked.

If you have nothing recorded yet it says so plainly and answers from general husbandry instead.

## What it will answer

Feed rates and rations, weaning and breeding ages, expected growth, what a normal loss rate looks like and whether yours points at brooding or heat, stocking density, rotation intervals, condition score, and what to look at when something seems off.

## What it will not answer

- **A medication dose, a route or a withdrawal period.** The label and your vet decide those. It will repeat a period you yourself recorded, and tell you that is where it came from.
- **Which rules apply where you are**, inspection or exemption limits, or what may be sold through which channel.
- **A diagnosis.**

When a question touches selling, processing, milk or moving animals on, it raises withdrawal first, and it treats a period nobody looked up as not clear.

## How to ask

1. Click a starter question, or type your own.
2. Press Enter, or click {button:Ask|primary|send}.
3. You see `Thinking…` and a spinner. A full answer can take a while. There is no progress bar and no way to cancel.
4. The whole answer appears at once.

If something goes wrong, your question is put back in the box so you do not lose it.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Thinking…` | It is working. Give it time. |
| `Ask a shorter question.` | Over 2,000 characters, or the conversation has run too long. Reload and start again. |
| `The advisor is not configured on this deployment yet.` | Ask is not switched on for you. Tell us. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Your question is back in the box. Try again. |

## Not on this page

- **Nothing you ask is kept.** Reloading the page clears the conversation, and there is no way to save, copy or export it. We record only that a question was asked, never the question or the answer.
- There is no button to clear the thread or start a new one. Reload the page.
- It cannot change anything. It reads your records and answers; it never records, moves or books anything.
- It cannot see a photo, a document or anything outside Livestock and your ground.
- There is no way to stop a long answer once it has started.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone can ask, and everyone gets the same answer. Nothing here writes to your records, so there is nothing to gate.
