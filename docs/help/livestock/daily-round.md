# The daily round

> One tap per group to say you looked and they are fine, and somewhere to write it down when they are not.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/livestock/log
> **Order:** 30

Open **Livestock** and click `Daily round`. The heading reads `What you saw today, 2026-09-03. Most days nothing happens — say so in one tap.`

Everyone can walk the round, including your accountant. The round is walked by whoever is in the pens.

## Why the record matters

A day with no entry is a day nobody looked, and that is a different fact from a day when nothing happened. Your loss rate is worked out over the days somebody actually walked the pens, so the empty rows are load-bearing.

## What you see

- **{button:All normal (6)|primary|check}.** Marks every group you have not yet touched today as normal. Only appears while something is left.
- **`Checked`.** How many of today's groups you have looked at. It turns green when you are done.
- **`Streak`.** How many days in a row the round was walked, across the whole farm.
- **`Lost today`.** Head recorded as lost today. It turns red when there are any.
- **The table.** `Lot`, `Where`, `Head`, `Lost today`, `Last checked` and `Today`.
- **`Noted today`.** Only appears when something was flagged, listing each group and what was seen.

Only groups with animals in them are listed. A finished group drops off.

## The columns

- **`Lot`.** The name, its species underneath, and a {badge:Withdrawal · 7 days left|primary} badge when something is holding them back. A group that is clear shows no badge at all.
- **`Where`.** The paddock, with the pen or barn after it.
- **`Last checked`.** `Never`, `Today`, `Yesterday` or `5 days ago`.
- **`Today`.** Either {button:Mark normal|outline}, or once you have checked it a {badge:Normal|outline} or {badge:Noted|primary} badge. {button:Something's up|outline} sits beside it, and reads {button:Edit|outline} once there is an entry.

## How to walk the round

1. Go down the list. For every group that is fine, click {button:Mark normal|outline}.
2. Or click {button:All normal (6)|primary|check} once to do every untouched group at once. You see `6 lots marked normal`.
3. For anything that is not fine, click {button:Something's up|outline}.

{button:All normal|primary|check} never overwrites a group you have already flagged, and the number it reports is what it actually recorded.

**{button:Mark normal|outline} shows no message.** The row just changes. On a slow connection it can look like nothing happened, so give it a moment before clicking again.

## How to record something you saw

1. Click {button:Something's up|outline}. The dialog is headed with the group's name and reads `What you saw today. Losses go straight to the head count, so the two never disagree.`
2. If you lost animals, type how many into `Head lost` and pick `What happened`: `Died`, `Culled` or `Sold live`.
3. Write what you saw into `Notes`, such as `Lame in the left hind. Water was frozen.`
4. Click {button:Record|primary}. You see `Recorded`.

The help reads `Leave both empty and this records a normal day — which still counts, because "nothing died" and "nobody looked" are different facts.`

Anything with a loss or a note shows as {badge:Noted|primary} rather than {badge:Normal|outline}. That is a bookmark for you, not a severity.

Losses go straight onto the head count in the same action, so the two never disagree.

**One thing to watch.** Recording `Sold live` drops the head count correctly, but it does not appear under `Lost today`, because a live sale is not a loss. The box is still labelled `Head lost`, which reads oddly next to the option. We are fixing that.

Checking a group twice in one day updates the first entry rather than adding a second.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `6 lots marked normal` | That many were recorded. Ones already done are not counted again. |
| `Recorded` | The check is in, and any loss is already off the head count. |
| `Nothing to check yet` | No group has animals in it. Start one and place head. |
| `The whole farm, today.` | Everything has been looked at. |
| `Record today and it starts.` | Your streak is at zero. |
| `Head lost has to be a number.` | The box had something other than a number in it. |
| `Only 40 head are in this lot.` | You recorded more lost than there are. |
| `Withdrawal · Not looked up` | Somebody treated them and never read the label. Treat them as not clear. |

## Not on this page

- The round cannot be walked for a past day. It is always today.
- A group with no animals in it is not listed, even if you want to note something about it.
- Everything else about a group lives on its own page: weights, treatments, breeding, photos, moving them.
- `Sold live` does not show under `Lost today`.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone. Every control on this page is open to owners, staff and your accountant alike.
