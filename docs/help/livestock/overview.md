# Livestock at a glance

> The animals you keep, what they cost to raise, and what you saw when you walked the pens. This guide is the map of the Livestock pages. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/livestock/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Livestock** in the sidebar. Four tabs run along the top of every screen: `Overview`, `Daily round`, `Feed` and `Ask`.

## What you see

- **A {{livestockLot|lower}}.** A group of animals kept and costed together. A pen of feeders, a flock of layers.
- **An animal.** One you have named, with a page of her own. Her weights, treatments, photos and calves are all hers. An animal is a {{livestockLot|lower}} of one, so both live on the same kind of page.
- **`Counted as`.** The stock line the head are counted in. Head of it go up when they arrive and down when they leave, and their cost lands against it. Beef and dairy are two, even though both are cattle.
- **`Overview`.** Every {{livestockLot|lower}} with its species, where it is, its age, its loss rate and its withdrawal. See [Your animals](lots.md).
- **One {{livestockLot|lower}}'s page.** Head, losses, age, feed, weight, withdrawal, where they are, breeding, tags, checks, treatments and weighings. See [One {{livestockLot|lower}}](lot.md).
- **`Daily round`.** One tap to say you looked and they are fine. See [The daily round](daily-round.md).
- **`Feed`.** The largest cash cost, and what each {{livestockLot|lower}} carried of it. See [Feed](feed.md).
- **`Ask`.** Husbandry questions answered against your own records. See [Ask](ask.md).

## Two ideas the whole tool rests on

**A count is the sum of its entries.** Head go up when animals are placed and down when they die, are culled, are sold live or go to a processing run. Nothing stores a running total, so the number can never disagree with its own history.

**"Nothing happened" and "nobody looked" are different facts.** That is why the daily round exists and why a day with no entry is not a normal day. Your loss rate depends on telling them apart.

## How to get started

1. Start a {{livestockLot|lower}} on the `Overview` tab, or add one named animal.
2. Pick what it is `Counted as`. That decides how the cost is reported.
3. Place head into it as they arrive.
4. Walk the `Daily round` each day and tap through it.
5. Record treatments and weighings on the {{livestockLot|lower}}'s own page.
6. Record feed, either issued to one {{livestockLot|lower}} by name or drawn for a shared feeder.

## Withdrawal, which is the one thing to get right

When you treat animals, the app records how long until they can be processed and until their milk can be sold. **It does not know those periods and will not guess them.** You read them off the label in front of you.

If nobody looks the period up, the animals read as **not clear**, not as clear. An unknown is not a zero. That state shows on the list, on the {{livestockLot|lower}}'s page and on the daily round until somebody reads the label.

## Not on this page

- Nothing tracks individual animals inside a {{livestockLot|lower}} unless you name them. Naming one makes it a record of its own that stays in the {{livestockLot|lower}}.
- A {{livestockLot|lower}} can only sit inside one other, one level deep.
- Head never leave for a processing run from here. That is Production's job.
- Nothing here gives you a medication dose or a withdrawal period. Your label and your vet decide those.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

The daily work is open to everyone. Any member can place head, record a loss, treat, weigh, feed, tag, move to a paddock, walk the round, add a photo, and put an animal into a {{livestockLot|lower}} or take it out. Which pen she is in tonight is a record of where she is, not a decision about the herd.

The shape of the herd belongs to the owner. Only an owner can start a {{livestockLot|lower}}, split one, name animals out of one, close or reopen one, record breeding or parents, record a birth, move an animal to breeding stock, or add and close a shared feeder.

Your accountant can do everything a staff member can, except add or remove photos. A photo is a file, and files belong to Documents, where an accountant reads and never writes.
