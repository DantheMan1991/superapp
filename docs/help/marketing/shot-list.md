# Photos to take

> Every place on your website where a photo belongs, what to take there, and a way to put the photo in from your phone.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/marketing/website/photos
> **Order:** 220

Open **Marketing**, click `Website`, and click {button:Open the shot list|outline|camera} in `Photos`. The list is read from your pages as they are now: every section that can carry a photo is a row, whether it has one, holds one of the drawn pictures Yosher put there when it built the site, or is empty. It is not a list you keep; add a section that takes a photo and it is on the list the moment you save the page. It reads as well on a phone as at a desk, and that is the idea: open it in the field, walk round, and take each photo where you stand.

## What you see

- **The first card.** One line that says where things stand: `3 of the 11 places for a photo have one: 6 still to take, 2 drawn stand-ins to replace.`, `Every one of the 11 places for a photo has one.` or, before any section takes a photo, `The pages have no place for a photo yet. Add a section that takes one.` Under it, owners read `Open this on your phone, walk round, and tap Take a photo on each row. A photo goes into the page's draft at once; publish from the Website page when it reads right.` Staff read `Only an owner can add photos.`
- **A section per page**, in the order of your menu, headed with the page's title and its address such as `/shop`, with `Edit the page` at the right for owners; it opens the page in [Editing a page](page-editor.md). A page with no section that takes a photo reads `No place for a photo on this page.`
- **A row per place.** The rows still to fill come first, the optional ones after the wanted ones, and the filled ones last. Each row has:
  - *The picture, or the shape of it.* A filled place shows its photo small. An empty one shows a dashed box in the shape the place wants, labelled `Wide`, `Landscape`, `Square` or `Any shape`; under it the shape again, and `· optional` on a place that reads fine without a photo (beside a big headline, or on a card that has an icon). A place holding one of Yosher's drawn pictures shows the drawing with `A drawn stand-in` under it.
  - *What the place is.* Its name: `Behind the headline`, `Beside the headline`, `Beside the story`, an item's own name in a What you offer section, a card's heading (or `Card 2`), `The photo`, `The gallery`, `The slideshow`, `Photo 1` for a photo already in a gallery or slideshow, or `Behind the section` for a section whose background is a photo. Under it, the section's kind and heading, such as `What you offer: By the cut and by the share`.
  - *What to take.* A sentence or two on the photo that would work there, in your industry's words when your site was built from an industry template (a homestead farm reads `Beef as the customer gets it, close, in daylight, on a plain background: the wrapped pack, the open carton, the full box. Square if you can.` under an item called Beef) and in plain terms otherwise. On a drawn stand-in the line starts `Yosher drew this one to hold the place.`
  - *`Describe the photo`*, on a filled place, for owners: a box for what is in the picture, for people who cannot see it and for search engines, with {button:Suggest|ghost|sparkles} beside it when the assistant is switched on (it reads the picture and fills the box with a sentence, yours to change) and {button:Save description|outline}, grayed out until the words differ from what is saved.
  - *The buttons*, for owners. On a phone or a tablet: {button:Take a photo|outline|camera}, which opens the camera, and {button:Choose from your phone|ghost|image-plus}, which opens your photos. On a computer: {button:Upload a photo|outline|image-plus}, which opens a file window. On either, {button:From the library|ghost|images} opens `Your site's photos`, every photo already on the site; click one to use it here. A photo you take or choose is uploaded, the button reading `Uploading…`, checked, resized for the web with the camera's data stripped, added to the library and put into this place, all in one go. On a filled place the same buttons swap the photo for another.

## How to take the photos your site asks for

1. Open **Marketing**, `Website`, and click {button:Open the shot list|outline|camera}. On a phone, sign in to Yosher in the phone's browser and open the same page; the list fits a phone's width.
2. Read the first card to see how many places are still empty, then go to the first page's rows. The rows that still need a photo are at the top of each page.
3. Stand where the sentence under the row suggests and tap {button:Take a photo|outline|camera}. Take the picture; the phone hands it to Yosher, the button reads `Uploading…` for a few seconds, and the row shows the photo with `Photo placed.` The page's draft now has it.
4. Type a few words in `Describe the photo`, or tap {button:Suggest|ghost|sparkles}, and click {button:Save description|outline}. `Description saved.` confirms it.
5. When the pages read right, go back to `Website` and click {button:Publish changes|primary}. Until then the photos are on your draft only; {button:Preview the draft|outline|external-link} shows them in place.

## How the drawn stand-ins work

When Yosher built your site from an industry template it drew a picture or two in your brand's colours to hold the places a photo belongs, so the pages did not open with holes in them. Those are the rows marked `A drawn stand-in`. They count as places still to fill, since a real photo of your own place beats any drawing. Replace each the same way as an empty place; the drawing stays in the library in case you want it back.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Photo placed.` | The photo is in the place and the page's draft is saved, with a version kept as with any save. |
| `Description saved.` | The words under the photo are saved with the page. |
| `That photo is over 12MB. Export it smaller and try again.` | The limit for one upload. |
| `That file isn't a photo Yosher can use. Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP.` | The file is not one of the three kinds. |
| `A site can hold up to 60 photos. Remove one first.` | The library is full; remove one from `Your site's photos`. |
| `That photo is no longer there. Upload it again.` | The photo chosen from the library was removed before it could be placed. |
| `That place is not on the page.`, `That section is no longer on the page.`, `That item is no longer in the section.`, `That card is no longer in the section.`, `That photo is no longer in the section.`, `That section no longer has a photo behind it.`, `That section does not take a photo there.` | The page changed since the list was opened, in the editor or by someone else. Reload the list and it reads the pages as they are now. |
| `That page no longer exists.` | The page was deleted since the list was opened. |
| `Only an owner can change how the business looks.` | Placing a photo is an owner's action. |
| `The upload didn't finish. Try again.` | The connection dropped before the photo reached Yosher. Nothing was changed. |

## Not on this page

Moving a photo to another place, taking one off a page, a gallery photo's caption and the order of a gallery's photos: [Editing a page](page-editor.md). Removing a photo from the whole site: the {icon:trash} in `Your site's photos`, described there too. Publishing: [Your website](website.md).

## Who can do what

Owners see every row and can take, choose and place photos and save descriptions. Staff see the list and the photos and change nothing; the line under the first card says so. Accountants see the same read-only list.
