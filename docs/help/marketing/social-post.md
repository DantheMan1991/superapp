# Writing one post

> The words, the picture and the time for one post, and the two buttons that hand it to you when it is time to post it.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/marketing/social/posts/*
> **Order:** 235

Open **Marketing**, click `Social`, and click a post in the list, or click {button:Write a post|primary|plus} to make a new one. This page is one post to one account. Type what it says, pick a photo and the shape it should be cut to, and say when it goes out. Nothing here posts anything: when the time comes you click {button:Copy the words|outline|copy}, {button:Save the picture|outline|download}, post it yourself, and then click {button:I posted it|outline|check}.

## What you see

- **`Back to social`**, at the top, with {icon:chevron-left}.
- **The heading.** `A post`, with `Write it, put a picture on it, and say when it goes out.`

### The words

- **The account this post is for**, such as `Facebook · @oakrowfarm`, at the top of the first card. **You cannot change it.** A post is written for one account's readers, at that network's length, cut to the shape that network shows. To say the same thing on another account, write a second post there.
- **{badge:Draft}**, **{badge:Scheduled}** or **{badge:Posted|secondary}** beside it, and **{badge:Time to post|primary}** once Yosher has reminded you.
- **`What it says`.** A box eight lines tall. Under it, `240 of 280 characters`; the number on the right is what that network takes, and it turns red with ` — X will not take this many.` when you go over. Saving is blocked until you are back under.
- **`A link to include (optional)`.** A page on your site such as `/shop`, or a full `https://` address. Yosher does not shorten it or track it.
- **{button:Save|primary}**, grayed out until something has changed and you are under the limit.
- **{button:Copy the words|outline|copy}**, which copies what is in the box. You see `Copied. Paste it where you post.`

### The picture

- **{button:Add photos|ghost}**, at the right, when this brand has a website. It opens [Photos to take](shot-list.md), where photos come from.
- **A row of photos** from this brand's website library. Click one to use it; the one in use has a colored ring. An account that belongs to your business rather than to a website offers the photos from all of your websites.
- **{button:No picture|ghost}**, to take the photo off again.
- **`Square`, `Tall`, `Full screen`, `Wide`.** What the photo is cut to. Yosher picks the one that network usually shows — square for Facebook, tall for Instagram, full screen for TikTok, wide for X and LinkedIn. Under them, a line saying what that shape is for, such as `The tallest an Instagram or Facebook feed will show.`
- **`Tap the part that matters`.** The photo with everything outside the cut dimmed. Click anywhere to move the cut there; the arrow keys nudge it. Your photo is never changed — only this post's copy of it is cut.
- **{button:Back to the middle|ghost|crosshair}**, to undo the tapping.
- **{button:Save the picture|outline|download}**, once the post is saved. It downloads the photo already cut, ready to attach. While you have unsaved changes it is replaced by `Save first, then the picture can be downloaded as it will look.`
- With no photos yet: `This brand's website has no photos yet. Add some, and they can go on a post.`, or for a brand with no website, `Photos live with a website. Build one for this brand, and its photos can go on a post.`

### When

- **`Put it on the calendar`.** A date and time box. It shows your business's own clock, so a post set for 7am is 7am at the farm wherever you happen to be.
- **{button:Schedule it|primary|calendar-clock}**, or {button:Change the time|primary|calendar-clock} once it is scheduled. Grayed out until you have written something.
- **{button:Take it off|ghost}**, on a scheduled post, which puts it back to a draft and forgets the reminder.
- **The line underneath.** `Times round to the nearest ten minutes, which is how often Yosher checks. It cannot post for you — it reminds you, in What needs you.`
- **{button:I posted it|outline|check}**, which records that you posted it and takes it off the list of things to do. Nobody is checking — it is your word for it.
- **{button:Delete|ghost|trash}**, which asks `Delete this post?` and explains `The words and the choice of picture go. The photo itself stays in your library.`
- On a post you have marked posted, the box is replaced by `Marked posted September 12, 2026 at 7:10 AM.`

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Saved.` | The words, the link and the picture are saved. |
| `On the calendar. Yosher will remind you.` | It is scheduled, rounded to the nearest ten minutes. |
| `Off the calendar.` | It is a draft again. Any reminder already raised stays in `What needs you`; close it there. |
| `Marked posted.` | Recorded. Nothing was sent anywhere. |
| `Copied. Paste it where you post.` | The words are on your clipboard. |
| `Deleted.` | The post is gone. The photo is still in your library. |
| `Write something first, then it can go on the calendar.` | An empty post cannot be scheduled. |
| `Facebook takes 5,000 characters; this is 5,140.` | Shorten it, then save. |
| `Pick a date and a time for it to go out.` | The date box is empty or unreadable. |
| `That post is no longer there.` | It was deleted since you opened it, perhaps by someone else. |
| `That photo is no longer there. Upload it again.` | The photo was removed from the library while you had it chosen. |
| `A link is a page on this site such as /contact, a full https:// address, or a mailto: or tel: link.` | The link box holds something that is none of those. |
| `Only an owner can change how the business looks.` | Writing and scheduling posts is an owner's job. |
| `Accountant access is read-only.` | Accountants see this page and change nothing. |

Owners do everything here. Staff read the post and cannot change, schedule or delete it. Accountants see the same read-only page.
