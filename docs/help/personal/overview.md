# Your personal space

> Your own space beside your business, for your own things: your workouts, your food and your health. This page is its home. Only you can open it.
> **Route:** /personal
> **Order:** 1

Your personal space sits beside your business. It is where your own things go, not the business's. Open it from the account menu with `Personal space`, and switch back to your business with the workspace switcher. This page is its home: it says who can see what you keep here, what is coming, and how to get back.

## What you see

- **The sidebar.** One row, `Home` {icon:dashboard}, which is this page. Each tool you use here gets its own row when it is ready. There is no Business or Settings group: your team, your bills and your business settings stay in your business.
- **`Personal`**, the title, with the line `Your own space, beside your business. Only you can open it.` under it.
- **The {icon:circle-question-mark} beside the title.** It opens this guide in a panel beside the page.
- **The `Private to you` card.** It says that nobody from your business can see anything here, that nobody can be invited in, and that Yosher staff cannot open it from the product either. If something goes wrong in your personal space, send us a screenshot, because we cannot look for ourselves.
- **The `Your tools` card.** While nothing is switched on it says `Nothing is switched on here yet.`, and what is coming: Workouts; Food, with what you eat counted against your targets, the week's meals planned and your recipes, then the shopping list; and Health, with your sleep, weight and tape measures, cold plunges and habits, and your progress week by week. Once a tool is on, the card lists it with a line on what it does, such as **Workouts** {icon:dumbbell}, **Food** {icon:utensils} and **Health** {icon:heart-pulse}; click one to open it. Under the list it says `The shopping list comes next.` Each tool appears on this page and in the sidebar as soon as it is ready.
- **The `Back to your business` card.** It tells you where the workspace switcher is.
- **The workspace switcher.** At the bottom of the sidebar on a computer, at the top right on a phone. Here it shows `Personal`. Open it and pick your business to go back.
- **Your account button.** Beside the switcher. It opens your Yosher account: your name, your email, and signing out.

## How to open your personal space

1. In your business, open your account button, beside the workspace switcher.
2. Click `Personal space`.
3. The first time, you see `A space of your own`, with what the space is for and who can see it. Click {button:Create my personal space|primary}. The button reads `Making your space…` while it works.
4. You land on this page. Your personal space now appears in the workspace switcher as `Personal`.

The next time you click `Personal space`, you see `Opening your personal space…` and land here straight away. You can also pick `Personal` in the workspace switcher.

## How to go back to your business

1. Open the workspace switcher, at the bottom of the sidebar or at the top right on a phone.
2. Pick your business. You land on its overview.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Opening your personal space…` | You already have a personal space, and Yosher is switching you to it. It takes a second. |
| `Making your space…` | Yosher is creating your personal space. Wait for it to finish. |
| `Personal spaces are not open yet.` | Personal spaces are not available to everyone yet. `Personal space` appears in your account menu when they are. |
| `Your personal space could not be made. Try again in a moment.` | Something went wrong while creating it, and nothing was left half made. Click {button:Create my personal space|primary} again. |
| `Your personal space could not be opened. Try again.` | Switching to your personal space failed. Click {button:Try again|primary}, or pick `Personal` in the workspace switcher. |
| `Your personal space is ready, but switching to it failed. Pick Personal in the workspace switcher.` | Your space was made, but the switch did not happen. Pick `Personal` in the workspace switcher to open it. |
| `This is somebody else's personal space` | You tried to open a personal space that is not yours. Nobody can open another person's. Pick one of your own workspaces in the switcher shown under the message. |
| `You already have a personal space` | A spare personal space was started but never finished. Pick your own personal space, or one of your businesses, in the switcher shown under the message. |

## Not on this page

- **The shopping list.** It comes to Food next, made from the week. It arrives on this page when it is ready.
- **Inviting somebody in.** A personal space is for one person. A space shared with a partner, such as a household meal plan, is not built. Ask us if you want it.
- **Deleting your personal space.** Not built yet. Ask us and we will remove it for you.
- **Settings for your personal space.** Its clock comes from your browser the day you create it. There is nothing to change here yet.

## Who can do what

Only you. Your personal space belongs to the person who created it. Nobody else can open it, whatever their role in your business, and nobody can be invited into it. Yosher staff cannot open it from the product.
