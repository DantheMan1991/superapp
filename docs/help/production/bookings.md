# Booked dates

> The dates you hold with an outside business, and what you have promised them.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/production/bookings
> **Order:** 50

Open **Production** and click `Booked dates`. Good plants are booked six to twelve months ahead, which is the whole reason this page exists.

## What you see

- **{button:Book a date|primary}.** Records a date you hold. Owners only.
- **`Coming up`.** Dates still ahead of you.
- **`Done with`.** Dates that have passed or been used.
- **The table.** Who, when, what you have promised, the deposit, and where it stands.

Each section only appears when it has something in it.

## How to book a date

1. Click {button:Book a date|primary}.
2. Pick the {{processor|lower}}.
3. Set the date.
4. Say how many head you have promised them.
5. Record a deposit if you paid one.
6. Add a reference and notes.
7. Save.

## How to turn a booked date into a day's work

Open the booking and start the {{productionRun|lower}} from it. That carries the plant and the date across, so you are not typing them twice.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing recorded` | No dates booked. |
| `Only an owner can change this.` | Booking and changing dates are owners only. |
| `That no longer exists.` | The booking has gone. Reload. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Tell us if it keeps happening. |

## Not on this page

- The reference, the notes and the day you paid a deposit are all recorded and shown nowhere.
- A booking can be removed even when it cannot be cancelled, which is us being inconsistent.
- {button:Remove|ghost} deletes with no confirmation.
- Nothing warns you that a booked date is coming up. Check the page.
- Nothing here books a date with the plant. You still ring them.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Only an owner can book a date, change one, remove one, or start a {{productionRun|lower}} from one. Holding a date is a commitment to a plant.

Writing a {{cutSheet|lower}} against a booked date is open to everyone, an accountant included, and so is everything on the sheet once it exists.
