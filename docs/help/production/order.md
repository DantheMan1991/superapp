# One {{cutSheet|lower}}

> The instruction you print and hand to the plant with the animals.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/production/orders/*
> **Order:** 40

Open **Production**, click `Every {{cutSheet|lower}}`, then click a name. Or open it from a {{productionRun|lower}}'s own page.

## What you see

- **{button:Print the order|outline|printer}.** Prints the sheet and stamps the day it was printed.
- **{button:Remove|ghost}.** Deletes the sheet. There is no confirmation.
- **`What they were asked to do`.** Every line, what it will cost, and anything the app could not price.

The page has a header made for the printed page that you never see on screen, and a `Quoted` block that you see on screen and never on the print.

## How to add what you want done

1. Add a line for each thing you want. Give the quantity.
2. The app finds the price itself, from what that plant charges for that kind of work at that head count.
3. It stamps the label, the price, the unit and any minimum onto the line, so a later price change does not restate this sheet.

**When the app cannot price a line it says so rather than guessing**, and tells you the real figure is higher than what is shown. The usual reason is that nobody has counted the head.

Some lines are quoted per hanging pound, which is why a red-meat plant's quote cannot be settled until the {{killSheet|lower}} arrives.

## How to print it

1. Click {button:Print the order|outline|printer}.
2. Your browser's print dialog opens.
3. The `Printed` column on the list fills in.

Print it and send it with the animals. The plant reads this rather than guessing.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `3 lines could not be worked out because nobody has counted them` | The real figure is higher than shown. Give a head count. |
| `That no longer exists.` | The sheet has gone. Reload. |
| `Only an owner can change this.` | Recording what a plant charges is owners only. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Tell us if it keeps happening. |

## Not on this page

- The head count cannot be changed after the sheet is written, though it is what the price band is checked against.
- {button:Remove|ghost} deletes with no confirmation.
- There is no customer here and no price to a customer. This is what you are asking the plant for.
- Prices come from the {{processor|lower}} directory. They cannot be typed on a line here.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Adding, counting, removing and printing are open to everyone. Only an owner can record what a {{processor|lower}} charges, which is what these lines are priced from.
