# Your {{processor|plural|lower}}

> Who does your processing, what they take, what they charge, and how they are inspected.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/production/processors
> **Order:** 70

Open **Production** and click the {{processor|lower}} directory.

A {{processor|lower}} is an outside business that does part of a {{productionRun|lower}} for you. A butcher, a co-packer, a subcontractor. Same relationship whatever the trade.

## What you see

One panel per {{processor|lower}}, holding:

- **Who they are**, with badges for how they are inspected.
- **The inspection note.** This is the one that matters, because **it decides where the meat may be sold.**
- **`What they take`.** The animals and the work they will do.
- **`What they charge`.** Their price list, by animal and by category.
- **`Cuts they do`.** What you can ask for on a {{cutSheet|lower}}.
- **`What you think`.** Your own note on them.

{button:Add a processor|primary} at the top adds one. Owners only.

## Why the inspection matters

Every box that comes off a {{productionRun|lower}} is stamped with how the work was inspected, and that stamp travels with the meat.

- **`USDA inspected`.** May be sold across state lines and through any channel, wholesale included.
- **`State inspected`.** Usually equal to federal within the state and not across state lines. Check the programme before selling out of state.
- **`Custom exempt`.** Cut for the owner of the animal and stamped not for sale. It may be eaten by whoever owned it live, **which is why halves are sold before the kill and never after**.
- **`Not inspected`.** Narrow, and it varies by state. Typically direct to the person eating it, in state, with no resale.
- **`Not established`.** Nobody has recorded it, so nothing can say where the meat may go. It is the first thing to ask them.

## How to record what they charge

An owner records the price list by animal and by category, including any head bands and minimums. Those prices are what a {{cutSheet|lower}} is worked out from.

**You can photograph a rate sheet instead of typing it.** The app reads it and proposes rows. It never averages a range and never guesses a unit, and **nothing is saved until a person confirms it**, at which point the ordinary owner-only save runs.

Some prices are per hanging pound, which is why a red-meat quote cannot be settled until the {{killSheet|lower}} arrives.

## Not on this page

- A {{processor|lower}} cannot be removed once added. The `Not in use` badge exists and nothing can set it.
- Removing a price, a cut or a handle asks for no confirmation.
- Recording a plant here does **not** make them a vendor, and their bill will not appear under `Processing not invoiced` until they are one. Ask us rather than adding them a second time.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Only an owner can add a {{processor|lower}}, change one, record or remove what they charge, or add and remove cuts. Everyone else can read the whole directory, which is what somebody standing at a plant needs.
