# Discovery

> What you learned about a business before you took it on, and what the copilot made of it. Every discovery you have run is here.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/professional-services/discovery
> **Order:** 30

Open **Professional services** in the sidebar, then click `Discovery` under the list. This is where you write down what a business told you, work out what their problems are costing them, and produce the two documents that come out of it. To begin one, click {button:New discovery|primary}.

## What you see

- **{button:New discovery|primary}.** Opens the dialog that starts one. Everyone on the team sees it.
- **{icon:circle-question-mark}.** Opens this guide beside the screen.
- **`Business`.** Who the discovery is about. Click the name to open it. A discovery that is not pointed at anybody yet shows `Not attached` beside the name, and one that came from your website's health check says `From the health check` underneath.
- **`Status`.** `Open` while you are still learning, `Report ready` once a report has been written. You can move it back.
- **`Exchanges`.** How many times you and the copilot have gone back and forth.
- **`Last touched`.** The day something last changed, as year, month and day. Newest first, always.
- **The empty list.** Before you have run one you see `No discovery yet`, and an invitation to start one.

## How to start a discovery

1. Click {button:New discovery|primary}. The dialog reads `Who it is about, and anything you already know going in.`
2. Pick the `{{client}}`. Only businesses already in your CRM are offered, because a discovery is about one of them. If the list is empty, add them to your CRM first.
3. Fill in `What you know going in` — how you met, who referred them, what they said the problem was. Up to 10,000 characters. The copilot starts from this, so it is worth a few sentences.
4. Click {button:Start discovery|primary}. You see `Discovery started` and land straight in the conversation.

## Not on this page

- You cannot start a discovery for a business that is not in your CRM. Add them there first.
- There is no search, no filter and no sorting. The list is newest first.
- You cannot delete from here. Open the discovery and delete it there.
- Discoveries are not shared with the business they are about. Nothing here is client-facing until you send them the report yourself.

## Who can do what

Everyone on the team can start a discovery, hold the conversation and write the report. That is deliberate: the person talking to a prospect is the person who should be writing down what they said.

Only an owner can delete one.
