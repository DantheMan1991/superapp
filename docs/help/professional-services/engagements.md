# Every {{engagement|lower}}

> The list of what you have taken on, with how each one's month is going. Agree a new one here.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/professional-services
> **Order:** 10

Open **Professional services** in the sidebar. Everything live is listed here, with the ones that need attention easiest to spot: an {{engagement|lower}} over its hours shows the overage in red. To take on something new, click {button:New {{engagement|lower}}|primary}.

## What you see

- **The heading.** Once you have {{engagement|plural|lower}}, it reads `4.5 h logged this month across what is live.` That is every hour logged this month against anything `Active`, added up. Before then it explains what the page is for.
- **{button:New {{engagement|lower}}|primary}.** Opens the dialog that agrees a new one. Owners only.
- **{icon:circle-question-mark}.** Opens this guide beside the screen.
- **`{{engagement}}`.** The name, with the first line of its scope underneath. Click the name to open it.
- **`{{client}}`.** Who the work is for.
- **`Kind`.** `Retainer`, `Project`, `Hourly`, or a word of your own.
- **`Status`.** `Proposed`, `Active`, `Paused` or `Ended`. Live work is filled in; everything else is outlined.
- **`This month`.** Hours logged this calendar month. With hours included it reads `11.5 h of 10.0 h`. Under that, `over by 1.5 h · $180.00` in red when it has gone over, or `near the limit` in gray once four fifths of the hours are used.
- **`Worth`.** The fee. An {{engagement|lower}} with no fee but a rate shows the rate as `$120.00/h`. One with neither shows a dash.
- **`Show ended`.** Under the table. Click it to include finished work; the link then reads `Hide ended`.
- **The empty list.** Before you have agreed anything you see `No {{engagement|plural|lower}} yet`. An owner is told to agree the first one. Everyone else is told an owner will.

The order is fixed: live work first, then paused, then proposals, then ended; within each, by {{client|lower}} and then by name. There is no sorting and no search.

## How to agree a new {{engagement|lower}}

1. Click {button:New {{engagement|lower}}|primary}. The dialog reads `What you have agreed to do, for whom, for how much, from when.`
2. Pick the `{{client}}`. The list is every business you already know, whether they came from here, from an invoice or from your CRM. To take on somebody new, pick `Somebody new…` and type their business name in the box that appears.
3. Read the line under it: `This cannot be changed later — work for somebody else is its own {{engagement|lower}}.` Check you have the right one. There is no way to move an {{engagement|lower}} to another {{client|lower}} afterwards, because its whole time log would go with it.
4. Type a `Name`, such as `Monthly bookkeeping`. Up to 200 characters. This is the only field you must fill in besides the dates.
5. Pick a `Kind`. To use a word of your own, pick `Something else…` and type it. Use lowercase letters, numbers and spaces only. A word starting with a digit is refused.
6. Set `Starts`. It defaults to today. Set `Ends` only if you have agreed an end date; leave it blank for work that runs on.
7. Fill in `Hours a month` if the fee covers a number of hours. Half hours are fine. Leave it blank or at zero and nothing will ever read as over.
8. Fill in `Fee` for what you charge for the period, and `Rate an hour` for what an hour beyond the hours is worth. The line under them reads `Hours a month is what the fee covers. The rate prices anything beyond it.` Both can be blank.
9. Add the `Scope` — what you have agreed to do, in the words of the agreement. Up to 5,000 characters. It shows on the {{engagement|lower}}'s page and its first line shows in the list.
10. Add `Notes` if you want to. Up to 5,000 characters, and only you see them.
11. Click {button:Add {{engagement|lower}}|primary}. You see `{{engagement}} agreed` and the new row in the list.

A new {{engagement|lower}} starts as `Active`. The hours you set become this {{engagement|lower}}'s allowance from the month it starts in, and every later change is recorded from the month you make it, so an old month keeps the figure that was agreed then.

Agreeing an {{engagement|lower}} also makes it something your accounts can group by. Tag a bill or a journal entry with it and your profit and loss can tell you what this piece of work actually cost.

## How to bring somebody new in

You do not have to add a {{client|lower}} anywhere first. Pick `Somebody new…` in the dialog and type the business name; they become a business your whole workspace knows, exactly as though you had added them in your CRM or invoiced them. Next time they are in the list.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `{{engagement}} agreed` | It worked. The new row is in the list. |
| `Pick a {{client|lower}}, or type a new one.` | You chose `Somebody new…` and left the name box empty. |
| `That {{client|lower}} no longer exists. Reload and pick again.` | The business was merged or removed since the page loaded. |
| `A kind must be lowercase letters, numbers and underscores.` | Your own kind has a capital, a symbol or a leading digit in it. Try plain words, such as `fixed fee`. |
| `Check the dates. An {{engagement|lower}} cannot end before it starts.` | `Ends` is earlier than `Starts`. |
| `Only an owner can change an {{engagement|lower}}.` | You are signed in as staff. Ask an owner. |
| `Check the details and try again.` | Something in the dialog is not filled in the way it needs to be. Most often the `New kind` box is blank. |
| `Something went wrong saving that.` | Something unexpected. Try again, and tell us if it keeps happening. |
| `No {{engagement|plural|lower}} yet` | The list is empty. An owner agrees the first one. |

## Not on this page

- There is no search box, and no way to sort or filter beyond `Show ended`.
- You cannot log time from the list. Open the {{engagement|lower}} first.
- You cannot edit terms or change a status here. Both are on the {{engagement|lower}}'s own page.
- Nothing here totals what you are owed, or what is unbilled across every {{client|lower}}.
- Nothing can be brought in from a spreadsheet.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Only an owner can agree a new {{engagement|lower}}. Staff and accountants see the whole list, every figure on it included, with no button in the top corner.
