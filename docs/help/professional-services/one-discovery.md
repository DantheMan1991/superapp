# One discovery

> The conversation with the copilot, and the two documents it produces: the one you send them and the one you keep.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/professional-services/discovery/*
> **Order:** 40

Click a business name in the Discovery list to open it. Tell the copilot what you learned, and it works out what the problem is costing them, does the arithmetic, and tells you what to ask next. When you have enough, click {button:Write the report|secondary}.

## What you see

- **`Discovery`.** Top left, with a {icon:chevron-left}. Takes you back to the list.
- **The heading.** The business's name, and under it what you wrote down going in. If you wrote nothing it reads `Nothing was written down going in.`
- **`Not attached`** and {button:Attach to a client|outline}. Only when the discovery is not pointed at a business in your CRM — usually one that came in through your website's health check. See below.
- **{button:Delete|ghost}.** Owners only. Removes the whole thing.
- **`Conversation`** and **`Report`** tabs. A small dot on `Report` means one has been written.
- **The status box.** `Open` or `Report ready`. Writing a report sets it; you can set it back.
- **{button:Write the report|secondary}.** Turns the whole conversation into the two documents. Grayed out until you have said something. Once a report exists it reads {button:Write it again|secondary}.
- **The message box.** What you type to the copilot. Up to 20,000 characters. {kbd:Ctrl+Enter} sends it.

## How to have the conversation

1. Type what you know, or what they said, in your own words. `He spends every Sunday doing quotes in a spreadsheet, about 5 hours. Loses maybe 1 in 3 jobs because he quotes too slow.`
2. Press {kbd:Ctrl+Enter}, or click the send button. You see `Thinking…` while it works.
3. Read what comes back. It will put a number on the problem, show its arithmetic, and usually end with the two or three questions worth asking next.
4. Go back to them with those questions, and bring the answers here. Repeat until the picture is solid.

The copilot works from what you tell it. It will ask for facts rather than invent them, and it will tell you plainly when somebody looks like a bad fit — a cheap no now beats an expensive one later.

## How to write the report

1. Click {button:Write the report|secondary}. It reads `Working…`; a long one takes a minute.
2. You see `Report written — see the Report tab`.
3. Open the `Report` tab. You get two things in one document: a **Business Health Check** in plain language for the owner, and a **Build Spec** for your own eyes.
4. Copy what you need out of it. Nothing is sent anywhere on its own.

Writing it again replaces what was there. If the conversation has moved on, that is what you want; if you have edited the text somewhere else, copy it out first.

## How to attach one to a business

A discovery that came in through your website's health check is about somebody who was not in your CRM yet.

1. Click {button:Attach to a client|outline}.
2. Pick the business from the list.
3. Click {button:Attach|primary}. You see `Attached`, and the list now shows their CRM name instead of the name they typed.

The conversation is untouched. It just gains a home, so it shows up against that business everywhere else.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Discovery started` | It worked. You land in the conversation. |
| `Report written — see the Report tab` | The two documents are ready. |
| `Attached` | The discovery now belongs to that business. |
| `Deleted` | The conversation and the report are gone, and you are back on the list. |
| `The copilot did not answer. Try again in a moment.` | The call failed. Nothing was lost — your message is still in the box. |
| `The AI copilot is not configured on this workspace yet — ask us.` | The copilot has not been switched on for you. |
| `The copilot answered with nothing — try again.` | A rare empty answer. Send it again. |
| `Have at least one exchange with the copilot first.` | You clicked {button:Write the report|secondary} before saying anything. |
| `That business is not in your CRM. Reload and pick again.` | The business was removed or merged since the page loaded. |
| `You cannot change this one.` | You are staff and tried to delete. Ask an owner. |

## The copilot and your prices

The copilot is told what your business does, what you sell and what a client would otherwise do, so it can price the problem against your actual offer. If nobody has told it that, you will see a line saying so, and it will ask instead of guessing at prices. Ask us to fill it in — every discovery after that gets sharper.

## Not on this page

- Nothing here is sent to the business it is about. The report is yours to send.
- You cannot edit a message once it is sent, and you cannot remove one from the conversation.
- The report cannot be edited here. Copy it out and edit it where you write.
- There is no export, no PDF and no email button.
- Deleting cannot be undone.

## Who can do what

Everyone on the team can start a discovery, talk to the copilot, write the report, change the status and attach one to a business. The person talking to the prospect is the person who should be writing it down.

Only an owner can delete one, because it destroys a conversation somebody had.
