# {{engagement|plural}} at a glance

> What each {{client|lower}} has agreed to, and the hours against it. This guide is the map of the {{engagement|plural}} pages. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/professional-services/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Professional services** in the sidebar. This is where you record what you have taken on for each {{client|lower}}, what it is worth, and every hour spent on it. Two pages make up the whole tool: the list of {{engagement|plural|lower}}, and one {{engagement|lower}}'s own page.

## What you see

- **An {{engagement|lower}}.** One agreement with one {{client|lower}}: what you are doing, for how much, from when. A month of bookkeeping, a website build, a block of advice. If you have agreed it with somebody, it belongs here.
- **The {{engagement|plural}} list.** Everything live, with its {{client|lower}}, its kind, and how this month is going. Owners agree new ones here. See [Every {{engagement|lower}}](engagements.md).
- **One {{engagement|lower}}'s page.** Its terms, this month's hours, every entry logged against it, and a month-by-month history. This is where nearly all the work happens. See [One {{engagement|lower}}](engagement.md).
- **Kinds.** Every {{engagement|lower}} has a kind, such as `Retainer`, `Project` or `Hourly`. You can type your own. Kinds are a word for grouping and nothing more — what the figures do comes from the fields you fill in.
- **Hours a month.** What the fee covers. Log more than this in a month and it counts as over. Leave it at nothing and there is no limit to be over.
- **Rate an hour.** What an hour is worth beyond the hours a month. On an {{engagement|lower}} with no hours included, it prices everything logged.
- **Status.** `Proposed` is agreed with nobody yet. `Active` is live work. `Paused` is on hold. `Ended` is finished. Ending can be undone.
- **Time.** Minutes against one {{engagement|lower}}, on one day, by one person. Anyone on the team can log it, including an accountant.
- **Onboarding.** The steps a new {{engagement|lower}} starts with, taken from your industry profile. They become ordinary jobs on your **Work** list, linked to the {{engagement|lower}}.
- **Money and blanks.** Figures show with your currency symbol. Anything not set shows as a dash, never as zero, because "no rate" and "free" are different.

## How to get going

1. Open **Professional services** and click {button:New {{engagement|lower}}|primary}.
2. Pick the {{client|lower}}, or choose `Somebody new…` and type their business name.
3. Give it a `Name`, pick a `Kind`, and set `Starts`.
4. Fill in `Hours a month`, `Fee` and `Rate an hour` if you have agreed them. Any of them can be blank.
5. Click {button:Add {{engagement|lower}}|primary}.
6. Open it and click {button:Start onboarding|outline} to raise the steps your profile says a new one needs.
7. Click {button:Log time|primary} each time you do some of the work.

## Not on this page

- Nothing is invoiced automatically. The figures tell you what to bill; you raise the invoice in Accounting.
- Nothing is posted to your accounts. What an {{engagement|lower}} costs you reaches the books when you tag a bill or a journal entry with it.
- There is no timer. You type how long something took.
- {{engagement|plural}} cannot be deleted. One you are finished with is ended, and that keeps its history.
- There is no report across {{client|plural|lower}} yet, and no way to see what is unbilled across everything.
- You cannot edit your onboarding steps yourself. They live in your industry profile; ask us to change them.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Agreeing an {{engagement|lower}}, changing its terms, and starting, pausing, ending or reopening one belong to the owner. They are decisions with a money consequence.

Logging time is open to everyone, an accountant included, because whoever did the work is the person who knows how long it took. Correcting and deleting an entry are open to the same people, since a slip is fixed by whoever made it.

Everyone can see every page, including the fee and the rate.
