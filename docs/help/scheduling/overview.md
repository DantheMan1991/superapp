# Scheduling at a glance

> The business's calendar: what is happening, when, and who is on it. This guide is the map of the Scheduling pages. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/scheduling/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Scheduling** in the sidebar. There are two pages: the calendar itself, and the page where you decide who can see what.

## What you see

- **An event.** Something happening at a time, on one calendar. A job, an appointment, time off.
- **A calendar.** What events live on. **A calendar, never an event, is what gets shared.** Everybody gets one of their own the first time they sign in, and it is private until you share it.
- **The calendar.** Day, week or month, with everything you can see on it. See [The calendar](calendar.md).
- **`Calendars`.** Where you add calendars, choose their colours, decide who can see them, and set up a subscribe link for your phone. See [Your calendars](calendars.md).

## The four levels of access

When you share a calendar you choose one of four. They stack, each one giving everything below it.

| What you pick | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| `Free/busy only` | Your times, with every event reading `Busy`. No titles, no places. |
| `Titles and locations` | The same, plus what each one is called and where it is. |
| `All details` | The whole event, notes included. |
| `Can edit` | All of that, plus adding and changing events on that calendar. |

**`Can edit` does not mean they can share it on.** Renaming, recolouring, archiving and sharing stay with the calendar's owner. Access never spreads sideways.

## The surprising one, worth knowing up front

**Your own calendar is administered by you and by nobody else, including the business owner.** An owner who needs to see somebody's schedule has to be given access like anybody else, which is a row that person can see and take away again.

Business calendars are the exception. Those belong to the workspace rather than a person, and every owner administers them.

## Marking one event private

A calendar is shared as a whole, but a single event can be marked `Private`. Then only you and the people on it see the details, however widely that calendar is shared. Everybody else still sees that you are busy.

## Not on this page

- Nobody is emailed. Adding somebody to an event puts it on their calendar in Yosher and nothing more.
- Cancelling an event does not tell the people on it. It simply disappears.
- Nothing shows you who has accepted an invitation, even though people can answer.
- The week always starts on Sunday and cannot be changed.
- There is no way to see the events attached to a customer or an invoice from that record's own page. The link only points one way.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone can keep a calendar, put events on it, share it, and set up a subscribe link. What you can do to somebody else's calendar is whatever they have granted you.

**Your accountant cannot use this module.** They can see it, and every button they press fails with `accountant access is read-only`. Nothing on the screen warns them first. They also cannot be given access to a calendar at all.
