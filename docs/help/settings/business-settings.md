# Business settings

> The time zone, the one setting here that applies to everyone in the business, and where the other business-wide settings live.
> **Route:** /dashboard/settings
> **Order:** 0

Open **Business settings** under `Settings` in the sidebar. Owners only. The page has one card, `Timezone`. It decides what counts as today everywhere in Yosher, so set it to where the business operates.

## What you see

- **`Timezone`.** The card reads `Decides what counts as “today” everywhere in Yosher: when an invoice becomes overdue, which month a journal entry closes into, and which tasks show up as due. Set it to where the business actually operates.` Under it are a list of zones, {button:Save|primary}, and the line `Right now that makes today [date].`
- **The zones on offer.** Eastern (New York), Central (Chicago), Mountain (Denver), Mountain with no daylight saving (Phoenix), Pacific (Los Angeles), Alaska (Anchorage), Hawaii (Honolulu), Eastern and Pacific for Canada, Atlantic (Halifax), Newfoundland (St. John's), United Kingdom (London), Ireland (Dublin), and UTC. If your business was set up with a zone that is not in this list, it appears at the top under its full name. A new business starts on Eastern (New York).

## How to change the time zone

1. Pick the zone from the list. {button:Save|primary} stays gray until you pick one that differs from the current setting.
2. Click {button:Save|primary}. The button reads `Saving…`, then you see `Timezone updated`, and the line under the form shows the new today.

## What the time zone changes

- The day an invoice becomes overdue, and the day a bill counts as due.
- Which month a journal entry falls into at month end.
- Which items What needs you calls due today.
- When the recurring entries in Accounting are made, at 6am your time.
- When the morning email goes out, at 7am your time.

It is one setting for the whole business, not one per person. Someone working from another time zone still sees the business's today.

## Not on this page

- **The currency symbol** shown on cards and in add-on tools was set when your industry set was installed. Ask us if it needs changing.
- **The words Yosher uses.** Some add-ons let a business rename things, for example calling a zone a paddock. Those words are set for you by Yosher. Ask us if a word is wrong for your business.
- **Your {{enterprise|plural|lower}}**, **Taking payments**, **Billing** and **Email setup** each have their own page under `Settings` in the sidebar.

## Who can do what

Only owners see the `Settings` group and this page.
