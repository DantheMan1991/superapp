# Business settings

> The one setting that applies to everyone in the business today, the time zone, and where the other business-wide settings live.
> **Route:** /dashboard/settings
> **Order:** 0

## Timezone

This page has one card, **Timezone**. Its description reads `Decides what counts as “today” everywhere in Yosher: when an invoice becomes overdue, which month a journal entry closes into, and which tasks show up as due. Set it to where the business actually operates.`

Pick a zone from the list and click **Save**. The button stays greyed out until you have picked something different from the current setting. When it saves you see `Timezone updated`, and the line under the form updates: `Right now that makes today [date].`

The list offers the common zones: Eastern (New York), Central (Chicago), Mountain (Denver), Mountain, no DST (Phoenix), Pacific (Los Angeles), Alaska (Anchorage), Hawaii (Honolulu), Eastern and Pacific for Canada, Atlantic (Halifax), Newfoundland (St. John's), United Kingdom (London), Ireland (Dublin), and UTC. If your business was set up with a zone that is not in this list, it appears at the top under its full name. New businesses start on Eastern (New York).

What the time zone changes:

- The day an invoice becomes overdue, and the day a bill counts as due.
- Which month a journal entry falls into at month end.
- Which items What needs you calls due today.
- When the recurring entries in Accounting are generated, at 6am your time.
- When the morning email goes out, at 7am your time.

It is one setting for the whole business, not one per person. Someone working from another time zone still sees the business's today.

## Settings that are not on this page

**The currency symbol** shown on cards and in add-on tools was set when your industry set was installed. Ask us if it needs changing.

**The words Yosher uses.** Some add-ons let a business rename things, for example calling a zone a paddock. Those words are set for you by Yosher. Ask us if a word is wrong for your business.

**Your {{enterprise|plural|lower}}**, **Taking payments**, **Billing** and **Email setup** each have their own page under Settings in the sidebar.
