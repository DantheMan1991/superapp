# {{enterprise|plural}}

> The parts of the business you want the money for on their own — and which tools each one works with.
> **Route:** /dashboard/settings/enterprises
> **Order:** 1

Open **{{enterprise|plural}}** under `Settings` in the sidebar. Owners only. A {{enterprise|lower}} is whatever you would want a separate profit figure for. Most businesses have between three and six, and you can change the list whenever you like.

It is **not** a second set of books. Everything here reports inside the same company — a {{enterprise|lower}} slices one trial balance, it does not keep its own. To keep separate books you add a company, under Accounting.

## What you see

- **The heading** — `{{enterprise|plural}}`, with `The parts of the business you want to see the money for on their own.` and {button:Add a {{enterprise|lower}}|primary}.
- **The table** — `{{enterprise}}`, `Kind`, `Notes`, and the actions on each row. Active ones first; retired ones below them, so you can put one back.
- **`What this list does today`**, the card at the bottom. It says plainly what is wired up and what is not, and it is rewritten whenever that changes. Read it — it is the honest answer to "will this actually show me anything".
- **When the list is empty**, the page says `No {{enterprise|plural|lower}} yet` with the same definition and one button.

## How to add one

1. {button:Add a {{enterprise|lower}}|primary}.
2. **`Name`** — what you call it. `Cabinet Shop`, `Excavation`, `Beef`. Required, and it has to be different from the others.
3. **`Kind`** — a rough type, from the list your industry suggests. Pick `Something else` to type your own. It is a label for you, not a rule.
4. **`Notes`** — anything you want to remember about it. Never printed.
5. {button:Add|primary}. It appears in the table straight away.

**Renaming later is safe.** Every record already tagged follows the new name, including in reports — the name is a display copy, and the handle underneath never moves.

## How to say which tools a {{enterprise|lower}} works with

{button:Edit|ghost} on its row, then **`Tools it works with`**.

Tick the tools that side of the business uses. **It only changes the menu down the left**, while you are working on that {{enterprise|lower}} — see *How to show only one side of your business* in the getting-around guide. Nothing is scoped, filtered or hidden: the books, the reports and every page stay exactly as they are.

- **Only the add-on tools are listed.** Accounting, Mail, Documents, Scheduling, Work and Time are always there, whichever side you are on, because every {{enterprise|lower}} posts to the same books.
- **Tick nothing and it is not offered** as a side to switch to. That is the default, and it is right for a business with one way of working — you never meet the idea.
- **This is where two {{enterprise|plural|lower}} on one company's books can have different tools.** A cabinet shop that runs jobs, stock and production, and an excavation crew that runs jobs, equipment and stock, are one set of books and two different menus.
- Only tools your business has switched on appear in the list.

**The same tick boxes sit on each of your companies**, one level up, under `Settings → Companies`. A company also has a `Line of business`, so it can take its tools from its trade and only tick its own when that trade does not describe it — a {{enterprise|lower}} has no trade to take them from, so it always picks. See the Companies guide.

## How to retire one, and put it back

1. {button:Retire|ghost} on its row. It asks first.
2. It stops being offered on new records, and drops out of the tools switcher.
3. **Everything already recorded against it keeps reporting** — last year's figures never move.
4. {button:Restore|ghost} on the retired row puts it back exactly as it was, tools and all.

## Who can do what

Owners only, to see this page and to change anything on it. It decides how the whole business reports, and what everybody's menu offers.
