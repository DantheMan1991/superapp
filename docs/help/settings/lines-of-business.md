# {{enterprise|plural}}

> The parts of the business you want to see a separate profit figure for, how to add and retire them, and what they change in your reports.
> **Route:** /dashboard/settings/enterprises
> **Order:** 40

## What one is

The page is described as `The parts of the business you want to see the money for on their own.` A {{enterprise|lower}} is anything you would want its own figure for. On a farm that might be broilers, beef and the market garden. Most businesses have between three and six.

Before you add any, the page says `No {{enterprise|plural|lower}} yet` and `Whatever you would want a separate profit figure for. Most businesses have between three and six, and you can change the list whenever you like.`

## Adding one

Click **Add** in the title row. The dialog is described as `A part of the business you want to see the money for on its own.`

- **Name.** Required. Renaming later is safe.
- **What kind.** If your industry set supplies kinds, this is a list, for example `Livestock` and `Crop`, plus `Something else` and `Name a new kind…`, which lets you type your own. If not, it is a plain text box for a grouping word, and `Optional. A word for grouping the list — nothing depends on it.`
- **Notes.** Optional.

Click **Add**. You see `Added`. A name that is already in the list, or one with no letters or numbers in it, is refused.

## The list

The table has three columns: the name, **Kind** and **Notes**. Active ones come first, then retired ones marked `retired`, each set in alphabetical order.

Each row has two buttons. **Edit** opens the same three fields. Its description: `Renaming is safe — every record already tagged with this {{enterprise|lower}} follows the new name, including in reports.` Click **Save**.

**Retire** asks you to confirm: `It stops being offered on new records. Everything already recorded against it keeps reporting, so last year's figures do not move. You can put it back.` Click **Retire it**. A retired row shows **Put back** instead, which restores it with no confirmation. Nothing here can be deleted, only retired.

## What the list does today

The panel at the foot of the page is the plain statement of what this list changes, and it is updated whenever that changes. Today it says:

- Everything here is registered as a reporting dimension, so the {{enterprise|lower}} appears in the grouping picker on the profit and loss report. Items and batches can name one, and a batch inherits its item's.
- What things cost now lands against them. Feed issued to a batch is charged to whatever that batch belongs to, stock sold takes its cost from the batch it came out of, and a fee for processing follows the batches that went in. What you sell does not yet, so the report answers what a {{enterprise|lower}} has cost you, not what it has made you.
- Nothing before the day this was switched on can be counted, and it never will be. Entries already in the books cannot be given a {{enterprise|lower}} without rewriting history, so a report over last year reads Unassigned however much you tag now.
- Retiring one stops it being offered on new records and leaves everything already recorded against it reporting exactly as before.

Two limits worth knowing. A batch is tagged when it is created and cannot be retagged afterwards. And the word used for these, {{enterprise|lower}}, comes from your industry set; ask us if it is the wrong word for your business.
