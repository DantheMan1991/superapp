# Time

> Keep a record of who worked, how long, and on which day, by running a clock or by writing hours down, and see the overtime in it. Three screens: the week, where time is recorded; the pay period, where it is added up the way you pay it; and People, where you say whose hours you keep and set the rules.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/time/**
> **Order:** 100

Open **Time** {icon:clock} in the sidebar. You land on the current week, with everybody's hours grouped by day. Before you can log anything, somebody has to be on the People list, so a brand new workspace starts there.

Time records what was worked. [Work](../work/overview.md) records what still has to be done. They are different lists on purpose, and nothing you log here ticks anything off there.

## What you see

- **Time.** The week. Everybody's hours, newest day first, with a running total for the week and for each day, every clock that is running right now, and each person's regular and overtime hours. This is where you start a clock or log an hour. See [The week](week.md).
- **Pay period.** The same hours grouped the way you pay them, broken into the weeks inside the period. Where hours are sent for approval, approved, and the period locked once payroll is run. Read this before you pay anybody. See [The pay period](pay-period.md).
- **People.** Everybody whose hours this business keeps, what they are paid, and the four settings behind every figure: your week start, rounding, how often people are paid, and which overtime rules you follow. Owners work here, and pay rates are owners-only. See [People](people.md).

## How to start from nothing

1. Open **Time** {icon:clock} in the sidebar. You see `Nobody can have time logged yet`.
2. Click {button:Add someone|primary}. That takes you to **People**.
3. Add each person whose hours you want to keep. They do not need to be able to sign in.
4. Set `Your week starts on` to the day your week runs from. Most businesses in the US use Sunday or Monday.
5. Go back with {button:Time|outline}. Click {button:Start a clock|outline} when work is beginning, or {button:Log time|primary} to write down hours already worked.

## Not on this page

Plenty, and all of it is coming. The hours you book to a field or a line of business do not yet show up as a cost in your books, there is no export for your payroll provider, and Time works out what people have earned but never what is withheld — your payroll provider does that, and always will. Ask us where it is up to.

Time also does not run your payroll and is not going to. When it can work out gross pay, it will hand that to whoever does your payroll rather than calculating tax.

## Who can do what

Owners can do everything, including setting pay rates, approving hours and locking a period. Staff can start and stop clocks, log, change and delete hours, and send hours for approval — but never see what anybody is paid. Accountants can read every hour, and also never see pay.
