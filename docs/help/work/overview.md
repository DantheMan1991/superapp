# Work at a glance

> Everything the business owes itself: what needs doing, who it is on, and when it is due. This guide is the map of the Work pages. Each page has its own guide.
> **Route:** /dashboard/m/work/**
> **Order:** 0

Open **Work** in the sidebar. There are two pages: everything outstanding, and the lists that work is grouped into.

## What you see

- **A piece of work.** A title, and then as much or as little as you want: notes, a start date, a due date, who it is on, and what it is about.
- **A list.** What work is grouped into, **and the only thing that decides who can see it.** Every business starts with one called `Work`.
- **A state.** Where a piece of work has got to: `To do`, `In progress`, `Blocked`, `Done` or `Cancelled`.
- **Everything outstanding.** All of it, as a list ordered by what is due or a board ordered by state. See [Everything outstanding](work.md).
- **`Lists`.** Where lists are made and who can see them is decided. See [Lists](lists.md).

## Three things the whole tool rests on

**Nothing is ever deleted.** `Cancelled` is how work leaves the list, and it keeps its history and its links. Lists are archived rather than deleted, and archiving keeps the work.

**A list decides who can see the work on it.** There are two answers, `Everyone in the workspace` or `Owners only`, and only an owner can pick the second. There is no per-person sharing.

**Who work is on has nothing to do with who can see it.** That is on purpose, so somebody covering a holiday can see what is waiting.

## How the day goes

1. Add work as it comes up, from the row at the top of the page. A title is the only thing you must type.
2. Look at `My work` to see what is on you, soonest first.
3. Mark things done from the row as you go.
4. Open something to add notes, dates, or what it is about.
5. Use the board when you want to see where everything has got to rather than what is next.

## What a piece of work can be about

Open one and you can attach a customer, vendor, invoice, bill, contact, company, deal, file, folder, or another piece of work. That is what turns "ring them back" into something you can find from the record it concerns.

Follow-ups raised elsewhere in Yosher are ordinary work items with one of those attached, so they turn up here alongside everything else.

A record you no longer have access to shows as `A record you cannot see` rather than disappearing, so you can tell the link was made.

## Two traps worth knowing

**An item saves in two different ways.** Its state and who it is on save the moment you pick them. Its title, notes and dates wait for you to press {button:Save|primary}. **Close the panel with unsaved text in it and that text is gone**, with no warning.

**Work with a start date in the future does not appear under `My work`.** It is not hidden behind a toggle, it is simply not there. Change who it is for to `Anyone` to see it.

## Not on this page

- Sub-items and checklists are not available yet.
- Work cannot be moved between lists after it is made.
- Nothing here repeats. Every piece of work is entered once.
- Nothing is emailed and nothing is notified. Work appears in your daily digest, and on this page.
- There is no priority, no ordering by hand, and no watchers.
- If you need any of this, ask us.

## Who can do what

Everyone can add work, change it, assign it to anybody, make lists and save views.

**Only an owner can set a list to `Owners only`**, which is the one thing that hides work from colleagues.

**Your accountant reads this module and writes nothing in it.** Both screens say so under the header rather than offering a control that fails, and the controls that would write are not drawn. They can never be given work either.
