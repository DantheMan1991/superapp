# Getting around Yosher

> Where everything is, how to jump straight to it, and where to find help on any screen.
> **Route:** /dashboard
> **Order:** 0

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left is the map of your workspace.

- **Workspace** holds the Overview, then What needs you, which lists the things waiting on you today, and this Guides page.
- **Modules** are the tools switched on for your business, such as Accounting, Documents or Mail.
- A section named after your industry holds the add-ons built for it. If none are switched on, that section is not shown.
- **Business** holds Hours, which is your retainer and the work log, and Team.
- **Settings** appears for owners only: email setup, billing, taking payments, your {{enterprise|plural|lower}} and business settings.

On a phone the sidebar sits behind the menu button in the top left corner. Tap it to open the menu, and tap outside it to close.

## Jump anywhere

The search box near the top of the sidebar lists every page you can reach. Start typing and pick one. On a keyboard, press Ctrl+K, or Cmd+K on a Mac, from any screen.

## Help on any screen

Every page has a small question mark at the right of its title. Click it to open the guide for the screen you are on, without leaving it. If a screen has no guide yet, the panel says so and links to the list of guides.

## The Guides page

Guides lists everything written for the tools switched on for your business, grouped the same way as the sidebar. Open one to read it in full. The **Open this screen** button at the top of a guide takes you to the screen it describes.
