# Getting around Yosher

> The first screen you see, the sidebar, the search box, your account and business menus, the phone layout, printing, and how to get help on any page.
> **Route:** /dashboard
> **Order:** 0

Yosher opens on the **Overview** page. The sidebar on the left takes you to every other page, and the search box under your business name jumps straight to one. Every page has a {icon:circle-question-mark} button beside its title that opens the guide for that page.

## What you see

- **Welcome back, [your business name].** The title of the Overview page. The `Overview` row in the sidebar brings you back here from anywhere.
- **`Hours used`.** A card that appears once your plan includes retainer hours and some work has been logged. The big number is the hours used this month, such as `4.5 h`. The line under it shows what is included and what you have bought, such as `of 10.0 h included · 2.5 h purchased left`. When you have used more than that, the number turns red and the line ends with `· over`. Click the card to open Hours.
- **`Subscription`.** Owners only. A card with your plan's name, or `No plan`, and a badge: {badge:Active|success}, {badge:Trialing|secondary}, {badge:Past due|destructive}, {badge:Canceled|destructive}, {badge:Incomplete|warning} or {badge:None|secondary}. Click the card to open Billing.
- **`Getting set up`.** Owners only, and only while one of your tools is waiting for something. A section under the figures and above `Your modules`, headed `Getting set up`, with the line `What each tool needs before it can do its job. A line goes away on its own once the thing is there.` Under it, one row per thing a switched-on tool cannot work without: the tool's name in small type, such as `Accounting`, the ask, such as `Add your bank account`, and a line on what it is for. At the right of each row, {button:Guide|ghost} opens the guide for the screen where it is done, and a second button names the act and opens that screen: {button:Set the date|outline}, {button:Add account|outline}, {button:Import a statement|outline}, {button:Open Banking|outline}, {button:Add an asset|outline}, {button:Add a kind|outline}, {button:Open Assets|outline}, {button:Add animals|outline}, {button:Add ground|outline}, {button:Add a place to sell|outline}, {button:Set prices|outline}, {button:Add a record|outline} or {button:Open Email setup|outline}. On a phone the two buttons sit under the words. There is nothing to tick and nothing to dismiss. A row disappears when the thing it asks for exists, whoever added it and however, and once the last row has gone the whole section is gone for good. See how to work through it, below.
- **`Your modules`.** One tile for each tool switched on for your business, with the tool's icon in its own color, its name, and a line on what it does. Click a tile to open the tool. The sidebar lists the same tools in the same colors.
- **The sidebar.** The Yosher mark at the top, then your business name, then the search box, then rows in groups:
  - `Workspace`. `Overview`, `What needs you`, which lists what is waiting on you today, and `Guides`, every guide for the tools you have. These three are always there.
  - `Modules`. One row for each core tool switched on: Accounting, CRM, Mail, Documents, Scheduling, Work. A tool that is not switched on has no row.
  - Your industry's name, such as `Homestead Farm`. The add-ons built for it: Land, Assets, Inventory, Livestock, Production, Retail. A business with no industry set sees `Add-ons` instead. With no add-ons switched on, the group is not shown.
  - `Business`. `Hours`, your retainer and the work log, and `Team`.
  - `Settings`. Owners only. `Email setup`, `Billing`, `Taking payments`, your {{enterprise|plural|lower}}, and `Business settings`.
- **The row you are on** is filled in. A tool's row keeps its color whether or not you are on it.
- **The count on `Mail`.** A number at the right end of the row is your unread mail across every connected mailbox, up to `99+`. A red dot instead of a number means a mailbox needs reconnecting, so the count cannot be trusted. No other row carries a mark, and an accountant never sees one, because that role has no mail.
- **The business switcher.** At the bottom of the sidebar, showing the business you are in. See how to switch, below.
- **Your avatar.** Beside the switcher. It opens your own account: profile, email addresses, password, security, and `Sign out`. Your account is the same across every business you belong to, so a change here follows you everywhere. Under `Security` the last item is `Delete account`. It asks you to confirm, then removes your sign-in and your place in every business you belong to. The businesses themselves, and the records that name you, stay.
- **The search box.** `Jump to…`, under your business name. See how to jump to a page, below.

The switcher and the avatar can appear a moment after the rest of the page on a slow connection. That is normal.

**In the Yosher app on a phone**, opening the app shows the Yosher mark on blue for a moment before the sign-in screen or your business appears. The first time you sign in the phone asks whether Yosher may send you notifications. Allow it, and your morning summary arrives as a notification as well as an email; tap it to open **What needs you**. Say no, and nothing changes except that the phone stays quiet. You can change your mind in the phone's own settings for the Yosher app.

## How to work through Getting set up

1. Read the top row first. The rows are in the order to do them: the day your books begin, then money, then places, then what you hold, then animals, ground and where you sell, then people, then mail.
2. Click the button at the right of the row. The screen where the thing is added opens. Add it there the way that screen's own guide describes. Click {button:Guide|ghost} first if you want that guide open beside you.
3. Come back to the Overview. The row is gone. Sometimes a new one has appeared behind it: adding a bank account brings `Bring in your transactions`, adding your first asset brings `Add somewhere to keep it`, and adding a place to sell brings `Set your prices`.
4. When the last row goes, the section goes with it and does not come back.

The rows you can see, and when each one is there:

| Row | It is there while | The button opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Say when your books begin` | Accounting is switched on and the first day of your company's books has not been set. | Opening position. |
| `Add your bank account` | Accounting is switched on and no bank account, card or personal account has ever been added. | Banking. |
| `Bring in your transactions` | An account exists and no transaction has ever come in, by import or by feed. | That account's Import statement page when you have one account, otherwise Banking. |
| `Add your equipment and buildings` | Assets is switched on and nothing has ever been added to it. | Assets. |
| `Add what you hold` | Inventory is switched on and no kind of thing has ever been added. | Items. |
| `Add somewhere to keep it` | At least one asset exists and none of them has `Things are kept here` turned on. | Assets. |
| `Add your animals` | Livestock is switched on and no group or named animal has ever been started. | Livestock. |
| `Add your ground` | Land is switched on and nothing has ever been added to it. | Land. |
| `Add where you sell` | Retail is switched on and no place to sell has ever been added. | Retail. |
| `Set your prices` | A place to sell exists and no price has ever been set anywhere. | That place's own page when you have one, otherwise Retail. |
| `Add the people you work with` | CRM is switched on and nobody has ever been added, here or as a customer or vendor in Accounting. | New record. |
| `Set up your mailboxes` | Mail is switched on and no mailbox on your own domain exists yet. | Email setup. |

Under the rows, `Not sure where to start? Walk through it with us and get a plan for your business.` opens a short conversation that writes you an ordered plan; see [Getting set up with us](setup.md). Owners only, like the card.

A row clears however the thing arrives. Typing a new customer's name on an invoice clears `Add the people you work with` just as adding a record in CRM does. A tool that is switched off has no rows.

## How to open a page

1. Click its row in the sidebar. The row fills in and the page opens.
2. For a tool, you can also click its tile under `Your modules` on the Overview page.

## How to jump to a page by name

1. Click `Jump to…`, or press {kbd:Ctrl+K} on Windows or {kbd:⌘K} on a Mac, from any page. Pressing the same keys again closes it.
2. Start typing. The list narrows to pages whose name or group matches.
3. Press the arrow keys to move and {kbd:Enter} to open the highlighted page, or click it.
4. Press {kbd:Esc}, or click outside the box, to close it without going anywhere.

It finds pages only. To find a customer, an invoice or a document, search inside the tool that holds it.

## How to switch to another business

1. Click the business switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Pick the business. You land on its Overview page, not on the page you were on.

The same menu offers a way to manage the business. It opens the same panel you find on the Team page.

## How to get help on any page

1. Click {icon:circle-question-mark} at the right of the page's title, on the same line as its buttons.
2. A panel opens at the right with the guide for that page. The page moves over to make room, so you can keep working with it open.
3. Anything in the guide that names a control, such as {button:New bill|primary} or the `Overview` row, is live in the panel. Click it and the real one on the page is ringed for a moment, so you can see where it is. If it is not on the page right now, the panel tells you.
4. Click {button:Open full guide|outline} at the bottom of the panel to read the guide on its own page, or `All guides` for the list of every guide. The panel closes when you go to another page.

## On a phone

1. Tap {icon:menu} at the top left. The drawer opens with the search box and the full sidebar.
2. Tap a row. The page opens and the drawer closes.
3. Tap outside the drawer, or {icon:x} in its corner, to close it without going anywhere.

The bar across the top also holds the Yosher mark, your business name, the business switcher and your avatar. Mail uses the whole width of the screen on every device. Every other page sits in a centered column.

## Printing

Print any page with your browser's print command. The sidebar, the top bar and the search box are left out, and the page's content spans the full width of the paper.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `No modules active yet` and `Your modules get switched on as part of onboarding — we'll take it from here.` | Nothing has been switched on for your business yet. We do that with you during onboarding. |
| `This list may be short.` and `Inventory could not be checked just now. Reload in a moment.` | Under `Getting set up`: one of your tools could not be asked what it is waiting for. The rows you can see are real; rows from the tool named may be missing. Reload the page. |
| `Nothing matches “what you typed”.` | No page name or group matches what you typed in `Jump to…`. Try a shorter word. |
| `No guide yet.` | On the Guides page: that tool has no guide written yet. |
| `No guide for this screen yet` | In the help panel: the page you are on has no guide yet. `All guides` lists everything written so far. |

## Who can do what

Everyone sees the Overview, the sidebar, the search box and the help panel. Owners also see the `Subscription` card, the `Getting set up` section and the `Settings` group. Accountants see no `Mail` row. Yosher's own staff see a link back to the platform's admin area under the sidebar rows.
