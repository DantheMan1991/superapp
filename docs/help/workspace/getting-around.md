# Getting around Yosher

> The first screen you see, the sidebar that takes you everywhere else, the search box, your account and business menus, the phone layout, printing, and where to find help on any page.
> **Route:** /dashboard
> **Order:** 0

## The Overview page

This is the page Yosher opens on, and the one the **Overview** row in the sidebar brings you back to. Its title reads **Welcome back, [your business name]**.

Two cards can sit under the title. Each one is a link.

**Hours used.** Shows once your plan includes retainer hours and some work has been logged. The big figure is the hours used this month, written like `4.5 h`. The small line under it reads `of 10.0 h included · 2.5 h purchased left`. If you have gone past your included hours and have no purchased hours left to cover it, the figure turns red and the line ends `· over`. Click the card to open Hours.

**Subscription.** Owners only. Shows your plan name, or `No plan`, with a badge underneath: `Active`, `Trialing`, `Past due`, `Canceled`, `Incomplete` or `None`. Click the card to open Billing.

Below the cards is **Your modules**, described as `Everything switched on for your business.` Each tile is one tool: its icon in the tool's own colour, its name, and a one-line description of what it does. Click a tile to open that tool. The same tools, in the same colours, are in the sidebar.

If nothing has been switched on yet, the shelf says **No modules active yet** and `Your modules get switched on as part of onboarding — we'll take it from here.`

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left is the map of your workspace. At the top is the Yosher mark, the word Yosher, and under it the name of the business you are working in.

The rows are grouped, and each group has a small caption:

- **Workspace.** Three rows that are always there. **Overview** is this page. **What needs you** lists the things waiting on you today. **Guides** is the list of every guide for the tools you have.
- **Modules.** One row for each core tool switched on for your business: Accounting, CRM, Mail, Documents, Scheduling, Work. A tool that is not switched on has no row.
- **A group named after your industry.** If your business runs an industry set, this group carries its name, for example **Homestead Farm**, and holds the add-ons built for it: Land, Assets, Inventory, Livestock, Production, Retail. A business with no industry set sees the caption **Add-ons** instead. If no add-ons are switched on, the group is not shown at all.
- **Business.** **Hours**, which is your retainer and the work log, and **Team**.
- **Settings.** Owners only. **Email setup**, **Billing**, **Taking payments**, your {{enterprise|plural|lower}}, and **Business settings**. Staff do not see this group.

The row for the page you are on is filled in. The icon on a tool's row is in that tool's colour whether or not you are on it.

Two marks can appear at the right end of the **Mail** row. A number is the unread count across your connected mailboxes. It stops at `99+`. A red dot instead of a number means a mailbox needs reconnecting, so the count cannot be trusted. Nobody else's row carries a badge, and someone with accountant access never sees one, because that role has no mail.

Under the rows, most people see nothing. Yosher's own staff see a link back to the platform's admin area there.

## Your business and your account

At the bottom of the sidebar are two controls.

**The business switcher** shows the name of the business you are in. Open it to see the other businesses you belong to, if any, and pick one. Picking one takes you to that business's Overview, not to the page you were on. The same menu offers a way to manage the business, which opens the same panel you find on the Team page.

**Your avatar** opens your own account: your profile, email addresses, password and security settings, and **sign out**. This is your account across every business you belong to, so a change here follows you everywhere.

Both controls appear a moment after the rest of the page on a slow connection. That is normal.

## Jump to any page

Just under the business name is a rounded search box that reads **Jump to…**. Click it, or press **Ctrl+K** on Windows or **⌘K** on a Mac, from any screen. Pressing the same keys again closes it.

Start typing and the list narrows to pages whose name or group matches. The captions in the list are the sidebar's own captions. Use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to open the highlighted page, or click it. **Escape** closes the box, and so does clicking outside it. When nothing matches, the box says `Nothing matches “what you typed”.`

It finds pages only. It will not find a customer, an invoice or a document. Search for those inside the tool that holds them.

## On a phone

On a phone the sidebar is out of the way. A bar across the top holds the menu button at the left, then the Yosher mark and your business name, and at the right the business switcher and your avatar.

Tap the menu button to open the drawer. It holds the Jump to box and the full sidebar. Tapping a row opens that page and closes the drawer. Tap outside the drawer, or the X in its corner, to close it without going anywhere.

Mail uses the whole width of the screen on every device. Every other page sits in a centred column.

## Printing

When you print a page, the sidebar, the top bar and the search box are left out. Only the page's content prints, and it spans the full width of the paper.

## Help on any screen

Every page has a small question mark at the right of its title, on the same line as its buttons. Click it and a panel slides in from the right with the guide for the screen you are on. You can read it and keep working. It closes when you go to another page.

At the bottom of the panel, **All guides** opens the Guides page, and **Open full guide** opens the guide on a page of its own. If a screen has no guide yet, the panel says so, and the Guides page shows everything written so far.

## The Guides page

**Guides** in the sidebar lists every guide for the tools switched on for your business, grouped the same way as the sidebar. A tool that has no guide yet says `No guide yet.` Open a guide to read it in full. The **Open this screen** button at the top of a guide takes you straight to the screen it describes.
