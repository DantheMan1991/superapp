# Telling us something is wrong

> The button beside the {icon:circle-question-mark} on every page, what to put in the box, what we do with it, and how you hear back.
> **Route:** /dashboard/feedback/**
> **Order:** 40

Every page has a {icon:message} button beside its {icon:circle-question-mark}, at the right of the page's title. Click it to tell us something is broken, ask a question about the page you are on, or suggest a change. We answer you in Yosher, on [Your reports](your-reports.md).

You never have to say which screen you were on. The button sends that with your message.

## What you see

- **The {icon:message} button.** Beside the {icon:circle-question-mark}, on the same line as the page's buttons. A red dot on it means we have answered something you sent and you have not read it yet.
- **`Something to report`.** The panel that opens from the right. Under the title it names the page you are on, such as `You are on Livestock`, and tells you we send that screen along with your message.
- **`We answered one of your reports`.** A row at the top of the panel, only when you have an unread answer. Click it to go to [Your reports](your-reports.md).
- **`What is it?`.** Three choices. Pick the one that fits and the rest of the panel changes its wording to match:
  - `Something's broken` — it did the wrong thing, or nothing at all.
  - `An idea` — something that would make this easier.
  - `A question` — you are not sure how this is meant to work.
- **`In a few words`.** A short title, up to 140 characters. This is what you and we both see in a list, so make it the thing itself: `Totals do not add up`, not `problem`.
- **`What happened?`.** The long box. For something broken, tell us what you pressed, what you expected, and what it did instead. For an idea, tell us what you are trying to do.
- **{button:Send|primary}.** Stays gray until the title has at least three characters and the long box has something in it.
- **`your reports`.** The link at the bottom of the panel. Everything you have sent, and what we said back.

## How to report something broken

1. Stay on the page where it went wrong. Click {icon:message} beside the {icon:circle-question-mark}.
2. Pick `Something's broken`.
3. Type a short title in `In a few words`.
4. In `What happened?`, write what you pressed, what you expected, and what it did instead.
5. Click {button:Send|primary}. The panel closes and you see `Sent. We will answer you in Your reports.`

## How to suggest a change

1. Open {icon:message} from the page the change would be on.
2. Pick `An idea`.
3. Title it with the thing you want, such as `Let me filter by paddock`.
4. In the long box, tell us what you are trying to do and what would make it easier. What you are trying to do matters more than how you would build it.
5. Click {button:Send|primary}.

## How to ask a question about a page

1. Read the page's guide first — click {icon:circle-question-mark} beside the {icon:message} button. Most questions are answered there.
2. If it is not, click {icon:message}, pick `A question`, and ask.
3. Click {button:Send|primary}. We answer on [Your reports](your-reports.md), and if the guide was missing something we fix the guide too.

## What we send with your message

You type two things. We send the rest, so you do not have to describe it:

- The page you were on, and anything after the `?` in its address.
- Which tool it belongs to, such as `livestock`.
- Whether you were in the Yosher app on your phone or in a web browser, and which version of the app.
- The size of your screen, and which browser you are using.

We never send what is on the page — no names, no numbers, no records. Only where you were standing.

## What happens next

1. Your report lands in front of us with `New` on it.
2. We read it, and answer on [Your reports](your-reports.md). A red dot appears on the {icon:message} button until you read it.
3. If we need more from you, your report moves to `Needs your answer`. Reply in the same place.
4. When we start on it, it moves to `Being worked on`, and to `Done` when it ships. If we are not going to do it, we say so and it moves to `Not planned` — you always get an answer either way.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Sent. We will answer you in Your reports.` | It reached us. Nothing else to do. |
| `That needs a short title and a description before it can be sent.` | The title is under three characters, or the long box is empty. Fill both in. |
| `We answered one of your reports` | At the top of the panel: click it to read the answer. |

## Not on this page

You cannot attach a photo or a screenshot yet. If a picture would help, say so in the box and we will ask for it. You also cannot see what anybody else at your business has reported — a report is yours alone.

## Who can do what

Everyone can report, ask and suggest — owners, staff and accountants alike. You see your own reports and nobody else's, including your owner's. Nobody at your business can read what you sent.
