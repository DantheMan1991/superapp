# Getting set up with us

> A short conversation about how your business runs, and the plan it writes for you: what to do, in what order, with a link to each screen.
> **Route:** /dashboard/setup
> **Order:** 5

Open the Overview and click `Walk through it with us` at the foot of the `Getting set up` card. Owners only. The heading reads `Let's get you set up` and the line under it `A few questions about how the business runs, then a plan for setting it up here.`

This is for the first week, when you are moving an existing business in and everything is a decision. It asks about the things we cannot see — whether your money is mixed with your personal spending, what your accountant holds, whether you ever tracked what things cost — and leaves out everything we can.

## What you see

- **Before you start.** A panel reading `A few questions about how the business runs, then a written plan: what to do, in what order, with a link to each screen. It takes about five minutes.` with {button:Start|primary|sparkles}.
- **The conversation.** One question at a time. What we say sits on the left, what you say on the right. The first question is always the one that changes the most: `is the business's money in its own bank account, or is it mixed with your personal spending?`
- **The box.** Type your answer and press Enter, or click {button:Send|primary}. Under it, `3 of about 8 questions. Enter sends.` Shift and Enter together give you a new line without sending.
- **`Your plan`.** Once it has enough, the questions stop and the plan appears under the conversation: two or three sentences describing your business as you described it, then numbered steps. Each step is an act, one line on why it matters for you, and a button to the screen it is done on. Some steps have no button, because they are things you do away from here — asking your accountant for a figure, digging out last year's statements.
- **The last line.** `The plan stays here. Nothing on it has been done for you, and nothing was changed by answering the questions — every step is a screen you go to.`
- **{button:Go through it again|ghost}.** Starts a fresh conversation. The old plan is replaced by the new one.

## How to walk through it

1. Click {button:Start|primary|sparkles}.
2. Answer in a sentence or two. Plain answers are best: `it's all mixed with personal`, `January the first`, `my accountant does it in a spreadsheet`, `never tracked what the feed cost`.
3. It will not ask you for anything already in here. It knows which tools you have, whether you have said when your books begin, how many bank accounts you have added and whether one of them is a personal one, and roughly how much you have entered so far. If it asks anyway, say so and it will move on.
4. If you change your mind, say so. The later answer wins.
5. After about eight questions it stops and writes the plan. You can leave the page and come back; the plan is still here.
6. Work down the plan, clicking through to each screen. Nothing ticks itself off — the `Getting set up` card on the Overview is the live list of what is still missing, and this is the order to do it in.

It stops after ten questions whatever happens, and writes the plan with what it has.

## What it does not do

**It changes nothing.** Answering the questions does not set the day your books begin, add an account or switch a tool on. Every one of those is a screen you go to, where you confirm it yourself. A conversation that quietly set the first day of your books would be the one place here where something you said in passing rewrote the accounts.

**It only knows this business.** It has no idea what other businesses do, and it will not tell you what you should charge or how to run the place.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Give it a moment, then send that again.` | Two answers within a few seconds. |
| `That is a lot in one go — say it in a sentence or two.` | Over a thousand characters. |
| `That conversation is no longer here. Start a new one.` | The conversation was replaced, most likely in another tab. |
| `This one is finished. Start a new one to go through it again.` | You sent something after the plan was written. |
| `That is as long as this goes. The plan is below.` | Ten questions is the limit. |
| `It could not answer just then. Try sending that again.` | Nothing was lost — send the same answer again. |

## Who can do what

Owners only. Staff and accountants following a link here land back on the Overview. The plan is a set of decisions about the business; carrying them out is open to whoever the screen allows.
