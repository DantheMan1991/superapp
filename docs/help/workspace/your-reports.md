# Your reports

> Everything you have told us about, what we said back, how far along each one is, and how to answer a question we have asked you.
> **Route:** /dashboard/feedback, /dashboard/feedback/*
> **Order:** 41

Everything you have sent us with the {icon:message} button is here, newest first, with our answers. Get to it from the `your reports` link at the bottom of the report panel, or from the `We answered one of your reports` row at the top of it. Click a report to read the whole conversation and reply.

Only you can see this page. Nobody else at your business sees what you sent, not even an owner.

## What you see

- **`Your reports`.** The page title. Under it: `What you have told us about, and what we said back. Only you can see these.`
- **A red dot at the left of a row.** We have answered and you have not read it yet. The title is bold as well, and `New reply` sits at the end of the line in red.
- **The title.** What you typed in `In a few words` when you sent it.
- **The status.** A chip beside the title:
  - `Sent` — it has reached us and nobody has looked yet.
  - `Needs your answer` — we asked you something. Open it and reply.
  - `On the list` — we are going to do it, but have not started.
  - `Being worked on` — somebody is on it now.
  - `Done` — it has shipped. Go and look.
  - `Not planned` — we are not going to do this one. The reason is in the conversation.
- **The line underneath.** What kind it was (`Something's broken`, `An idea`, `A question`), which page you sent it from, and when.
- **`Nothing reported yet`.** When you have sent nothing. It tells you to press the {icon:message} button beside the {icon:circle-question-mark} on any screen.

## Inside a report

- **`Your reports`.** The link at the top left goes back to the list.
- **The title, with its kind and status beside it.** Under them: when you sent it and from which page. The page name is a link — click it to go back to the screen the report is about.
- **The conversation.** Every message in order, oldest first. Yours are under your name, ours are under `Yosher`. Each one carries the date and time in your business's own time zone.
- **The reply box.** Always there, whatever the status. Type and click {button:Send|primary}.
- **A line above the box on a finished report.** `We marked this done. If that is not right, say so — we will pick it back up.` Or `not planned` instead of `done`.

Opening a report marks it read. The red dot on the {icon:message} button clears a moment later.

We also email you when we answer. It goes to the address on your Yosher account, it says what we wrote, and it links straight back to this page. You get one for a real answer and nothing else: no email when the status changes on its own, and none for the notes we write to ourselves.

## How to answer a question we asked

1. Open the report. Its status is `Needs your answer` and our question is the last message.
2. Type your answer in the box at the bottom.
3. Click {button:Send|primary}. Your reply appears at the end of the conversation and goes straight back to us.

## How to say a fix did not work

1. Open the report, even though it says `Done`.
2. Tell us what is still happening, and what you did to see it.
3. Click {button:Send|primary}. It goes back to the top of our list — a finished report that you reply to is treated as waiting on us again.

## How to add something you forgot

1. Open the report.
2. Type it in the box and click {button:Send|primary}. There is no limit on how many times you can add to one report.

Replying does not change the status. Only we can move it, and we say why when we do.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `Nothing reported yet` | You have not sent anything. Press {icon:message} beside the {icon:circle-question-mark} on any screen. |
| `New reply` | In red at the end of a row: we have answered and you have not read it. |
| `We marked this done. If that is not right, say so — we will pick it back up.` | We think it is fixed. Reply if it is not. |
| `That message is empty.` | You clicked {button:Send|primary} with nothing in the box. |
| `That report is not yours to answer.` | The report was not one of yours. Go back to `Your reports`. |

## Not on this page

You cannot delete a report or edit something you have already sent — a conversation stays as it was said, on both sides. You cannot see reports from anybody else at your business. You cannot turn the email off, because we only send one when a person has written you an answer.

## Who can do what

Everyone has this page and sees only their own reports. An owner cannot see a staff member's reports, and a staff member cannot see an owner's. Accountants have it too.
